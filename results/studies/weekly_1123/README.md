### The two pointers from the 11/20 daily

**(a) The absence points.** Every rig above replays games with NO injury report: its 8+ is the back-to-back + the line
+ the study angles. Live, hockey still adds the absence points to the read on top of a price that already has them (the
favorite's top point man out = +6 for the dog, two of the top three = +7). So a live dog could reach 8 on a missing
scorer alone - a set the replay says loses (the b2b-spot Dogs UNDER 8 went 18-31 on the 2020-23 rig). Fixed for the
max play: its score is read without the favorite's absence points (`sports.max_play_score`). The Ducks: 16.7 on the
card, 9.7 without the 7 - still the spot.
*Not changed:* the hockey Dog's own read and gate. "The hockey dog whose read is lifted by the favorite's missing
scorers" (-10.6% on 284 at the close, 11/12) is already a tracker lead (1-1 live) and is judged there, not re-studied.
Live, hockey dogs that named "opponent missing key players" are 8-6.
