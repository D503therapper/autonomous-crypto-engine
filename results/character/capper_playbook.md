### A3. Hooks, captions, on-screen text [knowledge, with general-reel-hook sources from search]

- **First 2 seconds** is the stake or the claim, never a greeting: a number on screen ("$5,000 on this"), a result ("this hit by half a point"), a provocation ("y'all faded me on this"), or a visual (cash, slip turning green). Generic Reels research agrees: a strong hook gives a specific outcome, a tight angle, a reason to watch now; open questions in the first seconds raise comments. [search] [teleprompter.com](https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/hooks-for-instagram-reels), [insense](https://insense.pro/blog/instagram-reel-hooks)
- **On-screen text** every video: the pick, the price, the stake, the record. Big, high-contrast, the slip readable on a phone.
- **Captions** are short, with the record and a question: "3-0 yesterday. Who's tailing today? 👇", "+22u since 9/27. Receipts pinned."
- **Pinned comment** carries the funnel link ("picks in bio", "Discord in bio") or the record.
- Common vocabulary: lock, hammer, tail/fade, slip, smack, cash, "units", "bankroll", "sweat", "cook", "free money" — the owner's own lingo (`sports_owner_lingo.py`) already overlaps most of this.

### A4. Cadence, timing, records

- **Cadence [knowledge]:** the fast growers post 2–5 shorts a day during football season: card drop in the morning, 1–3 sweat/result clips during games, a recap at night. The same content is cross-posted TikTok -> Reels -> Shorts -> X with a different first second.
- **Timing [search, general not betting-specific]:** Instagram Reels studies cluster around 8 AM and noon ET Monday–Thursday (SocialPilot, 50k accounts) and noon from Adobe's 22k-Reel sample; TikTok guides say 6–10 AM and 7–11 PM. [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/?p=553523), [PetaPixel/Adobe](https://petapixel.com/2025/06/20/these-are-the-best-times-and-days-to-post-reels-on-instagram), [SocialChamp](https://socialchamp.io/?p=232245). For betting, the actual schedule is dictated by game times, which beats any generic study — people want the card before the games and the sweat during them.
- **Records [knowledge]:** honest accounts show a running W-L and units in the bio or a pinned post; some link a tracker (Pikkit / Betstamp / Juice Reel auto-sync bets from 30+ books and let followers "tail" a verified bettor). [search] [Pikkit](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1586567110). Most accounts show win % only (easy to game with -300 favorites). Our dashboard's units + ROI + two separate records (unit plays, leans) is already stricter than the industry norm.

### A5. Growth, engagement and conversion tactics

Honest ones (copy):
1. **Free picks in public, always** — the record is built in the open. The paid thing is convenience/early access/community, never "secret locks".
2. **Reply to every comment the first hour**, pin the best, make the fader's comment the next video's hook.
3. **Series and storylines** — a bankroll challenge ("$1k to $10k", "the $100k season"), a weekly receipts show, a nemesis (the fader, the sportsbook, the ref).
4. **Duets / stitches / reaction-to-reaction** — stitch another creator's pick on the same game, then grade both.
5. **Giveaways tied to engagement** ("comment your parlay, best one gets $100") — fine on TikTok/IG if it's not a raffle for money with entry fee; keep it a merch/gift-card prize. [search: TikTok Shop bans raffles/lucky spins] [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-gambling-policy-bans-raffles-and-lucky-spins-sellers-beware/)
6. **Discord/Telegram as the home base** — free server for the daily card and the sweat chat, optional paid tier for the alert pings. Platforms: Discord + Whop or Winible for payments.
7. **Affiliate codes with a licensed sportsbook** — allowed only with the platform's written permission (Meta) and NOT at all on X paid partnerships since 2/2026 (A6).

Scammy ones (never):

### B4. Growth tactics (honest only)

1. **The card drop is the product; everything else is the show.** The 8 AM post has to be useful even muted — slip cards readable, prices and units on screen. People save and share utility; they comment on personality.
2. **Cross-post with a different first second.** TikTok gets the bizarre open (tiger/jet), Reels the slip open, Shorts the result open ("35–6. Told you."), X gets the static slip + record image and a one-line roast. Same edit otherwise.
3. **Turn comments into episodes.** Every day pick one fader and one tailer from the comments for the next day's video (concept 9). Reply to every comment in the first hour with a fact (score, price), never "lol".
4. **Series with stakes people can follow:** "THE $100K SEASON" (bankroll curve every Sunday), "DOG OF THE DAY" as its own weekly record, "WE GOT IN EARLY" as a Sunday-morning ritual, "THE MACHINE SAID NO" on no-Lock days.
5. **Stitch and duet** other cappers' picks on the same game (only public videos, no insults about them personally — roast the pick, grade both after). Stitching the big accounts is the fastest discovery lever in the niche.
6. **Trends with the character:** every trending sound/format gets a betting twist within 24 hours (the tiger reacts to the slip to the trending audio). Keep one trend-ride per day max; the rest stays on-format.
7. **Giveaways, not raffles:** "best build-your-own parlay from today's plays in the comments wins [merch/gift card]". No entry fee, no chance-based spin, no cash prize framed as a wager.
8. **Home base:** a free Discord/Telegram with the 8 AM card auto-posted by the engine and a sweat channel for game time. If subscriptions come later: the paid tier sells *speed and alerts* (the added-play pings, the early plays the moment they post) — never different picks. Payments via Whop/Winible; keep the public record identical for free and paid.
9. **Collabs:** co-sweat with a human creator on a Monday night game (them real, him AI — the contrast is the content). Appear on betting podcasts as "the AI guy with the public record".
10. **Live once a week** (TikTok Live or Kick) for the Monday/Thursday night pick: the long sweat, chat tailing, Discord link in the room.
11. **Receipts as the growth loop:** screenshot-friendly weekly wall, the same graphic every week, so viewers can repost it. The honest record is the thing competitors can't copy.

### B5. What to avoid (bans, reach limits, trust)

- **No sportsbook links, promo codes, logos, or "sign up here"** on TikTok, YouTube or X (X bans all paid gambling partnerships since 2/18/2026; YouTube bans references to non-certified sites since 3/19/2025; TikTok restricts gambling marketing). On Meta only with written permission as an approved affiliate. Show the slip as *our* graphic, not a branded sportsbook screen.
- **Never "guaranteed", "can't lose", "free money", "lock it in, it's over"** as a promise. "Easy money" lives after a win, never before it (YouTube removes "guaranteed returns" content).
- **No win %** anywhere (the owner's rule and also the scammer's favorite stat). Units, dollars, W-L.
- **Label the AI on every platform** (TikTok AI label, Meta's AI label / "AI-generated profile", YouTube's altered-content disclosure). Unlabeled AI people = reduced reach on Instagram. The owner's own face, disclosed, is the compliant form of this.
- **21+ audience setting**, "entertainment" in bio, no youth-coded content, no school/college-kid framing.
- **Never fake anything:** no re-shot "placement" after the result, no edited slip, no deleting a loss, no stake that isn't the engine's units × $1,000. Every placement clip carries the engine's timestamp; anyone can check the dashboard.
- **No parlay posts** (the owner's rule), no live bets against our own side (the dashboard's never_against_us), no changing a posted pick on camera.
- **No raffles / lucky spins / cash giveaways** (TikTok Shop 2026 rules; Meta's gambling definitions).
- **Don't post the broadcast feed.** Copyrighted highlights get muted/struck; use the engine's score graphics, the character's reaction, and a stylized "stadium" instead. If a real clip is unavoidable, keep it under a few seconds and transformative (reaction overlay).
- **Don't harass commenters** — roast the pick, pin the comment, never the person's private info; never pull from DMs.
- **Don't over-flex without the record on screen.** The FOS rule of thumb ("flexing lifestyle = probably a scammer") is the audience's default; the record badge on every frame is the antidote.
