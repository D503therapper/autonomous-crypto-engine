## Bug: junk pool (c096bb2)
- ANTFUN's buy reason read "1h +15101012%". The sale was a "trailing stop" at price 0.
- ANTFUN's real pool traded at about 0.0139 the whole time (GT snapshots from 07:31 to 10:36).
- Cause: best_pairs() picks the pair with the deepest *reported* liquidity. A junk pool for the same token, with fake reported liquidity and a price near 0, priced our position.
- Fixes:
  - Held coins now follow the pair they were bought in (pos["pair"]).
  - A 1h change above +5,000% is treated as a data error, so no buy.
- Tests added. All suites and pyflakes pass.
