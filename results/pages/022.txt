Lean – LA RAMS (-4) over New Orleans
Lean – Over (46)
· Rams RB Kyren Williams ran against a light box on 78% of his carries last week and he leads the NFL with 660 rushing yards versus light boxes this season. I expect Saints head coach Dennis Allen to bring 3rd LB Zack Baun onto the field to contain the Los Angeles ground game and the defensive scheme could give Los Angeles trouble in the passing game as well.
· Sean McVay’s offense against man-coverage is averaging just 68% of the yards per attempt they’re averaging versus zone (30th) and Matthew Stafford might struggle as the New Orleans defense has a 31.6% man-coverage rate (7th-highest).
· However, WR Cooper Kupp will have a favorable matchup on the inside as Saints nickelback Alontae Taylor is surrendering 1.37 yards per cover snap in the slot (6th-worst).
· The Los Angeles offense will get back starting right tackle Rob Havenstein, who ranks 11th in pass-blocking efficiency. Havenstein is worth 0.8 points by our numbers.
· Meanwhile, New Orleans will likely be without starting RT Ryan Ramczyk but they will have WR Chris Olave back on the field. Olave has a 55% success rate (19th).
· Rams’ interior defender Aaron Donald ranks 3rd in pass-rushing efficiency but he might be slowed as the Saints’ interior offensive line is at fault for 46% of the allowed pressures (10th-fewest).
· Our model favors the Rams by 5.8 points, with a predicted total of 47.9 points, and LA applies to a 71-34-3 ATS Thursday night situation.

Lean – LA CHARGERS (+12) over Buffalo
· Bills’ running back James Cook had a career-high 25 rushing attempts and a 64% success rate in last week’s win versus the Cowboys and I expect to see plenty more of Buffalo’s ground game on Saturday night as the Chargers’ rush defense ranks 30th according to our numbers.
· Josh Allen did not throw an interception last week and he shouldn’t have 14 interceptions this year (2nd-most) as he hasn’t been as wild as other seasons, recording just a 2.5% PFF turnover worth play rate (4th-lowest).
· Allen can continue to safe throws this week as RB James Cook is averaging 1.71 yards per route run (3rd) and Los Angeles is allowing 0.09 EPA/target to running backs (28th).
· Bills LG Connor McGovern has conceded only 19 pressures (12th-fewest) and he will contain interior defender Morgan Fox, who ranks 9th in pass-rushing efficiency.
· Chargers’ backup Easton Stick averaged 6.7 yppp against the Raiders, with most of his success coming in garbage time, which was most of the game in their lopsided 21-63 loss.
· Los Angeles WR Keenan Allen has a 59% success rate (8th) and he missed Stick’s first start and might be out again this week. A banged-up Allen would struggle against Buffalo nickelback Taron Johnson, who is allowing just 0.78 yards per cover snap in the slot (3rd).
· Stick will not have clean pockets to work from in this game. The Chargers lost backup center Will Clapp and it’ll be 3rd string C Brenden Jaimes versus interior defender Ed Oliver, who ranks 6th in pass-rushing efficiency.
· Bills edge defender Greg Rousseau ranks 13th in pass rushing efficiency and he will wreak havoc across from RT Trey Pipkins, who has surrendered 48 pressures (2nd-most).
· Stick will get the ball out quickly to RB Austin Ekeler, who is averaging 1.39 yards per route run (8th). Buffalo’s defense is allowing a league-high 52% pass success rate to running backs so Ekeler should have a productive game catching passes.
· Los Angeles fired Brandon Staley and made Giff Smith the interim head coach. We could see a boost particularly from the defense as Smith was the outside linebackers coach previously. Teams are 15-11 ATS since 2010 the game after firing a head coach with the Panthers not covering being the only instance in 2023.
· Home underdogs of more than 4 points are 45-22 ATS the week after losing by 35 points or more, including 10-2 ATS getting more than 10 points, and the market appears to have overreacted to last Thursday’s result.
· Our model favors the Bills by 10.0 points, with a predicted total of 45.4 points.

ATLANTA (-1) vs Indianapolis
· The Indianapolis offense will likely be without two influential starters on Sunday. Colts RT Braden Smith suffered a knee injury during the first quarter of the week 13 game against Tennessee and isn’t quite ready to come back. Backup RT Blake Freeland ranks 51st in pass-blocking efficiency out of 54 qualifying tackles.
· Indianapolis WR Michael Pittman is averaging 2.06 yards per route run (18th) but he will likely be sidelined and remaining outside WR Alec Pierce will be limited by cornerback AJ Terrell, who is allowing only 0.83 yards per cover snap (14th).
· Gardner Minshew will look for slot WR Josh Downs, who has a 55% success rate (20th). Falcons nickelback Dee Alford is surrendering 1.51 yards per cover snap in the slot (3rd-worst).
· The Colts will be contained on the ground as Atlanta’s defense is allowing -0.21 EPA/rush (2nd).
· The Falcons offense averages 31.6 runs per game (2nd) but they were stymied last week without three starters along the offensive line. Atlanta’s offense blocked for a league-low -0.1 yards before contact in Carolina, but I do not expect this to repeat on Sunday.
· Falcons’ center Drew Dalman and right guard Chris Lindstrom will likely be back on the field to lead Atlanta’s ground game versus an Indianapolis defense surrendering a 42.3% rush success rate (26th). Lindstrom is also will be critical in pass protection as he’s allowed a league-low 12 pressures.
· Desmond Ridder was benched, and I don’t think there is a major difference between him and Taylor Heinicke. Ridder has a 46.6% success rate and is averaging -0.06 EPA/play this season. Heinicke has a 43.3% success rate while averaging 0.05 EPA/play.
· Our model favors the Falcons by 0.1 points, with a predicted total of 41.9 points, but Indy applies to a 78-150-10 late-season road team situation. I used Atlanta in my pool, as they have a 52.4% chance of covering at -1.

· Tennessee’s defense will be without interior defender Jeffery Simmons, who ranks 12th in pass-rushing efficiency and has been sidelined for the last two games.
· The Titans will also likely be without both starting cornerbacks Sean Murphy-Bunting and Kristian Fulton. Backups Eric Garror and Tre Avery will get torched by Seahawks wide receivers DK Metcalf and Tyler Lockett.
