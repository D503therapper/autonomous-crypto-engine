Notre Dame released its newest injury report and set of depth charts on Monday as the Irish prepare to travel to Chapel Hill to take on the North Carolina Tar Heels.
The Irish got some bad news about WR Quincy Porter, who will
miss the entire season
after knee surgry scheduled this week.
Notre Dame does, however, get safety Luke Talich back from his wrist injury. Freshman Joey O’Brien filled in for Talich against Purdue and had a really good game — so expect more of a rotation moving forward as Notre Dame will try to keep bodies healthy and fresh for the entire season.
