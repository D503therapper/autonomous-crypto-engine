TITLE: Undefeated JMU welcomes Marshall team that’s had its number at Bridgeforth
DATES SEEN: ['Oct. 1, 2026']

“They’re tough,” Napier said. “The total defense, scoring defense, is middle of the pack, but the red zone space is where they really succeed in forcing some field goals and keeping the game close.”
Every Herd victory this season has been decided by a single possession, while the Dukes have yet to play in a tight fourth quarter.
Kickoff between JMU and Marshall is scheduled for 3:45 p.m. Saturday at Bridgeforth Stadium.
Availability Report
James Madison quarterback JC Evans is listed as questionable for Saturday. Napier says Evans remains day-to-day as he recovers from a lower body injury.
Wide receiver Za’Ron Collins is listed as doubtful after suffering an injury at Old Dominion.
Zach Greenberg, who did not play at Old Dominion, was not listed on the availability report, signaling he should be available against Marshall.
Marshall has ruled out linebacker Jalen Marshall.
