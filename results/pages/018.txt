Five-Star Plus+
product who flipped his commitment from the Bulldogs to the Commodores on National Signing Day, has appeared in all four of Vanderbilt’s games this season, including starting the last two. He has thrown for 746 yards and four touchdowns to three interceptions on 63% passing so far this season.

Meanwhile, Frazier could be in line to miss a second consecutive game after sitting out last week’s 41-13 win over Oklahoma. Despite missing last week, Frazier still leads Georgia’s crowded backfield with a team-high 202 rushing yards and five touchdowns. Kickoff between Vanderbilt and Georgia is set for 12:45 pm ET Saturday inside Sanford Stadium in Athens.
