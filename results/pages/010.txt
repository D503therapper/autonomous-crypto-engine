Vanderbilt football
has added two more names to its list of players with season-ending injuries ahead of the trip to Mississippi State.
Linebacker Josiah Broxton suffered a torn ACL, and running back Evan Hampton, who was injured against Kentucky, has a fractured leg, according to coach Clark Lea.
Both of those players were listed as out on the SEC's availability report ahead of the game Nov. 7 (3:15 p.m. CT, SEC Network) at Davis-Wade Stadium in Starkville.
Cornerback Jaylin Lackey and linebacker Jamison Curtis were both listed as probable after dealing with long-term injuries. Lackey hasn't played this season and Curtis played only in the opener.
Mississippi State quarterback Kamario Taylor is out for the season, and the likelihood is that Vanderbilt will see
AJ Swann
as the quarterback, as he was not listed on the report after suffering an injury last week.
