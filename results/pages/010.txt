COLLEGE STATION, Texas (KBTX) - A key pair of A&M starters began the week on the first Southeastern Conference availability report and was dropped Wednesday before the Aggies’ road trip to South Carolina.
Running back KJ Edwards and linebacker Noah Mikhail are listed as questionable for the game, which means there is a 50% chance they will play, according to SEC policy. Wide receiver Terry Bussey (out for season), offensive lineman Blake Ivy (out for season) and wide receiver Kelshaun Johnson remain out.
Edwards was leg-whipped during the Aggies’ win over The Citadel, resulting in a contusion on his leg. He reaggravated the injury and only picked up three carries after the first quarter.
Tuesday, the freshman spoke with the media and indicated he was back to full health.
“It happened on The Citadel week,” Edwards said. “Kind of got banged up, nicked up and bruised up. Alabama, just kind of landed on it wrong, so it was just kind of bothering me a little bit. I’m back right, back good, ready to go.”
Despite starting the season as the Aggies’ third-string running back, Edwards has a team-high 360 yards and two rushing touchdowns on 55 carries. He has 135 total snaps this season, second only to Rueben Owens II’s 279.
Mikhail has dealt with nagging injuries throughout the season, Elko said after A&M’s loss at Missouri. However, he’s played in every one of A&M’s games this season, save for The Citadel.
The sophomore is second on the team in tackles with 36, including a tackle for loss. Mikhail has played more snaps than any other linebacker on A&M’s roster, 322, and has recorded 18 defensive stops this season, more than anyone on the team, according to Pro Football Focus.
Johnson last saw action in A&M’s win over Arkansas and was officially listed as out on the SEC availability report beginning the Wednesday prior to the Alabama game.
This season, Johnson has four catches on seven targets for 34 yards.
South Carolina listed three players as questionable, including key contributors in wide receiver Donovan Murph and free safety J’Zavien Currence.
Murph has collected 205 receiving yards and three touchdowns on 11 catches and 20 targets. He’s played 274 snaps this season, according to Iron Football. Currence has 24 tackles in 236 snaps played.
The Aggies and Gamecocks kick off at 2 p.m. Saturday in Williams-Brice Stadium. The game will be broadcast on ESPN.
