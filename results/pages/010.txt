Thus far, the Aggies hold a record of 7-4 in midweek CUSA games. This will mark the third time that the Aggies collide with FIU during the midweek slate after also doing so in 2023 and 2024. In fact, FIU was the Aggies' first-ever midweek opponent when they faced off in Las Cruces on Wednesday, Oct. 4, 2023.
MOVE THE STICKS
Through five games, the Aggie offense has been one of the best in the country at converting on third down. Currently, NM State is converting on third down at a 46.3% clip to rank second in the conference and 37th in the nation.
THOMAS' COMING OUT PARTY
Sophomore tight end
Josiah Thomas
has emerged as one of the Aggies' top receiving targets as he has now tallied nine catches for 150 yards over the course of the last two games. With 60 yards against WKU on Thursday, he now has 226 receiving yards and is just two yards shy of matching his season total from last season when he played for Western Carolina.
After just three of the Aggies' 12 games were featured on national networks in 2025, the Aggies will find themselves in the national spotlight six times this season. This year, NM State is slated to play three games on ESPN2, two games on CBS Sports Network and one game on The CW.
Week 6 marks the fourth time this season that the Aggies will play on national television as Cooper Boardman (PxP) and Dave Clawson (Analyst) will be on the call for ESPN2.
WHERE TO LISTEN TO THE AGGIES
Once again, all games this season will air live on 99.5 FM KXPZ for those in Las Cruces and the surrounding areas. Radio broadcasts for all Aggie games will also be available to listen to nationwide via the TuneIn App. Radio play-by-play for Wednesday's game will be provided by MJ Newsom, with analysis provided by Cory Lucas.
Additionally, all home games will be available in Spanish on 96.7 FM La Equis with the call provided by Darien Salayandia (play-by-play) and Jose Aragon (analyst).
