San Jose State vs Hawaii Quick Picks and Prediction
San Jose State vs Hawaii Key Matchups and Game Preview
Hawaii’s season has unraveled quickly, and the root of the problem is an offense that cannot score consistently. San Jose State, meanwhile, has a reliable running game, a quarterback producing solid numbers and the benefit of an extra week of rest. Add in a five-game series winning streak and a pair of emotional homecomings for the Spartans, and the road underdog holds several edges in this matchup.
Hawaii Season Off the Rails
The Rainbow Warriors were considered a chic longshot pick to make the 12-team playoff out of the Group of Six, but the season has not gone as planned for head coach Timmy Chang. Hawaii has opened with three losses in its first four games, falling at Stanford, at home to UNLV and last week at Wyoming against a punchless offense. At this pace, the Rainbow Warriors may struggle to qualify even for the hometown Sheraton Hawaii Bowl, which keeps a spot open for them if they become postseason eligible.
Hawaii QB Micah Alejado Regression
The offense has been the biggest problem. Aside from brief eruptions in the opener at Stanford and two weeks later against New Mexico State at the Ching Complex, it has misfired consistently. Quarterback Micah Alejado has thrown only three touchdown passes one-third of the way through the season after tossing 24 a year ago. He has produced just one touchdown pass across the past three games. That kind of drop-off makes it very difficult to trust Hawaii as a favorite.

San Jose State Rested After Bye
The Spartans are properly rested for the nonstop flight to Honolulu after having last week off. They used the bye to regroup following a home loss to a physical Fresno State team. Extra preparation time and fresh legs are significant advantages for a team making a long road trip, especially against an opponent coming off a deflating loss.
Spartans Homecoming for Niumatalolo and Weaver
This is a special trip for island native and alum Ken Niumatalolo, the San Jose State head coach. It is also a homecoming for former Hawaii quarterback Luke Weaver, who is posting better numbers than Alejado, the quarterback who beat him out in Honolulu. Weaver has thrown nine touchdown passes so far, three times as many as Alejado. Expect plenty of extra motivation on the San Jose State sideline.
SJSU Five-Game Series Win Streak
San Jose State has won five straight meetings in this series. Niumatalolo’s Spartans won last fall’s shootout 45-38 in Silicon Valley to extend that streak. That game was high-scoring, but Hawaii’s offense is far less productive now, which changes the outlook for this year’s total.
