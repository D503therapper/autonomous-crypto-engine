2-Star Best Bet – *Washington (+1) over CHICAGO
· The Commanders remain undervalued after losing their fourth game in a row last Sunday. Washington gained 6.6 yppl to Tennessee’s 3.9 yppl but converted just two of 13 third and fourth downs. That failure to move the chains last week was nothing more than variance and is not likely to continue.
· The Commanders limited Derrick Henry to 3.6 yards per carry last week and the rush defense ranks 7th in our metrics which will be crucial on Thursday night.
· The Bears are passing on less than 40% of the snaps on offense. Nobody has finished the season with less than 40% pass-play rate since the Steelers in 2004.
· Chicago’s offensive line has allowed 47 pressures which is league-average, but they only have 129 pass snaps. Justin Fields is being pressured on 48% of dropbacks, which is the third highest rate in the league.
· Washington’s interior defensive line will have a field day in this matchup. DTs Jonathan Allen and Daron Payne each have 14 pressures. Bears guard Cody Whitehair was conceding just a 3.6% pressure rate, but he is sidelined with a knee-related injury. Chicago center Sam Mustipher ranks 31 in pass blocking efficiency.
· Commanders edge rusher Montez Sweat has 18 pressures (15th) and will line up across from rookie LT Braxton Jones, who ranks last in pass blocking efficiency.
· Bears head coach Matt Eberflus uses a shell defense that leaves open the underneath areas of the field and that should give Carson Wentz some gimme throws. Washington’s RBs have a 25% target share (4th-highest) and Chicago’s defense is surrendering 6.3 yards per target to running backs (8th-worst).
· Our model favors the Commanders by 3.5 points, with a predicted total of 39.4 points, and the matchups are favorable.
Washington is a 2-Star Best Bet at -1.5 or better and a 1-Star to -2.5
.

Tampa Bay (-8.5) vs PITTSBURGH
· Far from a painless beginning to a career for Kenny Pickett with his first start against the Bills and the Buccaneers on deck this week. Pickett threw for 5.6 yppp last week and his teammates dropped five passes.
· Steelers TE Pat Freiermuth suffered a concussion in the third quarter versus the Bills and will likely miss this game. Freiermuth is averaging 7.3 yards per target while Pittsburgh’s WRs George Pickens, Diontae Johnson, and Chase Claypool are combining to gain 6.0 yards per target.
· Buccaneers’ cornerback Jamel Dean is 4th in PFF coverage grade and he will shut down Diontae Johnson.
· Pickens is averaging 2.50 yards per route run the last two weeks and has an opportunity to keep it going on Sunday against Tampa Bay CB Carlton Davis, who has been targeted on 42 passing attempts (2nd-most).
· The Steelers defense has surrendered 28 explosive plays (31st) and can’t generate pressure without All-Pro edge rusher TJ Watt.
· Tom Brady will look for Chris Godwin in a favorable matchup on the inside versus Pittsburgh’s nickelback Arthur Maulet, who is allowing 1.82 yards per slot cover snap.
· Leonard Fournette had six receptions on flat routes for 46 yards and a touchdown last week, but he will likely be contained in this game because Pittsburgh’s defense is conceding 4.7 yards per target to RBs (8th).
· Tampa Bay’s rookie LG Luke Goedeke is off to a rocky start to his career, and he will struggle against All-Pro DT Cameron Heyward, who has 14 pressures (8th).
· Our model favors the Buccaneers by 10.3 points, with a predicted total of 46.1 points, but Pittsburgh applies to an 82-27-2 ATS bounce-back situation that plays on big home dogs after losing by more than 21 points. The Steelers are also 37-13-1 ATS after losing a game by more than 17 points, including 8-2 ATS under coach Tomlin.

Lean – Carolina (+10) over LA RAMS
Lean – Under (41)
· The lookahead line for this game last week was LA -9. Both the Panthers and Rams underperformed in week 5 so I think the move can mostly be explained by Baker Mayfield’s high ankle sprain.
· We would have bet Carolina in some capacity if Mayfield was healthy, but the market didn’t adjust nearly enough for the downgrade to backup quarterback PJ Walker. The move from a 9-point spread to -10.5 represents a change of only 3.6% in win probability and the line has since moved back to -10.
· Our numbers suggest the drop from Mayfield to Walker is more like 12% of win probability.
· Walker has a career 4.9 yppp but most of it was with sharp offensive coordinator Joe Brady. According to our metrics, Brady was about a point better at calling plays than current offensive coordinator Ben McAdoo.
· Walker’s 6.3% career interception rate is unsustainably high, but I don’t think an expected 4.0% interception rate is out of the question. We would project Walker for 4.5 yppp versus an average defense in average conditions. I think it’s unlikely the Panthers will score more than two TDs on Sunday.
· Carolina wide receiver DJ Moore was gaining 1.99 yards per route run for his career coming into this season but is averaging just 1.09 yards per route run in 2022. All-Pro cornerback Jalen Ramsey will lock down Moore in this game.
· Christian McCaffrey is gaining 1.36 yards per route run (7th) but he will be limited by a Rams defense allowing only 4.8 yards per target to running backs (10th).
· It’s worth noting the Panthers fired head coach Matt Rhule and defensive coordinator Phil Snow, but the new regime won’t make a significant difference in my opinion.
· The Rams currently rank 30th in EPA per play with the offensive line taking a massive step back from the Super Bowl season last year. Los Angeles has surrendered the most pressures in the NFL (84) after allowing only 144 all last season (7th-fewest).
· The Rams interior offensive line is in shambles. Starting center Brian Allen has yet to return from a knee injury in the season opener. Left guard David Edwards will likely be out after getting two concussions in the previous two weeks. Right guard Coleman Shelton replaced Edwards at center before his high ankle sprain in week 4. Backup guard Logan Bruss is out for the season with a torn ACL. Another backup guard Tremayne Anchrum is out for the season with a fractured fibula.
· Carolina’s interior defenders Matt Ioannidis and Derrick Brown should provide pressure on Matthew Stafford all game. Ioannidis ranks 17th in pass rushing efficiency and Brown two batted passes with four stops on defense last week.
· Los Angeles LT Joseph Noteboom ranks 2nd-worst in pass blocking efficiency, and he will not be able to slow down edge rusher Brian Burns, who has 23 pressures (3rd).
· Our model favors the Rams by just 8.3 points even after adjusting 3.8 points for Walker in place of Mayfield at quarterback for the Panthers. The predicted total is just 39.9 points.

Lean – KANSAS CITY (+2.5/+3) over Buffalo
· The market closed Chiefs -2 and 54 last January in the playoffs when Buffalo traveled to Kansas City. We’re seeing the total right around the same spot, but the Bills have flipped to decisive favorites. Patrick Mahomes had been favored in his first 41 home starts until this week.
· Mahomes completed 16 of his 23 attempts targeting the seams for +13.0 expected points added last Monday night. TE Travis Kelce snagged seven receptions and had a career-high four touchdowns against the Raiders, but Kansas City’s offense is likely to be shut down in the middle of the field in this matchup.
· Buffalo’s defense is conceding only 4.8 yards per target to TEs (4th). The Bills limited standout tight ends Mark Andrews and Pat Freiermuth to two receptions each in the last two weeks. Furthermore, starting S Jordan Poyer and LB Tremaine Edmunds are on track to return for this game.
· Buffalo edge defender Von Miller ranks 10th in pass rushing efficiency and he has too much speed for LT Orlando Brown, who has allowed 15 pressures (7th-most).
· Chiefs DT Chris Jones had four hurries, a quarterback hit, and sack versus Las Vegas. The Pro Bowl interior defender is likely to put pressure on Josh Allen in this game, as Bills RG Ryan Bates has surrendered 10 pressures (16th-most).
· I expect Allen to pick on Kansas City’s rookie cornerback Jaylen Watson when he does have time to throw. Buffalo WR Gabe Davis is averaging 7 targets over his last eight games when he hasn’t been on the injury report and Stefon Diggs is gaining 2.67 yards per route run (4th).
· Our model favors the Bills by only 0.1 with a predicted total of 52.7.
