Notre Dame football injury report vs. Miami: Irish without multiple starters for marquee matchup
Kyle Kelly
@ ByKyleKelly
11/02/26
On Monday, the Notre Dame football communications team listed senior linebacker
Drayk Bowen
(right ankle) as
out
and defensive linemen
Francis Brewu
(left knee) and
Jason Onye
(right knee) as
questionable
in its weekly availability update. Bowen, Brewu and Onye all suffered injuries in Saturday’s 35-13 win over Navy.
In his weekly news conference Monday, Freeman said Onye was injured on a chop block. Bowen and Brewu, however, were injured on what he described as normal football plays.
“Some of the injuries that happened could happen any game,” Freeman said. “Drayk’s was, somebody fell on his ankle. It was a pile. Brewu was the same thing. Somebody fell on his knee as he was trying to go make a play.
“Some things unfortunately happen in the game of football, and I don’t want to say it’s because we played Navy. We have to be alert of the high-lows (blocks) on the backside and try to protect ourselves. Also, if it’s illegal, then make sure the referees call it.”
The Irish are already without starting field cornerback
DJ McKinney
(right ankle) and field safety
Brauntae Johnson
(left foot).
McKinney’s injury, suffered Oct. 17 against BYU, will keep him out even longer. Last Thursday, head coach
Marcus Freeman
said the fifth-year transfer from Colorado would be out for “an extended period of football.” On Oct. 12, Freeman said Johnson will return in November. He has not played since the first half of the Wisconsin game Sept. 6.
Notre Dame will have junior linebacker
Kyngstonn Viliamu-Asa
. He does not have an injury designation after missing the Navy and Stanford games due to a left thumb injury.

When Freeman last spoke about Jagusah’s injury Oct. 12, he said they were hopeful he could return at some point this season, which is why he has not been ruled out for the year.
“Absolutely,”
Freeman said Oct. 12
, when asked if he continues to be hopeful Jagusah can play this season. “We have a developmental lift on Friday mornings, and I was in there with those guys, and I saw big Charles hang cleaning some good weight. He said that’s the first time he’s hang cleaned that much weight and there’s progress. He’s making progress in his bicep and tricep.
“I continue to remain hopeful, as does he. We just kind of got to trust the process. Like, keep working, keep working. When you’re dealing with nerves, you’re dealing with a traumatic injury like he had, it takes time. But as long as you continue to see progress, there’s a lot of hope. I hope we do see him this year.”
On Sept. 28, Freeman said Porter had been ruled out for the season. The Ohio State transfer had not appeared in a game this season due to a lingering left patella injury.
“He came to Notre Dame post-patella surgery, and it was a pretty long, tough healing proccess,”
Freeman said Sept. 28
