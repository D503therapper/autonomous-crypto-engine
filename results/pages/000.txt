Temple turns back to Jaxon Smolik at quarterback as it prepares for a short week
With Ajani Sheppard's struggles on Saturday and nursing an ankle injury, K.C. Keeler will start Smolik on Thursday night when Temple hosts Rice for its final home game of the season.
Temple quarterback Jaxon Smolik has thrown for 302 yards and four touchdowns this season.

Isaiah Vazquez / For The Inquirer
Temple’s offense stalls as Owls drop fifth straight with 36-10 loss to UAB
Smolik back at QB
Ajani Sheppard has played the bulk of snaps this season. On Saturday, he struggled to produce in the passing game.
Sheppard totaled 85 passing yards and one touchdown pass on 30 attempts against UAB and took Temple’s first safety of the season. Keeler attributed the struggles to injuries — both his and the offensive line’s. Sheppard injured his ankle at East Carolina.
Sheppard didn’t practice on Monday after getting banged up against the Blazers, giving Jaxon Smolik another shot. He last played in Temple’s 42-35 loss to Navy on Nov. 7 and threw three touchdown passes.
“Jaxon gets an opportunity to kind of show us where he is with things,” Keeler said. “I went to Coach Walker on Sunday, I said I want to see Jaxon out there.”
Tyler Walker is the offensive coordinator and quarterbacks coach.
Keeler also announced that running back Sam Brown V will miss the rest of the season with a rib injury. The Rutgers transfer was limited to five games due to injury.
Keeler maintains positivity
The 67-year/-/old coach is standing in uncharted territory. Keeler is experiencing consecutive losing seasons for the first time as a head coach.
“I don’t sleep. This affects me,” he said. “I’m built uniquely where I see life as a challenge, and I’m willing to hit anything head-on. And I have no problem taking responsibility. Like, when this season is over, there’s definitely gonna be a realization that I didn’t have my best season.”
