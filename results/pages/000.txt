North Texas Mean Green outlook
SAN ANTONIO, TEXAS - NOVEMBER 14: Chris Jimerson Jr. #12 of the North Texas Mean Green throws a pass during the second half against the UTSA Roadrunners at the Alamodome on November 14, 2026 in San Antonio, Texas. (Photo by Zach Del Bello/Getty Images) | Getty Images
North Texas watched its six-game win streak and perfect conference record snap in a 35-16 loss at UTSA. However, the Mean Green lost more than just a consequential football game to a fellow conference contender. In the early third quarter, starting quarterback Tayven Jackson suffered an upper body injury that will sideline him for the remainder of the regular season, although head coach Neal Brown isn't ready to rule him out for the postseason.
Jackson was an integral portion of a North Texas offense that ranked first nationally in yardage and second in scoring prior to last week. The quarterback entered this week at sixth in the FBS in passing yards, eclipsing 300 yards in each of the five conference matchups he completed. Jackson's absence paves way for sophomore Chris Jimerson Jr.'s first collegiate start. Jimerson provided the Mean Green a spark against UTSA, checking into the contest with a 21-3 deficit and trimming it to 21-16 behind 70 passing yards, 33 rushing yards, and a touchdown through both the air and ground.
Without altering the scheme, Brown will call an offense tailored to Jimerson's skillset, while also highlighting the skill players that made North Texas so explosive during its win streak. Running back Jahiem ranks sixth nationally with 1,218 rushing yards and fifth with 16 touchdowns, and his success is a significant determinant of team success. When White eclipses 100 yards on the ground, North Texas is 7-0. When he finishes under 70, the Mean Green are 0-3.
North Texas will also lean on star receiver Justin Stevenson to get Jimerson settled in. Stevenson managed at least 100 receiving yards in the four games preceding the UTSA loss, producing 937 yards and eight scores on the season. Another frequent target in this balanced offense is tight end Max Reese, who ranks second on the Mean Green in receptions, receiving yards, and touchdowns.
