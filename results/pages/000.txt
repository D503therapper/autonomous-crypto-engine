OXFORD, Miss. –
The SEC’s Thursday availability report for Georgia week is out, and No. 10 Ole Miss left tackle
Terez Davis
is no longer listed after being probable Wednesday. Defensive end
Kam Franklin
and linebacker
Keaton Thomas
remain questionable for Saturday night.
Franklin and Thomas were questionable on Wednesday’s report as well, so their status has not changed. Davis and cornerback
Cedrick Beavers
were both probable Wednesday and are no longer listed, while cornerback
Jalyn Crawford
and safety
MJ Preston
remain probable. Running back
Kewan Lacy
is out again. The report was posted at 7:10 p.m. CT on Thursday.
Ole Miss (6-2, 4-2 SEC) hosts Georgia (7-1, 5-1 SEC) on Saturday, Nov. 7, at 6:30 p.m. CT at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium. Follow every update on our
Ole Miss football injury report
page.

Terez Davis comes off the Ole Miss injury report
Davis, a left tackle, was probable on Wednesday and does not appear on Thursday’s report, which means he has no availability designation heading into the weekend. Cornerback Cedrick Beavers was also probable Wednesday and is no longer listed. Crawford and Preston stay probable.
Keaton Thomas and Kam Franklin remain questionable
Thomas and Franklin carry the same questionable tag they had on Wednesday, so Ole Miss has not cleared either defender with Saturday night’s kickoff about two days away. Linebacker Luke Ferrelli, who has been dealing with an ankle injury, is not listed on the report for the second straight day. Kewan Lacy is out again as he works back from the shoulder injury he suffered at Texas, and the same nine Rebels are out as Wednesday.
Kam Franklin: status and 2026 stats
Franklin is listed as questionable on Thursday’s report, the same as Wednesday. Updated statuses are expected on the SEC’s pregame availability report before kickoff.
Franklin has 25 tackles, including 15 solo stops and 10 assists, along with 5.0 tackles for loss, two sacks, a pass breakup and a fumble recovery he returned for a touchdown through eight games this season. His best game came at Texas on Oct. 24, when he had six tackles and a sack. Early in the third quarter, he recovered a fumble in the end zone for a touchdown after Blake Purchase sacked Arch Manning and knocked the ball loose, cutting the Longhorns’ lead to 24-10. In the season opener against Louisville, he had four tackles, two for loss, and a sack, and he was named a leader of the defense alongside Will Echoles in
our September feature on Franklin
.

Without Franklin, Ole Miss would lose one of its best pass rushers and run stoppers against a Georgia offense that scores 41 points per game.
Georgia injury report
Georgia’s report is nearly the same as Wednesday’s. Outside linebacker Amaris Williams is out, and receiver Thomas Blackshear, running back Chauncey Bowens, tight end Elyiss Williams, inside linebacker AJ Kruah, safety Tyriq Green and defensive lineman Joseph Jonah-Ajonye are questionable. Receiver Isiah Canion, who was probable Wednesday, is no longer listed.
How to watch Ole Miss vs. Georgia
Kickoff is set for 6:30 p.m. CT on ABC, with Sean McDonough, Greg McElroy and Katie George on the call. David Kellum, Harry Harrison and John Darnell have the radio call.
