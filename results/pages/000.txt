TUSCALOOSA, Ala. -- Alabama center Racin Delgatty has been downgraded to doubtful for the Tide's road trip to Tennessee on Saturday, according to the Thursday night availability report.

Here is the full availability report for the Crimson Tide and the Volunteers:
Alabama Availability Report
OUT
Ryan Coleman-Williams, WR
Desmond Umeozulu, LB
Cayden Jones, LB (out for season)
Nick Sherman, DB
Jeremiah Beaman, DL (out for season)
Terrance Green, DL
DOUBTFUL
Racin Delgatty, OL
PROBABLE
Justin Hill, LB
With Delgatty unlikely to play for the Crimson Tide, Alabama will likely turn to sophomore Mal Waldrep Jr., who competed with William Sanders for the Tide's starting left guard position. Waldrep filled in at left guard in week two, earning his first career start, while William Sanders kicked to right guard to replace an injured Michael Carroll.

No. 6 Alabama will travel to Neyland Stadium to take on the 14th-ranked Tennessee Volunteers on Saturday, October 17. Kickoff is scheduled for 2:30 p.m. CT on ABC.
