Akron is still searching for its first conference win, going 0-5 in MAC play so far and 1-8 overall.
Akron boasts both the second-best passing offense in the conference and one of the top running backs in Jordan Gant. Gant has rushed for 100+ yards in four of the Zips' five MAC games this season. He's posted 730 yards total, with 7 touchdowns and averaging 5.1 yards per carry. Gant is also second in the conference in all-purpose yards per game, averaging 100.0. Freshman Cibastian Broughton recently started at quarterback in Akron's loss to Ohio. He's played in four games and is 75-for-100 passing with 612 yards, 4 touchdowns and 1 interception. UA's top receivers are Marcel Williams and Kyan Mason. Williams leads the team with 37 catches for 417 yards and has 3 touchdowns. Williams has caught at least two passes in every game this season. Mason has 31 catches for 405 yards but has yet to find the endzone.
Linebacker Donelius Johnson leads the team with 80 tackles and has 4.5 tackles for loss, while defensive lineman Cyrus Durham leads the team with 3.5 sacks and 5.0 tackles for loss to highlight the defense.
