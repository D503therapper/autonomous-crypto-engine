As formal injury reports were not released for this game, it’s tough to know the status of players across both sides, but Florida State was severely banged up entering last week’s game, and it stands to reason that it will remain without multiple key starters on both sides of the ball. Florida has been playing without Brown for most of the season and has won consecutive games with its three defensive starters out, though all three are internally listed as questionable this week.
Even looking at the ‘Noles recent successes, they have come over .500 ACC teams before last week’s 28-10 loss to a sub-.500 NC State team. Even with its struggles and inconsistencies, which could again show on the road after a holiday, the Gators defense should have little issue containing the hosts for most of the game, considering they mostly rely on one man, and Baugh should keep the offense moving with junior quarterback Aaron Philo spraying the ball around to a variety of pass catchers.
Friday could also serve as an opportunity for Sumrall to send a message about how far Florida has progressed in Year 1, though of course, FSU players will be looking to flex for their new coach, who will be evaluating the roster top-down in the coming weeks. If the Gators can take care of the ball, they can win by a touchdown or more. Despite believing last week’s game would fall below the total, Florida’s defense remains impossible to wholly trust, so leaning over makes more sense.
