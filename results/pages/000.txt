SETTING THE SCENE
Off to the best five-game start at 4-1 since 2021, UTSA will host South Florida in a nationally televised Thursday night contest at the Alamodome. Kickoff for the annual Lights Out game presented by Cowboys AC & Heating is set for 6:30 p.m. on ESPN. The Roadrunners are coming off a 16-14 road win over Rice to open American Conference play, while the Bulls dropped a 17-13 decision to Temple at home to fall to 4-1 overall and 1-1 in the league. This will mark the third meeting between the two schools on the gridiron with each team protecting its home turf in the previous matchups. UTSA registered a 49-21 victory on Nov. 17, 2023, in what was a Friday night broadcast on ESPN2, while USF defeated the Roadrunners, 55-23, in a Thursday night ESPN telecast on Nov. 6, 2025.

LIGHTS OUT
UTSA literally has been lights out in the program’s annual Lights Out games with victories in both of the previous contests. The Roadrunners recorded their first-ever win against a top-25 team in the inaugural Lights Out game with a 44-36 victory over No. 25 Memphis on Nov. 2, 2024. UTSA raced past eventual conference champion and College Football Playoff participant Tulane, 48-26, in the second Lights Out game on Oct. 30, 2025.
PACKING THE DOME
UTSA fans have packed the Alamodome over the past half decade, as six of the program’s 10 largest home crowds have been recorded during that span. The 2026 home schedule got underway with a crowd of 33,403 taking in UTSA’s 45-16 victory over UTRGV on Sept. 5. The Roadrunners drew 45,778 fans for the 2025 home opener against Texas State, marking the third-largest attendance for a UTSA game at the Alamodome. Since the return from COVID-19 protocols for the 2021 season, 885,210 fans have filed into the Alamodome to watch the Roadrunners, an average of 26,036 per contest. In the 2024 season opener against Kennesaw State, the program eclipsed the two-million fan mark, and now 2,311,463 spectators have watched the Roadrunners at the Alamodome, an average of 24,854 over 93 home contests. UTSA set NCAA modern startup program records in its first season by drawing 56,743 fans to the inaugural game against Northeastern State on Sept. 3, 2011, and by averaging 35,521 fans for six home contests.

SUCCESS ON THE ROAD
The Roadrunners have experienced success on the road so far in 2026. UTSA pulled out a 31-26 victory over Texas State in the first road contest of the season on Sept. 12. The Roadrunners dropped a 30-6 decision to No. 1 Texas on Sept. 19 but registered their second road win of the campaign with a 16-14 decision over Rice on Oct. 3. UTSA’s two road wins this year already match last year’s total and they have surpassed the 2024 schedule’s lone road triumph over Coastal Carolina. UTSA logged a 3-3 record on the road in 2023 after posting back-to-back 5-1 road ledgers in 2022 and 2021.
SWEET SIXTEEN
The Roadrunners is in its 16th season of football this year. UTSA started its program from scratch and, following a practice year in 2010, played its first season as an FCS Independent in 2011 before joining the Western Athletic Conference for the 2012 campaign. UTSA moved into Conference USA in 2013 and became a full-fledged FBS member starting with the 2014 season. The Roadrunners set NCAA modern startup program records in 2011 by drawing 56,743 fans to their inaugural game against Northeastern State and by averaging 35,521 fans for their six home contests that season. UTSA made its first postseason appearance at the 2016 New Mexico Bowl in its sixth season of play. The Roadrunners made history in 2021 by capturing their first conference championship with a 49-42 decision against WKU and capping a 12-2 ledger with their third bowl game. They repeated as league champions with a 48-27 win over North Texas in 2022 and made their third straight and fourth overall bowl appearance, ending the year with an 11-3 mark. In 2023, the Roadrunners notched their first bowl victory with a 35-17 decision over Marshall in the Frisco Bowl to cap a 9-4 ledger that include a 7-1 mark in their debut in the American. The 2024 campaign saw UTSA win four of its last five games, starting with a 44-36 victory over No. 25 Memphis for the program’s first-ever top-25 win and ending with a second straight bowl victory, a 44-15 triumph over Coastal Carolina in the Myrtle Beach Bowl, to finish at 7-6 overall. The Roadrunners capped a seven-win season in 2025 with a 57-20 rout of FIU in the First Responder Bowl, and they now have registered at least six wins in 10 of the 15 seasons in program history and reached the seven-win plateau eight times, including in each of the first six years of the

SCHEDULE BREAKDOWN
UTSA’s 12-game regular season schedule features four non-conference games and eight American Conference contests. The Roadrunners kicked off the campaign on Sept. 5 with a 45-16 home triumph over UTRGV, which played its inaugural season of football in 2025 as a member of the Southland Conference. UTSA played back-to-back road games at Texas State and Texas and closed out the non-league slate at home against Colorado State on Sept. 26. The Roadrunners have home conference games scheduled against South Florida (Oct. 8), Navy (Oct. 17), North Texas (Nov. 14) and Tulsa (Nov. 27 or 28), while they will travel to Rice (Oct. 3), Tulane (Oct. 24), Florida Atlantic (Nov. 5) and UAB (Nov. 21). The American Conference Championship Game will be played Dec. 5.
STAYING IN STATE
The Roadrunners will be staying close to home for the majority of the 2026 schedule. UTSA is slated to play its first seven games in the state of Texas, not leaving the Lone Star State until the Oct. 24 contest at Tulane in New Orleans, Louisiana. The Roadrunners have two more home games after that, giving them nine total regular season matchups inside their home state. In addition to the game in New Orleans, UTSA also will visit the states of Alabama (UAB) and Florida (Florida Atlantic) this fall.
