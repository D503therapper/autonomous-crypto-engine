Oregon State CB depth in flux again vs. San Diego State
Oregon State
's cornerback depth is once again in flux.
Isaiah Houi
is listed as questionable on OSU's availability report Thursday for Saturday's game with San Diego State.
Houi, who has started the past two games for the Beavers, has 12 tackles (one for loss) and three pass breakups.

If he is unable to play, OSU would be down to three scholarship cornerbacks: TJ Crandall, Trey Glasper and Blake Thompson.
Safety Sailasa Vadrawale III has taken reps at corner during practice the past two weeks as a contingency. Moving Vadrawale would alleviate some stress, as OSU's depth at deep safety is better with Drake Vickers, Jaheim Patterson and Xander Chisholm already in the rotation and Harlem Howard back off injury.
