Wake Forest wide receiver Carlos Hernandez (undisclosed) is questionable to play in the team's Week 7 contest against California. Across six games this season, Hernandez has racked up 49 receptions for 720 yards and four touchdowns, so he would be missed if he's unable to go this week. Hernandez has at least five receptions in each contest, while also going for at least 69 receiving yards - he has a touchdown in all but two games. Hernandez has been the clear top pass catcher for the Demon Deacons, leading the team in catches, receiving yards and touchdowns. Jack Foley and Kameron Shanks are the only other pass catchers on the team with over 10 receptions, and would help lead the passing game if Hernandez cannot go in Week 7.

Ole Miss starting safety Edwin Joseph (ankle) is out for the team's Week 7 matchup against Missouri. Over his first five games, Joseph had posted 18 combined tackles and also swatted down two passes. Oklahoma offensive lineman Michael Fasusi (shoulder) is questionable to play in the team's Week 7 game against Kentucky, while another offensive lineman, Eddy Pierre-Louis (undisclosed), is doubtful. Indiana running back Khobie Martin (forearm) is doubtful to play in Week 7 against Ohio State. He got injured in the team's season opener, and has not played since. Martin didn't get a carry or catch in his first game action, but had 505 rushing yards and six touchdowns across 78 carries last year - he also caught a pass for 14 yards. Without Martin around most of this season, and likely again in Week 7, Indiana has relied on Lee Beebe and Turbo Richard. Beebe has 98 rushing attempts for 551 yards and four touchdowns, while Richard has added 597 yards and five scores across 71 rushing attempts. Beebe has four receiving yards across two catches, while Richard has three receptions for 47 yards. Ohio State will be a tough challenge for both of those backs, as the Buckeyes allow just 246.8 total yards and 13.2 points per game so far this season.
