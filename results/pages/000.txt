Notre Dame football injury report: Irish will get defensive starter back vs. SMU
Kyle Kelly
@ ByKyleKelly
11/19/26
Share
Notre Dame starting defensive tackle
Francis Brewu
is no longer listed on the Fighting Irish’s availability report ahead of Saturday’s game against SMU.
The 6-foot-1, 285-pound junior was removed from Thursday’s availability update, meaning he is expected to be available to play. Notre Dame listed Brewu as questionable Monday with a knee injury that has sidelined him for the last two games. He suffered the injury Oct. 31 against Navy.
In addition to Brewu, sophomore defensive lineman
Christopher Burgess Jr.
was removed from Thursday’s availability update. He had previously been listed as out with a left tibia injury suffered before preseason camp. Saturday will mark the first time this season that he will be available to play.
There were no other changes from Monday’s availability update, meaning junior safety
Brauntae Johnson
(left foot), sophomore left tackle
Will Black
(concussion) and sixth-year defensive lineman
Jason Onye
(right knee) all remain questionable.
During a Thursday Zoom call with reporters, Irish coach
Marcus Freeman
said Johnson has been practicing this week. Freeman also said junior
Styles Prescod
would replace Black at left tackle if Black cannot play. Freeman did not provide an update on Onye.
Notre Dame’s football communications team will provide a final update on the availability of Johnson, Black and Onye approximately one hour before Saturday’s game, which is scheduled for 7:30 p.m. ET on NBC.

Notre Dame removed senior offensive tackle
Charles Jagusah
from its injury report Monday, giving him a chance to play for the first time since the 2024 season’s national championship game. Freeman confirmed Thursday that Jagusah has been practicing this week, marking the next step in his return-to-play progression.
“His next phase is getting to practice,” Freeman told reporters in Monday’s news conference. “And start to return to play. Let’s see what he can do in practice. Same thing with (senior offensive lineman)
Chris Terek
. We took him off the injury report. This is the next phase for him.
“That doesn’t mean he’s going to go out there and start on Saturday, but that means he is actually practicing starting this week. That is a huge — that’s amazing for that young man that has been through so much to be able to be at the point now where he can return to practice. …
“If he can help us and we can find a role, how cool would that be to do that on Saturday? His journey has been amazing in terms of the things he’s overcome. I’m so excited to see that guy out in practice this week.”
Freeman also explained Monday that Johnson’s availability would depend on how his body responded to football-specific movements in practice.
“He hasn’t been in practice,” Freeman said Monday. “He’s been training and rehabbing. We’re going to try to integrate him back into practice and see how his body responds. There are some football-specific movements that you do in practice that you can’t simulate and just rehab with Coach Landow (strength and conditioning coach
Loren Landow
) and (head athletic trainer)
.
“So, we’ll start to incorporate him Tuesday and see how he responds. If he responds well, get him in there Wednesday and see if there’s an opportunity for him this weekend to help us. If not, we’ll try to get him ready for the next one. This is the next phase of his progression of returning to play.”
When could Notre Dame get Drayk Bowen and DJ McKinney back?
Freeman said Oct. 29 that McKinney, a fifth-year transfer from Colorado, would be out for “an extended period of football” with a right ankle injury. The Irish have not provided a timetable for his return.
Bowen will miss his third consecutive game after suffering a significant right ankle injury against Navy on Oct. 31. Freeman has not provided a timetable for his return, although Bowen has not been ruled out for the season.
