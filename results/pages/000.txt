The Mustangs should have opportunities against a Syracuse defense allowing 337.1 yards and 26.3 points per contest. However, injuries could influence how SMU approaches the matchup.
SMU entered the week dealing with several absences, including running backs Dramekco Green and Link Rhodes, defensive lineman Ira Singleton IV and safety Tyren Polley Jr.
Polley is expected to miss the remainder of the season with a pectoral injury, while Rhodes is sidelined for the year with a knee injury.
Green's hand injury has created additional depth concerns at running back.
Wide receiver C. Reed, tight end M. Uter and kicker Nick Reed have also appeared on injury trackers with uncertain availability.

Their statuses require confirmation through the ACC's updated availability report before Saturday.
Syracuse faces potentially more significant questions at quarterback.
The Orange have used multiple quarterbacks this season because of injuries.
Steve Angeli, Amari Odom and Malachi Nelson have each produced 300-yard passing performances, but all three have dealt with availability concerns.
Odom and Nelson were ruled out for last week's North Carolina matchup, while Angeli's status remained uncertain. Their availability against SMU could substantially change Syracuse's offensive outlook.
