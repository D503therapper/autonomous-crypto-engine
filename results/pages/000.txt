About the Policy
For the second consecutive year, Atlantic Coast Conference institutions will provide public reports on the availability of football, men’s and women’s basketball, and baseball student-athletes to participate in each Conference game. Reports will identify when a student-athlete’s availability to participate in the upcoming contest is uncertain for any reason. This policy is intended to promote the well-being of student-athletes and institutional staff members, the protection of sensitive information, and the integrity of competition.

Key Information
Reporting Requirements
For Conference football games, availability reports shall be submitted each day starting three nights before the game and on game day two hours before the scheduled game time. For basketball and baseball games, availability reports shall be submitted the night before the game and two hours before the scheduled game time. However, only one report is submitted on the second and subsequent days when a team plays a game on consecutive days.
Student-Athlete Status Designations
For reports submitted before game day, a student-athlete will be designated with one of the following availability statuses:
Available
Probable
Questionable
Doubtful
Out
On game day, a student-athlete will be designated with one of the following availability statuses:
Available
Game Time Decision
Out
Availability Status Definitions
Available
Fully available to play in the game.
Probable
Likely to play; 75% chance.
Questionable
Uncertain to play; 50% chance.
Doubtful
Unlikely to play; 25% chance.
Out
Will not play in the game.
Game Time Decision
Final decision will be made closer to the game.
