TITLE: Troy Hosts Southern Miss in Sun Belt Opener - Troy University Athletics
DATES SEEN: []

Troy opens Sun Belt Conference play Tuesday night, hosting Southern Miss in a nationally televised game at The Vet. Kickoff is set for 7 p.m., and the game will be televised on ESPN2.
Fans can purchase $10 reserved tickets and $5 general admission tickets for the game. As part of the Take a Kid to the Game promotion, all youth 12 and under get in free with a paid adult ticket. Visit TroyTrojans.com/FBTickets or call 334-670-3681 to purchase tickets.
Troy (2-2) returns home following a pair of road games at No. 20 Missouri and Utah State. The Trojans opened the season with back-to-back victories over Sam Houston and Alabama State. Troy has won five straight games against the Golden Eagles (1-3) dating back to the 2021 season, including all four as Sun Belt foes.
This is the earliest meeting between Troy and Southern Miss as conference opponents; they met in the third SBC game in 2022 and have closed out the season each of the last three years.
The Trojans are looking to bounce back from a disappointing trip out West, where they dropped a 21-10 contest at Utah State. That came on the heels of a near-upset at Missouri the week before, as Troy held the Tigers to just 230 yards of total offense.
Despite winning the Sun Belt West Division in three of the last four years, the Trojans will be looking to win their league opener for just the third time in the past eight years. Troy was 5-1 against the Sun Belt West last season and is 18-6 against the division since moving from the East in 2022; the Trojans are 17-1 against the SBC West in the three years they won the division.
Southern Miss defeated Alcorn State (49-3) to open the season but have since dropped consecutive games to Auburn (43-8), UConn (48-20) and Tulane (24-21) in Blake Anderson's first season leading the program.
