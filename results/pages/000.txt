"What you saw the last couple weeks was also some solid defenses and some adverse situations," Carty said, "and we didn't overcome those. And that's unfortunate because that is what I've seen from an offense and a team that we've been around, is that somebody can overcome maybe having an absence at quarterback.
"We can overcome rain. We can overcome an injury. We can overcome maybe being a little bit outmanned at certain places, like we might have been at Virginia. And we didn't do that as a unit on offense and as a team."
Now they shall attempt to overcome at Middle Tennessee, or that is Carty's hope from the 10 days that have followed the gut-wrenching loss to Liberty.
