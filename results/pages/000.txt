Willie Simmons Coach’s Show Previews VICE Night on Monday Evening
Offensive Lineman Zaire Flournoy will join the program looking ahead to FIU's first CUSA game of the season
10/4/2026 12:00:00 PM
Story Links
MIAMI
— FIU football will host the next 2026 edition of the
Willie Simmons
Coach's Show on Monday evening, Oct. 5, at 6 p.m. from Twin Peaks Doral.
Host
Corey Brooks
will sit down with head coach
Willie Simmons
to preview FIU's upcoming Conference USA (CUSA) opening matchup against New Mexico State on Wednesday, Oct. 7 at 7:30 p.m., during VICE Night, as the Panthers will debut their new pink-inspired uniforms on ESPN2.
FIU offensive lineman
Zaire Flournoy
will join the show as a senior leader on the Panthers' offensive line. In addition, Amber Borreno, FIU's Program Manager for the Center for Leadership & Service, will discuss Relay For Life and efforts to raise awareness for Breast Cancer Awareness Month, which is observed annually in October.
For those unable to attend, the show will also be live-streamed at fiusports.com/TwinPeaksLive. Panther Nation can find additional coverage on FIU's social media accounts.
Twin Peaks will have its traditional Happy Hour deals extended until 7 p.m. for fans to enjoy in the patio area, of which are:
$1.50 Off 22 Oz. Peaks Drafts
$5.95 Select Appetizers
$1.50 Off Wine by the Glass
$3.75 Shots
During the event, promotional ticket giveaways will also be done for those in attendance. The show will of course lead into the NFL's Monday Night Football, of which Twin Peaks Doral will have its traditional deals, which are as follows:
$3.50 Shots
$5 Coors 22 oz. Drafts
$6 Heineken 22 oz. Drafts
$8 Caribbean Lemonade
Twin Peaks Doral will also host watch parties for all FIU away contests this season. A complete list of dates and times for both the
Willie Simmons
Coach's Show and the watch parties is below.
