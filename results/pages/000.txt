The Eastern Washington football team is headed to St. George for the first time ever this weekend for a Saturday afternoon matchup against Utah Tech, the newest member of the Big Sky Conference.
That the Thunderbirds (1-9, 1-6 Big Sky) sit at the bottom of the standings is perhaps not surprising. Utah Tech only made the jump to Division I FCS football in 2020, and it hasn’t won more than four games in a season since then.
But that the one-time national champion Eagles are right there with them at 2-8 overall and 1-6 in the Big Sky, well, that is a bit more surprising.
“We’re chasing being better, week in and week out, and we took a huge step back from two weeks ago to last week in terms of where we were headed,” EWU head coach Aaron Best said during Tuesday media availability. “We were on an upward trajectory, and last week I think we played our worst game of the year in all three phases. That’s hurtful.
“At the end of the year you should be playing better ball, more consistent ball, and we just haven’t done that.”
Historically, the Eagles have only been 2-8 overall one other time since moving up to Division I in 1984. That was in 2022, before the Eagles won their final game against Northern Colorado to finish 3-8. In 1988, the Eagles started the season with a tie, 31-31 against Portland State, and finished 2-8-1 overall.
So, if the Eagles are unable to get a win this week against Utah Tech or next week at home against Portland State, this year’s would go down as their worst record as a Division I program.
