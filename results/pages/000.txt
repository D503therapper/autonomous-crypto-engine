Meanwhile, there are two Bears players designated as
questionable

Jordan has served as a starter for Baylor for all of 2026, tallying 30 total tackles, 22 of which were solo, six passes defended and three interceptions, the latter accounting for 37.5% of the team's total so far this season. Should he be unable to play, fifth-year Reggie Bush II (no relation to the NFL running back), who has also tallied an interception this season, and senior Stilton McKelvey are listed as the Bear's backup options at the position.

Sophomore Michael Turner has made the most of this change with 19 of his 37 total carries on the season coming in the last three weeks.
