River Bell on the Line as Nicholls Hosts Southeastern Thursday Night
Story Links
Tickets
ESPN+
Nicholls Colonels vs. Southeastern Lions
Thibodaux, La. (Manning Field at John L. Guidry Stadium)
Thursday, Nov. 19 -- 6 p.m. CT
ESPN+:
LINK
Live Stats
:
Link
• The Nicholls State University football team wraps up the 2026 season with the annual River Bell Classic against rival Southeastern. The Colonels fell in their road finale at nationally-ranked UIW while the Lions rallied for a Senior Day win over McNeese. Nicholls is aiming to snap a two-game skid in the series and send its Senior Class off with the first win at Guidry Stadium in the River Bell Classic since 2018. Kickoff is scheduled for 6:00 p.m. and the game will air on ESPN+.
• The upset-minded Colonels held the lead over No. 22 UIW in the third quarter last weekend, but the Cardinals scored three unanswered touchdowns to keep their winning streak going as the Colonels fell, 42-24, at Benson Stadium. Nicholls found itself down 21-10 right before halftime but
Preston Welch
subbed in at quarterback and lofted a touchdown pass to
Devin Scura
with 33 seconds remaining. The defense came up clutch five minutes into the third when
Charlie Diedrich
, who garnered FCS National Freshman of the Week honorable mention, picked off a pass and returned it seven yards to give the Colonels their first and only lead of the day. But it would be the last points for the Colonels as UIW scored the final three touchdowns, extending their winning streak to five games and remain undefeated at home.
• Southeastern ended a three-game losing streak with a come-from-behind win over McNeese in its home finale. Trailing 17-0 late in the third, the Lions used a long touchdown pass, a fumble recovery for another score, and their best offensive drive to score 21 unanswered points for the victory. For the season, the Lions knocked off FBS member ULM and are the lone squad to beat Lamar in SLC play.
• While the River Bell Classic has been a back-and-forth series with SLU holding a 19-17 advantage thanks to its two-game winning streak, the Lions have won four straight in Guidry Stadium. Nicholls posted a 44-0 shutout in 2018 en route to its first of two straight Southland championships, but Southeastern has left Thibodaux with a victory in ever matchup since. In the last meeting at Guidry in 2024, the Lions kicked a field goal as time expired for a 19-16 victory.

A WIN OVER SOUTHEASTERN WOULD...
... improve the Colonels' record to 18-19 against the Lions.
... snap a four-game losing streak at home in the series with the last win being in 2018.
... secure a winning record at home for the eighth time in the last 10 seasons.
... be the fifth win for the Colonels -- their most since 2023 (6).
... improve the Colonels' home record to 8-3 under coach
Tommy Rybacki
.
BY THE NUMBERS
2
-- Linebacker
Charlie Diedrich
recorded his second interception of the season last weekend at UIW. Not only was it the freshman's second pick, but it was also the Colonels' second defensive touchdown on the season as he returned it seven yards for a score.
12
--
Kasai Jones
racked up a career-high 12 tackles last weekend at UIW, including a sack/forced fumble. Jones has 66 tackles on the year, just one off the team lead.
20
-- The Colonels have created 20 turnovers this season, ranking 12th in the FCS. Nicholls had six turnovers against MVSU -- three picks, two fumbles and a botched kick return, a fumble recovery at Kansas State, and two interceptions and another fumble against UTRGV.
Skylar Husband
notched his second interception of the season against Lamar and in the last home game, Nicholls recovered two fumbles. Husband notched his third pick of the year on the final play in the win at NSU, and
Charlie Diedrich
added one against McNeese.
Gary Bailey Jr.
and
Kevin Martin Jr.
added picks against East Texas A&M while
Kasai Jones
returned a fumble for the team's first defensive touchdown of the season. At UIW, Jones forced a fumble that was recovered by Diedrich, who later return an interception 7 yards for a touchdown. In 2025, Nicholls finished 17th in the FCS with 21 turnovers gained.
50
-- Freshman
Patrick Gales
reeled off the longest rush for the Colonels this season, going for 50 on a carry in the second quarter against East Texas A&M. Gales rushed for over 100 yards in his previous two games before totaling 88 yards at UIW.
77.6
-- Quarterback
Chase Richardson
has established himself as one of the top rushers in the SLC as he currently ranks first in the league with 77.6 yards rushing per game. Richardson has the fourth-most rushing yards at 543 despite playing in only seven games. Richardson had rushed for 100 yards or more in three straight games before leaving the game last week in the first half with 74 yards.
92
-- For the second straight year, the Nicholls roster features 92 Louisiana natives which is the most of any Division I roster this season. The Colonels have held the distinction for most of
Tommy Rybacki
's tenure on staff.
263
-- Nicholls notched a season-high for rushing yards for the second time in the last three games, totaling 263 yards at UIW. The Colonels have had at least 200 yards on the ground in each of the last four games.
