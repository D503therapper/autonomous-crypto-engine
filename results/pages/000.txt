Penn’s starting quarterback vs. Yale will be a game-time decision, says Rick Santos
The first-year head coach also addressed the team’s blunder in a loss to Columbia last weekend, describing this season as “foundational” for the young Quakers.
Penn coach Rick Santos said last weekend's loss to Columbia in the final seconds is "not what we want to be defined for."

0:00 min
When Penn hosts Yale on Friday night at Franklin Field, who will start under center for the Quakers remains a mystery.
Penn freshman quarterback Miles Teodecki is healthy and cleared to play, but head coach Rick Santos says it’ll be a “game-time decision.”
“We’re going to find out when we get to game day,” he added. “Both [junior quarterback Karson Siqueiros-Lasky] and Miles are going to take reps right now.”
Siqueiros-Lasky added: “It’s just a game of being prepared. Prepare like you’re the starter, regardless if you’re going to start or not.”
Last Friday against Columbia, Siqueiros-Lasky finished with 374 yards and two touchdowns. He also threw four interceptions. Penn gave up a 17-point lead, and miscommunication on the final play
resulted in a 28-27 loss.
In the final seconds, Penn got into field goal range, but was out of timeouts. Instead of spiking the ball to set up a clean kick, the field goal unit scrambled onto the field and rushed Mason Walters’ 51-yard field goal attempt, which fell short.
