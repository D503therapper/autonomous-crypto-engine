CLEMSON — Clemson head coach Dabo Swinney met with the media virtually via Zoom on Wednesday evening following the Tigers’ practice.
In his final media availability ahead of Saturday’s game vs. rival South Carolina, Swinney commented on where the Tigers stand from an injury perspective.
Swinney said that starting slot wide receiver Tyler Brown and the other players dealing with injuries right now are “day-to-day” to play against the Gamecocks.
“Everybody’s day-to-day, working their way,” Swinney said.
He added that they will keep injury statuses in-house until Saturday, as they don’t have to release an availability report this week, with it being a non-conference game.
“So, we’re not going to release a report this week,” Swinney said. “We’re just gonna show up and see who can play, and go get after it.” Saturday’s game between the 7-4 Tigers and 6-5 Gamecocks at Clemson’s Memorial Stadium is set to kick off at 3 p.m. The game will be televised on ESPN2
