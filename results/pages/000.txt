** Ball State hopes to even its MAC record at 1-1 following last week's MAC-opening loss at Kent State. The Rockets come to Muncie with four non-conference games under their belt, playing Ball State in their MAC opener.
** Ball State fell 26-13 at Kent State last week while the offense played without starting quarterback
Keldric Luster
. Injured with his left foot in a boot all week after the Liberty game, Luster didn't practice last week and gave way to reserve
Tyler Mizzell
, who took the reigns and passed for 164 yards against the Golden Flashes. Wide receiver
Donovan Hamilton
totaled a career-best 80 yards on seven catches and scored Ball State's first touchdown.
** Defensively, the Cardinals allowed 354 yards in total offense to Kent State, and struggled to contain the Golden Flashes who built a 17-0 lead before halftime. For the second straight week, fourth-year safety Sterling Smith recorded a career-high nine tackles to pace the Cardinals. Second-year linebacker
Mikeah Webster
had a career-best eight stops in his first action of the season, after missing the first three games with a lower leg injury.
** A continued area of concern for Ball State remains pre-snap and post-play penalties. Down from 11 penalties in games against both Stony Brook and Liberty, the Cardinals drew just seven yellow flags at Kent State, for 60 yards. Last year, the Cardinals averaged only 4.67 hankies per game -- second in the MAC and 22nd nationally.
