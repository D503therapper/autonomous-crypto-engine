Penn State took off for Seattle on Thursday afternoon out of Harrisburg International Airport. It is scheduled to arrive in Seattle around 8:15 p.m. ET.
“In our past, we’ve done this trip too — a couple times we’ve had to go to the West Coast, and even though we’re in a different time zone in central time zone, we always tried to go, if it was a day game, we tried to go especially on Thursday to get ourselves there, get settled in, and try to, from a sleep standpoint, try to get on the best rhythm that we possibly can and not lose sleep,” Matt Campbell said on Tuesday.
“I think the time that you would have to get up early to leave Friday morning not only gets you at home on Friday but then it also gets you on the trip out there. If it was a later game, maybe it would have been a different approach, but the fact of it being when the game time is plus the travel time, we just felt like this was the best situation for us.”
