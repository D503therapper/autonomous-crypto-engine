CHAPEL HILL, N.C. — Following a loss to Syracuse that saw North Carolina without several of its starters, the ACC has released its initial availability report ahead of the Tar Heels’ Saturday night matchup with No. 1 Miami. UNC starters
Peyton Waters
,
Jordan Hall
and
Andrew Threatt
have all been listed as questionable ahead of the Halloween night matchup with the Hurricanes.
Waters, one of Carolina’s starting safeties, has been listed as questionable for the second consecutive week after missing last weekend’s matchup with Syracuse due to a shoulder injury he sustained during the first quarter of UNC’s Oct. 17 win over Duke. The Los Angeles native was considered a game-time decision heading into last weekend’s loss before ultimately being ruled out.
Along the offensive line, Hall, the Tar Heels’ starting left tackle, reappears on the injury report after returning from a three-game absence last weekend. Elsewhere in the trenches, Threatt, UNC’s starting left guard, makes his first appearance on the list after exiting Carolina’s loss to Syracuse in the second quarter with a lower-body injury.
Elsewhere, Agumadu, a key part of UNC’s linebacker rotation, makes his first appearance on the injury report despite appearing to emerge from last weekend’s loss unscathed. He ranks sixth on the team with 32 total tackles this season.
For the Hurricanes, starting right guard Max Buchanan remains sidelined after suffering an injury in Miami’s Oct. 3 win at Clemson. Meanwhile, reserve wide receiver Joshua Moore and running back CharMar Brown have also been ruled out.

UNC will release updated availability reports at 8 p.m. each day leading up to Saturday’s matchup, with its final report due two hours before kickoff.
Under the ACC’s availability reporting policy, a probable designation indicates a 75-percent likelihood of playing, while questionable and doubtful designations indicate 50-percent and 25-percent likelihoods, respectively.
