Series History
Jan 4, 2015; Mobile, AL, USA; Arkansas State Red Wolves head coach Blake Anderson watches his team against the Toledo Rockets during the second quarter of the 2015 GoDaddy Bowl at Ladd-Peebles Stadium. Mandatory Credit: John David Mercer-USA TODAY Sports | IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect
Along with Blake Anderson, the Golden Eagles and Red Wolves share a big part of each other's histories. Southern Miss and Arkansas State first met in 1960 in Jonesboro, where the Red Wolves took a narrow 14-13 win. Over the last 66 years, the two teams have met 14 times in the regular season, and once in one of the most intriguing postseason games of all time.
