Injuries plague UMass football in 24-0 loss to Toledo
by
Ryan Ames
November 11, 2026
and get essential news each day.

The UMass football team fell back to .500 following its 24-0 loss to Toledo at the Glass Bowl on Wednesday night.
The Minutemen (5-5) mustered just 188 yards of total offense against the Rockets (8-2) and were without William “Pop” Watson III for much of the contest after the quarterback lasted just three plays before leaving the game with an injury.
