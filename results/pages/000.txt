JONESBORO, Ark. (KAIT) - The next 8 games will determine the story for Arkansas State football in 2026.
Sun Belt play kicks off Saturday and it’s also rivalry week. The 2-2 Red Wolves take a trip to 3-1 Louisiana.
“Everything runs through him, their quarterback (Lunch Winfield),” senior safety AG McGhee said. “He runs the show for them. Last year, they came here, he kinda had a show, they came out with a win. So executing, performing, tackling him, getting him down on the ground, stopping him, stopping the offense. Sustaining road focus, come up with a task and a plan.”
Trey Owens accounted for 270 yards and 2 TD in last week’s win over Kennesaw State. The freshman quarterback was asked about a stout Ragin Cajun defense. “They’re very good at disguising coverages and rolling certain coverages after blitzes,” Owens said. “One thing we noticed is they have everybody returning on their defense. They don’t have a single transfer, so they’ve been in that program and they know their defense well. They’re #19 in the nation in total defense, so it’s a great challenge for us. I’m looking forward to it, looking forward to the challenge.”

The Red Wolves provided injury updates on the
Sun Belt Conference Availability Report
. Kenyon Clay is listed as a Game Time Decision on the conference availability report. The All-Sun Belt running back missed last week’s game with a lower leg injury. Tre Stevens is listed as Out along with DE Sultan Badmus and OL Baker North. Stevens is 3rd on the team with 25 tackles, he suffered an injury in the non-conference finale. Badmus and North have played in multiple games in 2026.
”(Clay) done a really remarkable job of really willing himself to get back,“ head coach Butch Jones said on Tuesday. ”He’s (Tre Stevens) is another individual who has done a really good job in the training room. I think when you have an older player like Kenyon that leads the illustration of someone you want to emulate. The way he takes care of his body, the maturity. And then the players see him each and every day what he’s doing to get himself back. And so they see the standard that’s set in place by our older players, and that helps an individual like Tre Stevens. Plus Tre is as competitive as an individual as we have in our program, just like Kenyon.”
It’s Red Wolves and Ragin Cajuns in Lafayette, Louisiana. Kickoff is Saturday night at 7:00pm on ESPN+.
