Most of these injuries for the Aggies have already been known about or involve players who weren't getting snaps even when healthy. But there are a few notable additions or revelations about injuries. Chris Joe missed the game against Troy and is now listed as out for the season. Nikko Boncore had a bit of a breakout game against Troy, catching four passes for 66 yards, but is now listed as out for this game. Jackson Nelson has been in a walking boot since a Week 1 injury and Harrison Taggart's situation is obviously going to leave him out of commission (why he isn't listed as out for the season is odd).
In the initial injury report, Asher Cunningham was listed as questionable with John Randle Jr. and Tavo Motu'apuaka being listed as doubtful. Those designations have since changed as Cunningham is now probable and both Randle and Motu'apuaka are now listed as out.

Carson Rasmussen is the starting left guard for the Broncos who left the Western Michigan game partway through and is listed as out for this week. Two names to keep an eye on who are questionable are running back Sire Gaines and wide receiver Cam Bates. Neither are the top producer at their position, but they are contributors to the offense and the Broncos would be somewhat worse off without either.
Statistical Leaders
Passing
Utah State: Grady Brosterhous — 55/98 (56.1%), 635 yards (158.8/game), 4 TD, 6 INT
All-Time Series: Boise State leads 25-5
First-Ever Meeting: Nov. 15, 1975 (Utah State won 42-19)
Most Recent Meeting: Nov. 28, 2025 (Boise State won 25-24)
Year after year, the reality of this series from Utah State's perspective eats away at the program and its fanbase. The Aggies have won a single game against Boise State in the 21st century, an unexpected and wild 52-26 rout in 2015. And USU fans should thank the stars for that game because without it, the Broncos would own one of the longest winning streaks against a single opponent in NCAA history. Boise State has won 22 out of the last 23 matchups, dating back to 1998.
