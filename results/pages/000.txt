ATHENS — Georgia All-American KJ Bolden was a surprise addition to the Bulldogs’
injury report
Wednesday night.
Bolden is UGA’s fourth-leading tackler with 22 stops this season and one of four players with an interception.
UGA, ranked No. 1, plays Auburn at 3:30 p.m. on Saturday in Sanford Stadium.
The Tigers will be without preseason All-American linebacker Xavier Atkins, who is out unrelated to injury.
Auburn also listed offensive lineman Tai Buster as being out, along with tight end Hunter Herring.
Georgia tailback Nate Frazier, who injured his rib in a Sept. 26 game at Arkansas, was not listed on the injury report.
