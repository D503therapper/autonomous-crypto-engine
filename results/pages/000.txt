Florida State and Louisville have released their initial injury reports ahead of Friday night's clash, according to

. The Seminoles are without key receivers and defensive backs, while the Cardinals have their starting quarterback listed as probable. This report matters because it tells you exactly who will step onto the field when the teams meet at 7 PM on ESPN. Fans need to know the starting lineup before the game starts.
Florida State's Missing Pieces
The Seminoles are dealing with a long list of players who will not be available. Running back Samuel Singleton Jr. is out, along with wide receiver Devin Carter. The defense loses three defensive backs: Karson Hobbs, Jamari Howard, and Zae Thomas. Linebacker Blake Nichelson and Jordan Crutchfield are also out. The team is missing several other players on the defensive side of the ball, including Daylen Green and Noah LaVallee. These absences force coach Mike Norvell to shuffle his depth chart significantly before kickoff.

On the positive side, linebacker Mikai Gbayor and Karon Maycock are probable. Defensive back Antonio Cromartie Jr. and punter Daniel Hughes are also expected to play. However, defensive lineman Darryll Desir is questionable after not playing last week. The team released this list on Tuesday night to give fans a clear view of the roster status.
Cardinals Get Their Starter Back
Louisville has good news regarding their offense. Starting quarterback Lincoln Kienholz is listed as probable after being questionable earlier in the week. He started against NC State last week, so he is familiar with the game flow. The Cardinals also have defensive back Koen Entringer and lineman AJ Green listed as probable. However, they are without defensive back Antonio Watts and tight end Travis Egan. Starting quarterback Lincoln Kienholz's status is the biggest story for Louisville fans heading into the game.
The Cardinals are also missing several other key players. Running back Keyjuan Brown is questionable, along with offensive lineman Cason Henry. Defensive lineman Daylen Russell is also in the questionable group. The team is missing a large chunk of their offensive line depth with Bryten Close and Sam Secrest out. Wide receiver Montavin Quisenberry is also out for the game.
