Louisville and Stanford get together for just their second battle on the gridiron Saturday at 3:15 in L&N Federal Credit Union Stadium. The game will be streamed on the ACC Network. The initial injury report from the ACC was released last night and Louisville is missing a big name following the bye week.
On the Louisville side, listed as
OUT
:
WR #7 Lawayne McCoy
DB #9 Antonio Watts (out for season)
TE #44 Travis Egan
P #48 Jacob Barker
OT #63 Bryten Close
OL #67 James Glover-Tyson
TE #90 Hamilton Atkins
DL #98 Sam Dawson
The development here is Lawayne McCoy being listed as out. McCoy has emerged as one of the top targets for quarterback Lincoln Kienholz. The junior Florida State transfer has had multiple catches in each game this season including a catch of 30+ yards or more in all but one of Louisville’s games to date. He is 8th in the country with 20.5 yards per reception on 25 catches for 513 yards and four touchdowns.

Tre Richardson will have to pick up the slack. Richardson has only had one catch a pieve in the previous two games.
Nothing else is out of the ordinary on the OUT side, obviously seeing Antonio Watts’ name on that weekly list is a huge blow on the defensive side of the ball.
Listed as
DOUBTFUL
:
OL #70 Anwar O’neal
O’neal is a starter that has been in and out of the lineup all season. He returned earlier than usual from his initial injury for the SMU game but was ruled out for the Syracuse game and is now doubtful for the Stanford game. Lance Robinson will have to be ready to bounce around and play multiple positions again.
Listed as
QUESTIONABLE
:
DB #21 Jaydin Broadnax
DL #90 Daylen Russell
DL #99 Jerod Smith II

Rusell and Smith are guys that have seen significant playing time and each player has 16 tackles on the season. From a depth standpoint it would be nice to have at least one guy available. It is good to not see AJ Green, Micah Carter and Demeco Kennedy on the list this week. All three have previously been on weekly reports.
Listed as
PROBABLE
:
DL #3 Tyler Thompson
DB #12 DJ Waller Jr
OL #72 Sam Secrest
TE #85 Justyn Reid
DJ Waller was out for the Syracuse game, so from a depth perspective good to see him back likely to go.
Tyler Thompson was the ACC Defensive Lineman of the Week for his efforts at Syracuse. The North Carolina transfer finished the night with three tackles for loss, two sacks and a fumble recovery.
Reid has two catches on the season but has been a major piece as the H-back in the running game. His presence would be missed on the offensive line for a passing game missing one of their top targets.
