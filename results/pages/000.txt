Decade of the Govs
Now officially in the latter half of the 2020s, Austin Peay State University is one win away from exceeding its 43 wins in the 1980s as the winningest decade in program history. The Governors are 43-32 in the 2020s and their .573 winning percentage is the best in a decade in program history, edging out the .551 (32-26-1) winning percentage in the 1940s.
The APSU Govs are 25-12 at home during the 2020s, and their .676 winning percentage is the second best in a decade in APSU history, trailing only the .714 (20-8) winning percentage at home in the 1940s.

Offensive Options
Quarterback Destin Wade makes the offense hum for North Alabama. The signal-caller has scored nine times on the ground and added another six through the air for the Lions, favoring freshman Xavier Johnson (35 catches for 679 yards) as his preferred target. However, the Lions have struggled on third down (29.7 percent, 114th nationally) this season; the Govs will look to limit explosives on early downs.
Defensive Definition
Despite getting just four games out of Vili to this point in the season, the North Alabama defense has emerged as one of the best in the conference. The Lions get into the backfield; North Alabama leads the UAC in sacks (23) and tackles for loss (58), with junior Avery Howard leading the way in both categories as an individual (7.0 and 11.0, respectively).
Next Up For APSU Football
At long last, the Austin Peay State University football team has reached the bye week for the 2026 season, getting a well-earned respite next Saturday. The APSU Govs return to action Nov. 7 for the first of two in a row at Fortera Stadium, hosting longtime rival Eastern Kentucky in a 3:00pm contest for Stacheville’s Salute to Service game.
