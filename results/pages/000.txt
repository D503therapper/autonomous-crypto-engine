TITLE: Montana looks to end rivalry skid in Brawl of the Wild showdown
DATES SEEN: ['November 16, 2026']

The stakes extend beyond the rivalry. Montana needs a win at Bobcat Stadium to remain in contention for a share of the Big Sky title and a first round bye in the FCS playoffs.
Home field advantage has been a strength for Montana State under coach Brent Vigen. The Bobcats have won 45 of 47 games in Bozeman during his tenure and have beaten Montana in each of their trips to Bobcat Stadium under Vigen.
Montana quarterback Keali’i Ah Yat said the Grizzlies are preparing for a loud crowd.
“I try my best to block out the noise and the crowd,” Ah Yat said. “That has no effect on what we’re going to do this week. Obviously, we’ve got to be ready for it. It’s going to be loud in there. Playing there last year was loud, so we’ve got to be ready for that. I think we will be, and I’m just excited. I can’t wait to go there and play.”
Kickoff is scheduled for noon Saturday at Bobcat Stadium.
