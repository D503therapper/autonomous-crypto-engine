Clemson updates status of starters for Miami game on ACC report
Brandon Rink
·
SMS
@
Clemson (3-1, 2-0 ACC) hosts No. 4 Miami (4-0, 2-0) for a primetime Death Valley clash this Saturday (7:30 p.m./ABC).
The initial ACC availability report was released on Wednesday evening. Clemson starting left guard
Elyjah Thurmon
was listed as questionable after an injury sustained last week at Cal.
Thurmon has played in all four games to this point, logging 192 snaps and grading a 56.3 on PFF. He was limited to 17 snaps at Cal.
Reserve defensive end
Armon Mason
was listed as questionable as well. DT
Devarrick Woods
and Nickel
Corian Gipson
, who were ruled out last week at Cal, did not appear on the report.
Clemson head coach
Dabo Swinney
gave a positive review of team health just about an hour to the report release.
"I think they've done a good job and gotten a little bit better each and every day, so we're trending in a good direction," Swinney said.
Xavier Lucas, the No. 1-rated CB in the last transfer portal class, is also listed as questionable. He last played in the Stanford opener, getting in on two pass breakups.
Lucas was ruled out in the last ACC availability report for Wake Forest before last week’s game with Central Michigan.
Miami reserve running back CharMar Brown (13 carries for 97 yards, TD) and wide receiver Joshua Moore (seven catches for 107 yards, TD) were among recent season-ending injuries.
