GAME NOTES: Sam Houston •
Middle Tennessee
Series vs the Blue Raiders: Series tied 2-2
HUNTSVILLE –
Sam Houston closes the home portion of its 2026 season on Saturday when it hosts Middle Tennessee at Bowers Stadium.
Kickoff for Saturday's game is set for 1:00 p.m. CT and will air on ESPN+. Jason Metko and John Harris will have the call from Bowers Stadium.
The game can also be heard on the Bearkat Sports Network with Jason Barfield and Brian Adams on the call. The BSN broadcast can be heard online on GoBearkats.com, the Bearkat Sports app and locally on KSHU 90.5 FM.
KATS CLOSE HOME SLATE SATURDAY
- Sam Houston plays its final home game of 2026 under co-interim head coaches
Eric Raisbeck
and
Freddie Aughtry-Lindsay
, who assumed their roles on Oct. 28 following the dismissal of
Phil Longo
. The Kats wrap up the season next Saturday at FIU
- The Kats are in search of their first CUSA win of 2026 and their first win over an FBS opponent at Bowers Stadium since defeating Liberty, 20-18, in the finale of the 2024 regular season
- Sam Houston is one of seven FBS programs to make a head coaching change during the 2026 season, along with Arkansas State, Baylor, Florida State, Maryland, NC State and UTEP. Arkansas State and UTEP are the most recent additions to the list, doing so this week
- November has been Sam Houston's most successful regular-season month since moving to FBS in 2023, with the Kats posting a 7-6 record in the month compared to 8-25 from August through October. The Kats went 3-1 in November in 2023, 2-1 in 2024 and 2-2 in 2025, and the month accounted for all of the program's victories in both 2023 and 2025
SAM HOUSTON vs THE BLUE RAIDERS
- Saturday marks the fifth all-time meeting between Sam Houston and Middle Tennessee, with the series tied 2-2. The Kats won the only previous meeting in Huntsville, taking a 23-20 victory in the 2023 season finale
- The programs first met in the 1956 Refrigerator Bowl in Evansville, Ind., where Sam Houston won 27-13 to complete a perfect 10-0 season
- The teams' next meeting came in the opening round of the 1991 NCAA Division I-AA playoffs, with Middle Tennessee claiming a 20-19 overtime victory in Murfreesboro. The Blue Raiders have won both meetings on their home field
- Middle Tennessee enters the game 5-5 overall and 3-3 in CUSA under third-year head coach Derek Mason. The Blue Raiders are coming off a 28-23 loss at Liberty and are led by quarterback Nicholas Vattiato, who has thrown for 2,104 yards and 17 touchdowns, and running back Jekail Middlebrooks, who has rushed for 574 yards and seven touchdowns
THE LAST TIME
- Middle Tennessee claimed a 31-17 victory in the teams' last meeting on Nov. 22, 2025, in Murfreesboro, Tenn.
- Sam Houston scored on its first two possessions to build a 10-0 lead, but the Blue Raiders answered with 31 consecutive points to take control
-
Landyn Locke
completed 25 of 40 passes for 281 yards and two touchdowns, connecting with
Chris Reed
on scoring passes of 54 and 4 yards. Reed finished with four catches for 67 yards, while
Grady O'Neill
led the Kats with eight receptions for 89 yards
- Roman Gagliano threw for 268 yards and a touchdown and added 67 rushing yards and two scores for the Blue Raiders, who finished with 460 yards of total offense to Sam Houston's 360
PREGAME NOTES
- The Bearkat defense enters Saturday off its best outing of the season, limiting Kennesaw State to 271 yards of total offense, including just 90 on the ground, the fewest rushing yards Sam Houston has allowed in a CUSA game this season. The Kats recorded six tackles for loss and two sacks, with
Brian Bates Jr.
and
Tyler Bailey
sharing the team lead with six tackles apiece
- Sam Houston is allowing 138.0 fewer yards per game than it did in 2025, lowering its total defense average from 483.5 to 345.5 yards per game. The Kats have held two of their last five opponents below 300 yards of total offense
- Sam Houston ranks seventh nationally in passing defense, allowing just 163.4 yards per game, and eighth nationally in third-down defense, holding opponents to a 30.5 percent conversion rate. The Kats lead CUSA in passing defense
-
Chris Reed
has caught a touchdown pass in consecutive games and leads Sam Houston with 485 receiving yards and five touchdown receptions, matching his team-leading total from 2025. He has combined for 11 catches, 128 yards and two scores over the past two games
-
Landyn Locke
moved into 19th place on Sam Houston's career passing list against Kennesaw State, bringing his career total to 2,615 yards. Locke has thrown 14 touchdown passes this season, including last week's 10-yard scoring connection with Reed
