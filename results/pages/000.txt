The Tigers have won nine of the last ten meetings in the series and hold a four-game winning streak in Doak Campbell Stadium entering Halloween.
With the contest approaching swiftly, the Seminoles are keeping their eye on a few injuries.
Florida State and Clemson released their initial availability reports on Wednesday night. At that time, the two teams ruled out a combined 16 players.
On Thursday, the Seminoles and Tigers provided their second availability report.

There were a few changes to take note of.

Florida State downgraded senior safety K.J. Kirkland from questionable to doubtful. The Seminoles will likely have to rely on their depth in the back end once again, with senior cornerback Quindarrius Jones already ruled out.
On the positive side, fifth-year linebackers Mikai Gbayor and Omar Graham Jr., along with junior quarterback Malachi Marshall are no longer on the report. That basically means they were upgraded from probable to available.
Graham Jr. exited the loss to Miami in the second half with an apparent neck injury/concussion. It's good to see him back in the mix.
