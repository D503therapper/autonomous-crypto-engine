With less than 24 hours until kickoff, South Carolina and Kentucky have released the Friday night update to their new mandatory player availability report via the SEC.
A final availability report will be put out 90 minutes before kickoff, 2 p.m. in this case.
[Join GamecockCentral for in-depth Gamecock coverage and The Insiders Forum]
The Gamecocks’ Friday night entry listed offensive lineman Jakai Moore and defensive back Emory Floyd as out. Defensive back David Spaulding is questionable while EDGEs Gilber Edmond and Bryan Thomas Jr. and linebacker Bangally Kamara are listed as probable.
Shane Beamer last spoke about the injuries Thursday night during his call-in show.
“I think we’re in pretty good shape with those guys,” Beamer said Thursday. “Jakai Moore obviously is out and will be for a while. The rest of those guys all practiced (Thursday) and we feel good about and we’ll see how they continue to progress. Emory Floyd is listed as doubtful and there hasn’t been much change to that, but those other guys, we feel good about, and obviously we’ve got a little bit less than 48 hours until we have to play.”

Thomas, Floyd, and Spaulding did not play in the season opener, while Kamara played sparingly.
Moore, who was set to be the Gamecocks’ “swing man” due to his ability to play tackle and guard, is going to be out for a while. That’s also the case for reserve offensive lineman AJ Parks, though he wasn’t listed on the report.
Redshirt freshman safety Zahbari Sandy is out for the season after suffering a knee injury during spring practice.
Kentucky’s projected starting running back Chip Trayanum is listed as out for the game.

The
SEC recently approved a player availability injury report system
for football, men’s and women’s basketball and baseball.
SEC school personnel must submit availability reports three days before each football game, with daily updates leading to a final report 90 minutes before game time. For basketball and baseball, reports are filed the night before, with an update on game day.
According to the SEC, penalties for inaccurate or late reports will range from $25,000 to $100,000 for football and anywhere from $15,000 to $25,000 for basketball and baseball.
When schools submit injury reports, players will be designated as available, probable, doubtful, and questionable before game day. On game day, designations will be available, game-time decision or out. The model follows injury reports similar to the NFL.
