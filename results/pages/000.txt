The ACC released its initial availability report for Saturday's game, listing seven

Starting nickelback

"We'll see, but he's practicing," Swinney said. "So, we'll find out today. He practiced last night, so we'll see."
Woods also missed the Cal game after suffering an injury against North Carolina. Swinney described the defensive tackle as "day to day" Tuesday, but his absence from the report is another positive development for a Clemson defensive line already dealing with several long-term injuries.
Vizzina's availability gives Clemson additional security behind freshman starting quarterback Tait Reynolds. The redshirt junior quarterback has been recovering from a left elbow injury suffered against Georgia Southern and did not play in Clemson's past two games.

Starting left guard Elyjah Thurmon and defensive end Armon Mason are Clemson's two questionable players. Thurmon left the Cal game after 17 snaps and did not return. Gavin Blanchard replaced him at left guard and played the final 57 offensive snaps.

Tight end Luka Gilbert

Mark Fletcher Jr.

Ahmad Moten Sr.

Clemson
