The Latest Mississippi State Injury Report Raises New Questions Before Texas
Two defensive starters face uncertain availability and a starting lineman is out ahead of Mississippi State’s Texas visit.
Marvin Uzor
|
Share on Facebook
Mississippi State Bulldogs
Texas Longhorns
Mississippi State needed good news Friday night, and the final full
SEC availability report
before the Texas game delivered the opposite.
Safety Isaac Smith and linebacker Tyler Lockhart
were both downgraded to doubtful, leaving a defense that has lost several starters this season even thinner heading into Austin.
Isaac Smith and Tyler Lockhart trend toward sitting
Smith’s status hurts the most. The senior from Fulton, Mississippi, led the SEC with 127 tackles in 2024, ranked seventh nationally, and posted 64 more last season while playing a hybrid safety and linebacker role. However, he did not feature against Oklahoma, and Mississippi State has not disclosed his injury.
Oct 3, 2026; Starkville, Mississippi, USA; Mississippi State Bulldogs linebacker Tyler Lockhart (11) reacts during the second quarter against the Alabama Crimson Tide at Davis Wade Stadium at Scott Field. Mandatory Credit: Petre Thomas-Imagn Images | IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect
Lockhart was questionable on Thursday before Friday’s downgrade. The sophomore from Kilmichael earned Freshman All-SEC honors from the league’s coaches in 2025 and has become a key piece of the linebacker rotation.
Worse yet, both players sit at positions where depth was a concern long before this week. Fatt Forrest and Zakari Tillman will likely shoulder more of the load at linebacker, while names like Duke Johnson and five-star freshman Bralan Womack could see a bigger share of snaps at safety.
DJ Chester’s absence reshuffles the line
The offense took its own hit Thursday when left guard DJ Chester was ruled out. He had started all seven games this season and allowed five quarterback pressures and one sack, according to Pro Football Focus, which made him one of the steadier pieces up front.
His absence leaves left tackle Miles McVay, center Cannon Boone, right guard LJ Pruitt and right tackle Jakeheem Shumpert-Perkins working next to a new partner. For a quarterback who will be making his first or second start of the season, a reshuffled line is the last thing anyone wanted.
Mississippi State’s full injury picture
Oct 24, 2026; Starkville, Mississippi, USA; Mississippi State Bulldogs head coach Jeff Lebby talks with defensive lineman Gus Cordova (40) prior to the game against the Oklahoma Sooners at Davis Wade Stadium at Scott Field. Mandatory Credit: Petre Thomas-Imagn Images | IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect
Here is where the Bulldogs stand heading into Saturday night:
QB Kamario Taylor (season-ending foot surgery) - Out
LB Jalen Smith - Out
OL DJ Chester - Out
DL Diesel Moye - Out
DL Trevion Williams - Out
WR Ayden Williams - Out
S Isaac Smith - Doubtful
LB Tyler Lockhart - Doubtful
Mississippi State coach Jeff Lebby still has not named his starting quarterback
between Billy Puckett and AJ Swann
, and that decision will not come until game night.
Texas will play without Colin Simmons
On the bright side, the Longhorns are pretty much banged up too. Edge rusher Colin Simmons was ruled out Friday with an ankle injury after moving from questionable to doubtful to out over the course of the week.
Simmons leads the FBS with 10.5 sacks and 16.5 tackles for loss, so his absence is a massive break for a Mississippi State line missing its starting left guard. Texas will also be without left tackle Trevor Goosby and edge rusher Richard Wesley.
Oct 10, 2026; Dallas, Texas, USA; Texas Longhorns edge Colin Simmons (1) celebrates during the game between the Sooners and the Longhorns at Cotton Bowl. Mandatory Credit: Jerome Miron-Imagn Images | IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect
Across the field, the news is better for the Texas offense. Receiver Cam Coleman and running back Hollywood Smothers were removed from the report on Thursday, and receiver Kaliq Lockett was listed as probable.
For a Bulldogs defense missing so many familiar faces, Saturday night will lean heavily on players who have waited most of the season for their turn.
