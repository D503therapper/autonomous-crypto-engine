Sophomore receiver Brady Blackburn has been Craig’s top target this year with 432 yards on 22 catches and two touchdowns. Right there with him, though, is tight end Seamus Gilmartin with 279 yards and a score. This duo will look to take some of the pressure off Craig, especially if the rushing attack has trouble getting going. That, though, has not been a problem for Harvard yet this year.
Xavier Bascon and D.J. Jordan have been a dynamite one-two punch for the Crimson this season, racking up a combined 603 yards and eight scores. Bascon is currently the among the top of all Ivy League ball carriers with 65 total touches this year and an average of five yards per run. Dartmouth will need to have an answer for both of these two or it will be a long day.
That answer, they hope, will come in the form of senior linebacker Zyion Freer-Brown. Freer-Brown has been excellent over the middle for the Big Green this year, registering 50 tackles, two sacks and four TFLs. He and Nico Schwikal will be the main enforcers on the defense for Dartmouth and will need to have a productive day against Harvard’s smashing ground game.
When Craig opts to pass, the Big Green will look to guys like Sean Williams to make plays. Williams, a senior defensive back, is tied at the top of the conference with three interceptions this year and also has two defended passes. He will be one of the better secondary players on the field this Saturday and will play a big role in keeping Blackburn in check. If that doesn’t happen, it’s tough to see Dartmouth walking out with a win.
Prediction
No team has even been able to keep it close with Harvard this year but Dartmouth is built differently than most of the teams the Crimson have faced up to this point. That stout offensive line will open the doors for the Big Green to make some things happen offensively that will allow them to stay in this game. Betting against Craig and this Harvard bunch in their own building, though, is ill advised. Craig will put on that Superman “S” in the fourth quarter and carry his group in their first close contest of the year. Harvard prevails in a tight one to get one, big step closer to the Ivy League championship.
