SEC football fans now have more clarity on the status of their respective rosters ahead of Saturdays in the fall. The league instituted a new policy in 2024 that requires schools to submit
official availability reports
.
Each Wednesday of SEC contests, both schools will share an availability report. It will be updated nightly, with one final ruling an hour before kickoff on Saturdays. Players must be listed as out (0% chance to play), doubtful (25% chance to play), questionable (50% chance to play), or probable (75% chance to play).
The second report for the No. 15 Kentucky vs. Oklahoma game dropped on Thursday night. The ‘Cats could still be without RB1 on Saturday night while the Sooners could be without a handful of starters.

There was only one change for Kentucky in Thursday’s update: South Alabama defensive line transfer Tyler Thomas has been downgraded from doubtful to out after not playing a snap last week against LSU.
Kentucky will be without Grant Grayton, Antwan Smith, Jaden Smith, and Tavion Wallace again on Saturday. Alex Afari Jr. is listed as questionable as he continues his eligibility fight in court. CJ Baxter pops up on the availability report for the first time this season. It looks like Kalen Edwards could return after a one-game absence. Nic Anderson is trending towards playing after being pulled from last week’s game.

The Sooners saw two changes in Thursday’s report. WR Parker Livingston and starting OL Eddy Pierre-Louis were downgraded from questionable to doubtful. Heath Ozaeta, who remains questionable, replaced Pierre-Louis in the lineup last week.
Oklahoma will not have starting wide receiver Trell Harris once again. The Sooners are dealing with multiple injuries at wideout, running back, and offensive line. This team could be without multiple starters on Saturday depending on how the questionable situations go. Eli Bowen and Michael Fasusi are both starters.
Both teams are dealing with some significant injury situations this week.
