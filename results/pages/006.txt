When Rice has the ball
Rice uses a
triple-option attack
similar to Army but out of a shotgun set, running the ball on 75% of plays and ranking eighth in the country in rushing at 268.9 yards per game. UTSA's defense has struggled against the run this season, allowing 213.3 yards per game to rank 133rd in the FBS. The Roadrunners believe those struggles are tied to individual breakdowns rather than structural issues, but the ability to remain sound on assignments will be especially tested against the Owls.
