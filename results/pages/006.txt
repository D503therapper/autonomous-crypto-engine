New Mexico linebacker Jaxton Eck out for 'a while' after suffering shoulder injury
Multiple Lobos listed as out or questionable for Saturday's game against Hawaii

New Mexico linebacker Jaxton Eck will be out for “a while” after breaking a bone in his shoulder, head coach Jason Eck told the Journal on Thursday.
The 6-foot-1, 228-pound senior did not practice this week and was observed with his arm in a sling before he was ruled out for Saturday’s game at Hawaii. Jason Eck said the preseason Mountain West Defensive Player of the Year’s bone break is in the
same shoulder he hurt in the Rate Bowl
on Dec. 27.
Eck added that he does not believe Jaxton’s injury is season-ending, but expects him to miss an extended period of time. His designation was first released via an initial availability report from the Mountain West on Thursday, one that saw multiple key Lobos listed as out or questionable ahead of Saturday’s game.

Only one Lobo was newly ruled out for the rest of the season on the initial report: backup linebacker Nick Wheeler, who Eck said suffered a torn ACL. Without Jaxton and Wheeler, UNM is expected to rely on linebackers Mercury Swaim and Clay Martineau for the near future.
Swaim (a team-high 25 total tackles) has started every game for UNM this season while Martineau (14 total tackles) has seen the field as a reserve linebacker and edge rusher in special packages.
Starting cornerback Frankie Edwards III (9 total tackles) has also been ruled out for Saturday’s game, per the league’s availability report. Eck said the 5-10, 178-pound junior suffered a concussion, and expects him to return as soon as next week.
Wide receiver Massiah Mingo (ankle) and running back Kiefer Sibley (shoulder) are questionable for Saturday’s game, per the availability report. A 5-9, 170-pound freshman, Mingo has caught five passes for 56 yards; Sibley has rushed for 205 yards, the second-best mark on the team.
Eck said he is hopeful both Mingo and Sibley will be able to play Saturday.
A second availability report will be released in the hours before Saturday’s game. All Mountain West teams are required to submit availability reports with designations, but without injury specifics, prior to each league contest.
