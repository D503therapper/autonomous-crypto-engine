By the Numbers
Kickoff is at 6:30 p.m. CT on ESPN2.
JMU is 7-1 overall and 4-1 in the Sun Belt; Troy is 3-4 and 1-2.
Yes, But
JMU’s strong home form makes it the clear challenge, but the Dukes are coming off an overtime loss and will be without their leading rusher. Troy’s road offense has struggled, yet its defense has been more reliable away from home, ranking near the top of the Sun Belt in yards and points allowed.
State of Play
Troy’s quarterback choice may depend on Crowder’s availability; Kilcrease has scored or thrown a touchdown in each of the past 2 games.
Troy will need its pass rush and turnover-forcing defense to disrupt JMU’s high-powered home offense.
What's Next
Thursday’s matchup will test whether Troy can turn its defensive strength into its first road win, while JMU looks to protect its home streak despite Pettaway’s absence.
