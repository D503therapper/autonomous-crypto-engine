The first availability report for this weekend’s game between Stanford and Wake Forest has been posted.
The Deacons might be down a couple of key players from their two-deep on defense.
Defensive end
Tyler Walton
is listed as doubtful and linebacker
Deuce Dyce
is questionable to play against the Cardinal.
Neither Walton nor Dyce has started a game for the Deacons this season. But both of them are in the top 20 of Wake’s defense for snaps taken (Pro Football Focus).
The most notable part of this for Wake Forest is probably the players who don’t appear. Quarterback
Gio Lopez
draw “bad blood” from his hip
. Left guard
Clinton Richard
only played 11 snaps before exiting last week’s game against Louisville with an injury, but he does not appear on the availability report.
Wide receiver
Ny Carr
and cornerback
Devin Cook
are the only two Deacons ruled out. Freshman receiver
Elijah Otieno
is also listed as doubtful.
On the Stanford side of things …
Stanford has gotten healthier on its offensive line over the last couple of weeks.
The Cardinal’s secondary is another story.
Stanford has already ruled out starting cornerback
Brandon Nicholson
. He’s started every game this season and is fourth on its defense for snaps played (222). He’s also been good, with a
PFF
grade of 72.6 in those snaps.
Stanford has six defensive backs listed as out for Saturday’s game, but Nicholson is the only who has played this season.
The Cardinal listed two players as questionable — linebacker Jahsiah Galvan and defensive lineman Jacobi Murrray. Galvan started and played 58 snaps against Georgia Tech last weekend, with PFF crediting him with 10 tackles and five run-stops.
