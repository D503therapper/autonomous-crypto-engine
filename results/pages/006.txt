SPRINGFIELD — With the change to mid-week games for Missouri State (1-3) football, head coach Casey Woods will be speaking to the media on Fridays.
He gave an injury update on his team on Friday, Oct. 2.

Linebackers Heiko Malone and Dylan Dixon, along with defensive lineman Malachi Madison, have suffered season-ending injuries. Those injuries came early in the season, though.
