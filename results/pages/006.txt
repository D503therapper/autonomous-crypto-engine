The Crimson Tide is coming off a bye week, as numerous injured players had extra time to heal ahead of the LSU game. Alabama was without wide receiver Ryan Coleman-Williams, cornerback Zabien Brown, running back EJ Crowell, center Racin Delgatty and nose tackle Terrance Green during the home win over Texas A&M on Oct. 24. All of them being questionable shows some progress, as head coach Kalen DeBoer provided an update on the group during Monday's press conference.
"A lot of these guys from Zabien Brown to Ryan Coleman-Williams, EJ, Racin, Terrance Green in particular, those guys are all working onto the football field,"
DeBoer said
. "They're not all in the exact same spot, but they're all out there going through workouts. Some are taking team reps, some are taking non-contact reps.
"But I think the progress by all of them is headed in a good direction. We'll see. I think a lot of it has to do with how their bodies respond after they're out there being pushed a little bit more."
Details for Recently Injured Alabama Players:
Ryan Coleman-Williams: knee – missed last two games
Zabien Brown: lower leg – missed Texas A&M after first-half injury vs. Tennessee
EJ Crowell: lower leg – missed Texas A&M after first-half injury vs. Tennessee
Racin Delgatty: lower leg – missed last two games
Terrance Green: reaggravated undisclosed injury – missed last two games
Will Sanders: lower-extremity – sustained against Texas A&M and is out for a couple of weeks
DeBoer provided a bit more clarity on Wednesday night's
Hey Coach
radio show.
"Guys are making good progress," DeBoer said. "I think that's what a lot of it was said on Monday. A lot of guys out there going through practice. Some of it is just how are they responding to the physicality of what the practice gives, because it's going to be another level when you talk about the contact and what a football game entails. I know a lot of that report will be coming out here, as we have to do with the SEC report."
Quarterback Jett Thomalla and running back AK Dear are the only two new names on the availability report. Thomalla hasn't played yet this season, while Dear has only seen the field in two games.
Edge rusher Desmond Umeozulu hasn't played since the win over Florida State as he's been dealing with turf toe. DeBoer shared on Monday that Umeozulu won't be available until "toward the very back end of the regular season." Freshman (JUCO) defensive back Nick Sherman still hasn't made his Crimson Tide debut, as he sustained an injury during a fall scrimmage.
