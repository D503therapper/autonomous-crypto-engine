Illinois vs. Michigan State predictions
Illinois 31, Michigan State 17:
Both teams are at risk of letting their season get away from them as they cross paths in Week 6. The Illini are winless in the Big Ten and saw quarterback Katin Houser have just a so-so day in the surprise upset dealt to them at home by Purdue in Week 5. The
Boilermakers
snapped a 19-game conference losing streak at Illinois' expense. Now the Illini head to Michigan State with a chance to push the conference foe to a fourth straight loss. Michigan State offense is in a coma, producing one touchdown in the last two games and none in the last six quarters. Leading rusher Cam Edwards went out for the season at the outset of that offensive stretch. Michigan State has two starters on defense questionable and two backup corners out. Spartans opponents so far are a combined 21-5. —
