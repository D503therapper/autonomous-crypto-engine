Safety KJ Bolden, a semifinalist for the Jim Thorpe Aware for nation's top defensive back, is still listed as questionable, but running back Chauncey Bowens was downgraded from questionable to out.
Bolden missed Georgia's last game, a 27-24 loss to Auburn on Oct. 17, with a lower right leg injury.
"He's doing all he can to get back and be able to play," coach Kirby Smart said Wednesday on Atlanta's 680 AM. "I know he wants to really bad, but only time's probably going to be able to tell on that."
Bolden has 22 tackles, one interception and two pass breakups.

"We need that toughness, week in and week out," inside linebacker Raylen Wilson said. "Especially on the perimeter with our DBs. It starts with him mostly. He's battling through his injury he's going through. He's taking it pretty well because we know injury comes with this game."
Wide receiver Isiah Canion is still listed as questionable.
Canion, the Georgia Tech transfer, has missed the last four games with what Smart has called a "lower extremity injury."
Bowens sustained a lower body injury in the win against Vanderbilt and will miss his third straight game..
