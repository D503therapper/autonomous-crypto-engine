Cumbie called the two straight shutouts “pretty remarkable.” But he also said there’s room for improvement.
“We’ve got to do a better job of getting off the field on third downs,” he said.
Offensively, Cumbie is looking for more production from the run game.
That could get a boost with the return of Dylone Brooks. Cumbie said Brooks should be ready to play Saturday. He was injured against Louisiana on Oct. 10 and has missed the last two games.
Cumbie said South Alabama will provide another challenge.
The Jaguars rank third in the conference in total offense at 421.9 yards per game and 10th in defense at 413.6.
South Alabama ranks No. 1 in the conference in rushing yards per game at 214.3. Keenan Phillips and PJ Martin rank third and fourth, respectively, at 73.4 and 67.1.
