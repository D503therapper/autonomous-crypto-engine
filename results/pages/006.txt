TITLE: 2026 Louisiana Football Preview - The Arkansas State Game - Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns
DATES SEEN: ['Oct. 5, 2024', 'Sep 19, 2026', 'Sep 21, 2026', 'Sept 28, 2026', 'September 1, 2001', 'September 28, 2026']

The Louisiana Ragin' Cajuns football team officially opens its Sun Belt Conference slate on Saturday when it hosts Arkansas State in the 82
nd
annual Homecoming game at Our Lady of Lourdes Stadium.
Kickoff is scheduled for 7 p.m. The game will be available on ESPN+ with
Dan McDonald
(pxp) and Steve Peloquin (color) providing commentary. Fans can also listen to the broadcast on the Ragin' Cajuns Radio Network and Varsity Sports Network with
Jay Walker
(pxp), Cody Junot (color) and Dawson Eiserloh (sideline) on the call.
Louisiana (3-1) opens its 26
th
season of Sun Belt Conference play after closing out its non-conference slate last Saturday with a 34-7 win over American Conference member Charlotte. The Ragin' Cajuns forced four turnovers and limited the 49ers to 137 yards of total offense, while pulling away in the second half from a 10-7 lead at the break.
Brent Gordon Jr.
, accounted for three of Louisiana's takeaways with an interception and two fumble recoveries as the Ragin' Cajuns turned in their best defensive performance since 2001.
