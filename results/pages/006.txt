Florida State
will try to take down the No. 3 Miami Hurricanes on Saturday, and they'll have to do so on the road where they've lost 11 straight. An
updated injury report
has been released, and there's one change for FSU.
Running back Sam Singleton Jr., wide receiver Devin Carter, defensive backs Karson Hobbs, Jamari Howard, and Jordan Crutchfield, plus linebackers Blake Nichelson, Izayia Williams, and Daylen Green will miss this game. Also, linebacker Noah LaVallee is out after being listed as questionable.

Defensive lineman Darryll Desir, defensive back Zae Thomas, and offensive lineman Paul Bowling are all probable.
For Miami, the same six players won't play. Wide receiver Joshua Moore, running back CharMar Brown, offensive lineman Max Buchanan, tight end Luka Gilbert, and defensive backs Jamboree Antoine and Romanas "OJ" Frederique Jr. are out. No players are questionable or probable.
Florida State and Miami will play at 7 p.m. ET on ESPN and Disney Plus. The
Hurricanes are massive favorites
and have won the last two against the Seminoles.
