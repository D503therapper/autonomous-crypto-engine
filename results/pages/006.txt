Wyoming Notes
On The Road Again:
Wyoming remains on the road as part of a two-game road swing that sees the Pokes traveling 1,950 miles with trips to Fargo and San Jose. The Pokes have played four road games in the first six games of the season. The last time UW has done that was the 2003 season when Joe Glenn and the Cowboys played four games in the first six games of the season.
JY Gets Another Sack:
Wyoming defensive tackle Jayden Williams recorded his second career sack and the second in as many weeks. He recorded a sack for nine yards on a third down to force a NDSU punt. He also added three tackles in the contest and has six in the last two games and 18 on the season for the Pokes. The 2.5 tackles for loss this season in a career-high in his three season with the Cowboys.
Drake in the Middle:
Wyoming defensive tackle Dante Drake had a strong game for the Pokes at North Dakota State. He recorded five tackles and added 1.5 tackles for loss to tie a career best and the most since he did so against San Diego State in 2023. He has 17 tackles on the season for the Pokes, which is a new single season best. He missed last season with an injury and he and Hayden Williams have combined for 35 tackles in the middle for the Pokes.
Holman Reaches 500 Mark and 1,900 mark:
Wyoming running back Markell Holman added a 12 yards reception for the Pokes against NDSU. He has now recorded 512 yards receiving in his career. He also went over 1,900 career yards in the contest at NDSU, as he sits at 1,901 career yards. He is 99 away from 2,000 in his career.

About San Jose State
The Spartans opened Mountain West play with a 20-16 victory over Hawai’i late Saturday in Honolulu. San Jose State’s defense had two interceptions and Luke Weaver passed for 246 yards and two touchdowns.
Jabari Bates rushed for 74 yards and Carson Clark had 74 yards receiving and a touchdown in SJSU’s balanced offense.
