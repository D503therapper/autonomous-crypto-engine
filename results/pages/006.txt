Injury Report: Arizona State vs. Colorado
by
Ryland Scholes
Nov 23, 2025, 12:45 AM UTC
Facebook
Link
It’s Senior Day for the Buffaloes at Folsom Field, as CU will kick off against Arizona State within the hour. Unfortunately, nearly a dozen Buffs will miss action against the Sun Devils due to injury.
Offensively, star offensive tackle Jordan Seaton will miss action due to a foot injury. Seaton missed action against WVU two weekends ago and still isn’t healthy enough to go against ASU. Seaton, who has established himself as one of the best tackles in the country, is integral to Colorado’s offense, and his absence will play a large role in tonight’s game. Additionally, offensive lineman Larry Johnson III will sit tonight, being Colorado’s second and final offensive player listed on the injury report.
Defensively, CU will be without a minimum of eight players, including cornerbacks DJ McKinney and RJ Johnson. With McKinney, a redshirt sophomore, celebrating Senior Day, there’s a very high likelihood that he has played his final game as a Buff. Safties Ben Finneseth and Carter Stoutmire will also sit tonight.
Up front, the Buffs will be without Amari McNeill, Brandon Davis-Swain and Tawfiq Thomas, all key members of their pass rush rotation. Inside linebacker Mantrez Walker is listed as out, while Jeremiah Brown is listed as a game-time decision.
With kickoff coming up shortly, make sure to follow along with Ralphie Report for all your gameday content!
