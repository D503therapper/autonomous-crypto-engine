Rutgers Football is just a couple of days away from taking on the Indiana Hoosiers in week five of the 2026 college football season. The Big Ten recently announced change to conference football availability reports, as teams will now be required to submit four availabilities reports a week, beginning three days before kickoff. One will also be released at least two hours before the game begins.
As for designations, not much will change there. Certain terminology is allowed to be used during the week before making the final gameday decision. From there, everyone looking at the reports knows who is and who is not expected to be on the field.
“Beginning this year, schools will submit four availability reports per week, beginning three days before a contest and concluding with a gameday report,”
the announcement read
. “Institutions will submit reports by 8pm ET/7pm CT/5pm PT three days (e.g., on Wednesday for a Saturday game), two days, and one day before scheduled kickoff. A final gameday submission will be due two hours before kickoff.
