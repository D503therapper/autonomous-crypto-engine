Tennessee football is preparing for a fight against Auburn this Saturday
The No.17 Volunteers face the unranked Auburn Tigers at 3:30 p.m. in Neyland Stadium on Oct. 3. As part of a mandate by the SEC, each team must release an initial injury report ahead of each conference matchup. Both teams have to submit an injury report. With this, teams are required to provide a status update on Wednesday before each league game. Each designation will come with a tag: available, probable, questionable, doubtful or out on the reports. On game day, the tags change to available, game-time decision or out.
A report must be submitted on Thursday and Friday, with the final notice coming 90 minutes before kickoff.
Here is a look at the injury front before Tennessee takes on Auburn.
Left guard Wendell Moe Jr. — OUT
Wendell Moe Jr. recently suffered a lower-leg injury. This occurred during last Saturday’s 20-17 loss to Texas. The starting left Guard and first-team All-SEC selection was helped off the field on the first offensive drive of the game after a player rolled over his leg. Moe Jr. never returned to the game.
Moe is a critical asset to the offensive scheme and pass protection for starting quarterback Faizon Brandon. Tennessee’s pass protection lacked against Texas, leading to 10 sacks on Brandon.
Quarterback Faizon Brandon — Active
Faizon suffered a scare early in the second quarter against the Longhorns last Saturday. After running the ball, Brandon lay down on his back awkwardly and stayed on the ground clutching his upper body near his shoulder. He was then taken to the medical tent to be evaluated. Brandon missed one full offensive drive before returning for the rest of the game.
Brandon was not made available to the media postgame as he was receiving treatment, but all signs point to full availability against Auburn.
Defensive back Qua Moss — Active
Qua Moss apparently suffered a head injury in the fourth quarter against Texas. While coming down to make a tackle against Arch Manning, Moss appeared to take a knee directly to the helmet as he hit the ground. He was able to walk off the field on his own and went straight to the medical tent to be evaluated.
Moss has been a valuable asset to the Vols and was important to the team before leaving the field. Moss recorded five tackles and a 57-yard punt return for a touchdown.
