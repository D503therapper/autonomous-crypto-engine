Colorado
football team is seeking its first Big 12 victory of the season when Texas Tech visits Boulder on Saturday.
CU might have to pursue that victory without a few key players, particularly on defense.
On Wednesday night, the first player availability report of the week was posted. CU (2-2, 0-1 Big 12) will host No. 12 Texas Tech (4-0, 1-0) on Saturday at Folsom Field (5:30 p.m. MT, Fox).
Key
Colorado
football players ‘doubtful’
Three players are listed as out, and it’s no surprise on any of them: receiver Danny Scudero, offensive lineman Demetrius Hunter and cornerback Preston Ashley.
Scudero has missed the last two games and head coach Deion Sanders said Tuesday that the receiver would likely not return until after the bye week, which for CU is next week. Hunter had surgery on Wednesday for a foot injury. Ashley has been out for a couple of weeks, as well.
Starting cornerback Jason Stokes Jr. and backup defensive backs Makari Vickers and Braylon Edwards are all listed as doubtful. Defensive end Lamont Lester Jr., who leads the Big 12 in sacks, and tight ends Zach Atkins and Charlie Williams also are listed as doubtful.
Receiver DeAndre Moore Jr. is listed as questionable.
