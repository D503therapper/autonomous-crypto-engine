COLUMBUS — Ohio State is set to travel for a massive top-15 showdown at Iowa on Saturday afternoon. Both teams enter this weekend with plenty of confidence, especially Iowa. The Hawkeyes are fresh off a big win on the road over Michigan.
It seems like the Buckeyes will be getting defensive end Zion Grady back from injury. His name didn’t appear on the report, clearing him for a potential return for the first time since the loss to Texas. Starting offensive lineman Phillip Daniels, meanwhile, is questionable.
Big Ten rules state that both programs must reveal an availability report beginning Wednesday night. It was released by both programs at 8 p.m.
