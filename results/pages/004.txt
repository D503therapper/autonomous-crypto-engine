COLUMBUS, Ohio (WSYX) —
Ohio State and Oregon head into Saturday's Big Ten matchup far from healthy.
In the preseason, this Week 10 showdown was supposed be the biggest of the weekend, and have national championship implications for both teams.
Now, OSU (6-2, 4-1 Big Ten) is just trying to stay alive in the conference with its starting quarterback likely out for the rest of the season. And the Ducks (5-3, 3-2), also without their starting QB, have a chance to spoil the Buckeyes' postseason aspirations.
Below are the injuries for Oregon:
RB Jordon Davison, out
QB Dante Moore, out
WR Messiah Hampton, out
TE Andrew Olesh, out
DB Aaron Flowers, out
ILB Brayden Platt, out
DL Matthew Johnson, out
OL Trent Ferguson, out
OL Dave Iuli, out
WR Jeremiah McClellan, probable
Below are the injuries for Ohio State:
QB Julian Sayin, out
RB Ja’Kobi Jackson, out
OL Jaxon Powell, out
TE Maxence LeBlanc, out
WR Brandon Inniss, questionable
WR Brock Boyd, questionable
LB Eli Lee, questionable
OL Ian Moore, questionable
Oregon and Ohio State kick off Saturday at 3:30 p.m. on CBS. DraftKings lists OSU as a 7.5-point favorite as of this writing Thursday.
