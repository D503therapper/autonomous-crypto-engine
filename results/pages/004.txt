CHENEY, Wash. —
Eastern Washington's fall sports teams are in the home stretch, and its basketball teams are ready for week two of their seasons. Men's basketball faces a Pac-12 foe tonight, but gets to play at Reese Court on Friday. Football travels to play Utah Tech on Saturday for the team's final road trip of the 2026 season. Women's basketball plays two games at home this week. Volleyball closes out its '26 home schedule with matches against the Grizzlies and Bobcats. Cross country is in Sacramento for the NCAA Regionals on Friday.
Football – at Utah Tech (Saturday, Nov. 14 at 1 p.m. Pacific Time)
Eastern Washington will complete its road schedule this week as they fly to St. George, Utah, for a matchup against Utah Tech. Cross Country – at NCAA West Regional (Friday, Nov. 13 at 9:00 AM)
Eastern Washington cross country heads to Sacramento, Calif., this week to compete at the NCAA West Regional on Friday, Nov. 13. The women's race is scheduled to begin at 9 a.m., followed by the men's race at 10 a.m. The Eagles will compete against some of the top programs in the West Region as they look to close out their postseason competition with strong performances.
Eastern is coming off the Big Sky Conference Championships in Flagstaff, Ariz., where the women finished eighth with 228 points and the men placed ninth with 254 points.
