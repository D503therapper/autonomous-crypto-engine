Air Force (6-2, 3-2 Mountain West) became bowl-eligible with a 35–26 win over UConn, and all it took was a fullback going back to quarterback and having the game of his life. With Evan Medders injured, senior Kemper Hodges carried 35 times for 200 yards and four touchdowns, including a 62-yarder. It was his first career 100-yard game. He skipped right over the part where you ease into a milestone.
The Falcons held the ball for 41:54. UConn had it for 3:35
in the entire second half
. That 11:37 drive covered 75 yards in 19 plays, and three of Hodges’ 11 first-down runs came on fourth down. Air Force has converted 20 of 24 fourth downs this season, best in the country. Army is second at 82.4%.
