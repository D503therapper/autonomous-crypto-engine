COLUMBUS, Ohio — Linebacker Christian Alliegro and defensive back Jay Timmons, a pair of starters for
Ohio State
football, were listed as questionable on Thursday's injury report — the second report of the week ahead of
Saturday's game against Maryland
.
Alliegro has appeared in every game for Ohio State this season, while Timmons hasn't played since getting hurt in the second half of a Sept. 26 win vs. Illinois.

Safety Terry Moore was also deemed questionable Thursday after not appearing on
Wednesday's report
. Moore, a reserve, is an option to see increased playing time with Leroy Roker listed as out.

Receivers Brandon Inniss and Devin McCuin, offensive tackle Phillip Daniels and defensive end Khary Wilder remain questionable, as they were on the first report.
Ohio State hasn't faced Maryland since the 2023 season. The Buckeyes have won all nine all-time matchups with the Terrapins, scoring more than 35 points in each meeting.
