The
Texas Tech football team
may have to wait another week to get a speedy receiver involved with the offense.
Malcolm Simmons missed last week's win over Arizona after Joey McGuire said the Auburn transfer was in line to become a starter at outside receiver. Simmons is now listed as doubtful on the first availability report for the Nov. 7 game against West Virginia.
Freshman Chase Campbell stepped in for Simmons last week on some of Tech's speed plays for receivers and could be in line to do so again.
Texas Tech will also be without Jalen Jones, who has missed the last few weeks with an injury. Starting receiver
Kenny Johnson
is also on the initial report, listed as questionable.
