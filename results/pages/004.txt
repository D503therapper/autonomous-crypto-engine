Troy vs James Madison Quick Picks and Prediction
Dukes Bounce-Back Spot After First Loss
James Madison suffered its first loss of the season on October 22, falling 34-31 in overtime at Appalachian State. The result stung, but the Dukes actually outgained the Mountaineers 423-366, suggesting the performance was better than the final score. Before that setback, James Madison had won seven consecutive games and scored at least 27 points in seven straight outings.
The Dukes boast one of the nation’s most productive offenses, averaging 41.4 points and approximately 456 yards per game. Quarterback Camden Coleman has been efficient, throwing for 1,567 yards with 14 touchdowns and only two interceptions.
Running back George Pettaway leads the ground game with 824 yards and eight touchdowns, but he suffered a lower-body injury against Appalachian State. His status is critical for an offense that averages more than 200 rushing yards per game. Defensively, James Madison has been just as impressive, allowing only 18.3 points per contest.
TROY Passing Game vs JMU Defense
Troy’s offense has been a problem all season, averaging just 22.9 points per game. Quarterback Goose Crowder has thrown for 1,162 yards with nine touchdowns and five interceptions, but he’s dealing with a recent shoulder injury that needs to be monitored.
Running back Gerald Griffin leads the Trojans with 398 rushing yards, and he’ll be running into a James Madison defense that allows approximately 110 rushing yards per game.
The passing game has its own concerns. Receiver TK Keyes, who has 282 receiving yards and three touchdowns, missed Troy’s previous game, which could further limit an already inconsistent aerial attack. If Crowder is less than 100 percent and Keyes remains sidelined, it’s hard to see Troy sustaining drives against this Dukes defense.
James Madison’s superior offensive balance, defensive efficiency, and home-field advantage should create plenty of separation. The Dukes have the firepower to build a commanding lead, while Troy’s offensive limitations should keep the overall scoring in check.
