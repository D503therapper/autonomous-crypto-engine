COLUMBUS, Ohio —
Ohio State
football listed receivers Brandon Inniss and Devin McCuin as questionable on its first injury report ahead of Saturday's matchup with Maryland.
Offensive tackle Phillip Daniels and defensive end Khary Wilder were among the other players listed as questionable. Safety Leroy Roker was the only notable player declared out.
