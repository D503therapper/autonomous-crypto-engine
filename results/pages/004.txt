HBCU Chris-tal ball Week 8: The MEAC championship chase begins
by
Chris Stevens
October 22, 2026
0
79
VIEWS
The weather is getting cooler, but the 2026 HBCU football season continues to heat up with MEAC conference play starting and more SWAC/CIAA/SIAC battles added into the mix.
Week 8’s games could provide separation in a few races while others are just getting started.
Before we predict Week 8’s matchups, let’s take a quick look back at an interesting Week 7 for your friendly neighborhood HBCU predictions analyst.
Week 7 Record: 16-9
2026 overall record: 159-71
Best Pick: Prairie View 30, Alcorn State 27 (Panthers won 41-38. High scoring game down to the last possession as expected.)
Worst Pick: Grambling 30, Alabama A&M 28 (Bulldogs won convincingly 61-31.
Grambling’s SWAC West title hopes only go as far as their defense does and well…
)
Game of the Week: North Carolina Central (2-5, 0-0 MEAC) at Delaware State (4-3, 0-0 MEAC), Friday Oct. 23, 5 pm EST, ESPN2
The DeSean Jackson-led Hornets announced themselves by spoiling Central’s homecoming a season ago and now DSU comes into this game a favorite against a young Eagles team looking to establish some consistent play.
NCCU’s passing game averages just 160 yards per game, so they’ll have to make the most of their chances against a vulnerable Del State secondary.
The Hornets meanwhile are looking to regain their nearly 300 rush yards per game form of 2025 and with Central allowing 154 yards per game on the ground, this could be what they need to get right.
The winner gets a leg up in the MEAC title race. The loser might have to wait until next year.
Prediction: Delaware State 23, North Carolina Central 20
Jackson State (6-1, 3-1 SWAC) at Bethune-Cookman (4-3, 2-1 SWAC), Saturday Oct. 24, 3 p.m. EST, SWAC TV
The Tigers rebounded nicely from their Grambling loss with a convincing homecoming win over FAMU, now they take a trip to the Florida Panhandle for a fight with an ever-improving Bethune-Cookman team looking to shake up the SWAC East standings.
The Wildcats have playmakers on both sides of the ball, but JSU is playing the best ball of anyone in HBCU football right now. Don’t expect that to change this weekend.
Prediction: Jackson State 31, Bethune-Cookman 21
Benedict (6-1, 4-1 SIAC) at Albany State (3-4, 3-2 SIAC), Saturday Oct. 24, ESPN Plus
A rematch of last season’s SIAC title game sees
the Tigers coming in off of a beatdown of Edward Waters
, proving their offense can provide more than enough points for their standout defense to play with.
The defending conference champion Golden Rams are still in the fight, and a win here would make things uncomfortable for Benedict and a couple of teams ahead of them.
Benedict, however, has been on the warpath since their surprising loss at Tuskegee, so with a trip to the SIAC title game in their sights, Albany State’s reign as conference champion could be coming to an end.
Prediction: Benedict 28, Albany State 24
