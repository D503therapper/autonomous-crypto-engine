Baylor Bears
TCU Horned Frogs
During his weekly
media availability
, Head Coach Dave Aranda fielded various questions. Ranging from player health to how the bye week has gone as well as preparation for
the upcoming TCU game
, Coach Aranda had plenty to say.
: "It's good to see everybody here, excited for this one. The message to the team is that we're not done getting all of the blocks we need to get in yet. We're not done pressuring the quarterback for four quarters yet. We're not done becoming who we can become yet. We haven't done anything yet. And so I thought last week was positive in terms of focusing on us.
"And the message last week was, how do we create momentum during a bye? And had the team kind of go through it and identify how they can practice, how they can go through their day-to-day. And I thought we achieved those things. We've got a really big test this week with TCU. I know that we're going to get their best game. They're a talented team. A lot of respect for Sonny and the coaching staff there. So excited about this matchup and excited about getting back to work."
Our Take
: Strong positive is that the first thing Coach Aranda said was that the Bears haven't finished improving. Coach Aranda seemed quite prepared in his opening statement as he has every week, but the immediate emphasis starting out with "We're not done yet" can be taken as a mantra for Baylor's practice this week, or a warning to opposing teams.

Our Take
: A healthy offensive line is a happy offensive line, and a happy offensive line tends to be more willing to throw themselves around to provide run-support. This is actually fantastic news as throughout the season the Bears have have issues with injuries and player placement across the offensive line. Now with an injury free offensive line, it should become much easier for the Bears to establish their running game.
