Offense
The Bulldogs present a fairly balanced attack on offense, but make most of their yards through the air. QB Blake Baker started the first two games before sitting out against Baylor with a fractured hand. It’s unclear whether he’ll be available this Saturday. Backup QB Trey Kukuk got the start against the Bears. Both quarterbacks boast ~60% completion rates, but Baker averages about double the yards per attempt while Kukuk is by far the better runner.
In fact, Kukuk, who has seen action in all three games, now leads the team in rushing with 127 yards on just 20 carries. He’s joined by the running back duo of Andrew Burnette and Jaden Miller, who each have 19 carries. The team’s top two receiving targets — by a lot — are WR Jalen Mickens and TE Eli Finley.
Perhaps this squad’s biggest issue this season has been their ability to convert third downs. Their conversion rate this season sits just over 40%, and perhaps worse, they’re just 2/7 on fourth down. They’re also prone to allowing sacks, having given up 9 on the year. Perhaps some of Army’s hungry defensive linemen can do something with that.
