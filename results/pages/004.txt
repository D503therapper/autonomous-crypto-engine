Cornerback, like quarterback, is a position suddenly thinned by injuries for Air Force.
And cornerback, like quarterback, has the depth to potentially absorb the hit.

Sophomore Ehimen Oyamendan did not play against Navy and is out this week as well, coach Troy Calhoun said. Jordan Elie-Stuart is also likely out, with Calhoun saying he’d “be surprised” if the sophomore came back and played this week.
A source has told The Gazette that both injuries could potentially require long absences for the players.

Oyamendan started the first three games of the season, with Elie-Stuart then replacing him this past week against Navy. The pair have combined for nine tackles and three pass breakups.
ADVERTISEMENT
Huskies quarterback Taron Dickens ranks 22nd in FBS with a .700 completion percentage. Last season he threw 38 touchdowns and two interceptions for Western Carolina.
“Go to work today, man. I’m juiced to go to practice today and watch these guys cover and get better,” Calhoun said Tuesday when asked the deep cornerback position that is growing more and more thin. “I’m fired up to watch our guys go collide on inside run during zone drill. And fired up to watch those guys play this week, too.”
The situation is comparable to quarterback, which remains the big injury-related news item of the week.
Liam Szarka
went down with a knee injury and will miss the remainder of the season, the team announced Monday.
Behind him, the Falcons have sophomore Evan Medders, who was already seeing occasional time to spell Szarka. The team also has Josh Johnson and Kemper Hodges, seniors who have started games in the past. Freshman in Howard Fisher IV has earned notice on the practice field and a spot on the depth chart.
