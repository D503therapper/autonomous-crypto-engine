This is a wildly productive offense, second in the country in total offense (550.8 ypg) and top of the table in terms of passing offense (392.5 ypg). They're averaging 37 points per game still led by quarterback
Kevin Jennings
. The veteran signal caller has thrown for nearly 1500 yards, 12 touchdowns, and completing 77% of his passes. He hasn't done too much damage on the ground, adding one score, but he's certainly capable. He's still turning the ball over plenty though, with five interceptions and some costly fumbles including one out of the end zone against Louisville.
This offense will be very familiar for the Eagles, but they haven't shown any ability to slow them down in two tries. They'll spread it among their receiving corps. Yamir Knight leads the way with 27 catches for 345 yards and two scores. He's going to work underneath and run after the catch. Jalen Cooper and ECU transfer
Yannick Smith
complement with size and verticality. Knight and Smith both are questionable this week and Smith didn't play last week. In the red zone, the tight ends Pittman and Ohrstrom have six combined touchdowns.
The rushing game isn't as potent, ranking 75th nationally, but they have a veteran interior and a capable running back in Kendrick Raphael, who is averaging six yards a carry, is dangerous out of the backfield, and has five touchdowns to his name. Raphael had 119 yards and a score against BC last season while at Cal. Their preseason All-ACC tackle did not play last week and is questionable ahead of this week.
