What a Win Over Penn State Would Mean For USC Trojans Season

The Trojans have goals to make the College Football Playoff for the first time in program history in their fifth season under Riley. Those hopes will be on the line Saturday against the Nittany Lions, as a loss likely means the Trojans would need to win out to be considered for a spot in the 12-team CFP bracket.
USC still has ranked opponents remaining on the schedule, including matchups with the No. 5 Ohio State Buckeyes (Oct. 31), No. 7 Indiana Hoosiers (Nov. 14), and No. 21 UCLA Bruins (Nov. 28). These three games will also be crucial to the Trojans' chase for a spot in the CFP.
The Trojans' road matchup against the Wisconsin Badgers on Oct. 24 at Camp Randall Stadium is also a game USC can’t afford to overlook. Wisconsin is 4-1 and undefeated in Big Ten play, with conference wins over Penn State and Michigan State.
