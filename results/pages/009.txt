SOUTH BEND, Ind. (WNDU) - One of Notre Dame’s key defenders may miss Saturday’s top-10 matchup at BYU, according to the availability report released by the team Monday afternoon.
Star cornerback Leonard Moore remains questionable with a concussion he suffered on Oct. 1
against North Carolina
. In five games this season, he has been targeted 13 times, allowing six catches for 57 yards and no touchdowns,
according to Pro Football Focus
. He is still seeking his first interception of the season.
On the flip side, linebacker Kyngstonn Viliamu-Asa is back on the depth chart after missing
Saturday’s game against Stanford
with a thumb injury and defensive lineman Jason Onye has been upgraded to questionable after missing the past five weeks with
a left ankle injury
suffered in Notre Dame’s
season-opening win over Wisconsin
.
Players ruled out
Freshman cornerback Chaz Smith has been ruled out for the season with a left knee injury. He previously suffered
a scaphoid fracture in his right wrist
during fall camp.
The following five players also have been ruled out for Saturday’s game:
Christopher Burgess Jr., defensive lineman — left tibia
Tiki Hola, defensive lineman — right knee
Charles Jagusah, offensive lineman — left humerus
