STARKVILLE —
Mississippi State
football's SEC availability report for the
Vanderbilt
game was released on Nov. 5.
Neither quarterback
AJ Swann
nor starting running back Fluff Bothwell are on the report after getting injured in the previous game at Texas.
The Bulldogs (4-4, 2-4 SEC) host the
Commodores
(3-5, 0-5) at Davis Wade Stadium on Nov. 7 (3:15 p.m., SEC Network). Swann would likely be MSU's starting quarterback for the second game in a row after Kamario Taylor's season-ending foot surgery.
Starting safety Isaac Smith remains out, while starting linebacker Tyler Lockhart is questionable. Wide receiver Ayden Williams was probable on the initial report but dropped from it on the Nov. 5 update.
