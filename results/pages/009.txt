Tulane visits South Florida on Saturday, and Tony Tellez is laying the points with USF -6.5. Tony trusts USF’s offense and home scoring to cover the number. Here are his ncaaf picks for Tulane-South Florida, with the odds, form and the matchup breakdown.
Tulane comes to Tampa at 6-5 to face a South Florida team also sitting at 6-5. DraftKings, via ESPN, makes USF a 6.5-point home favorite. USF is a -238 moneyline favorite and Tulane is +195. ESPN’s matchup predictor leans to the Bulls at 67.9% to Tulane’s 32.1%, lining up with Tony’s side.

USF, meanwhile, completes about 65% of its passes at 7.1 yards per attempt. On the road, Tulane averages under 20 points per game, while USF posts around 32 at home.
