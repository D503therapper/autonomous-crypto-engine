When's the next update? Explaining SEC's weekly availability reports
The SEC posts multiple availability reports throughout the week heading into conference matchups, with initial reports required to be posted by 7:10 p.m. CT Wednesday. Additional reports arrive no later than 7 p.m. Thursday and Friday evening, with a final pregame availability report no later than 90 minutes before kickoff, meaning this week's final report will come no later than 10:15 a.m. CT Saturday.
According to reporting policy, "probable" describes a player with at least 75% expectancy of playing, "questionable" means a player has a 50% chance of playing, and "doubtful" means a player is unlikely to play, or has a 25% chance of being available.
Availability reports don't include details on injuries, nor additional context surrounding a player's status.
