Desperate for a win, Florida State football (2-2, 0-1) welcomes Virginia (3-1, 1-0) to Tallahassee on Saturday afternoon at 3:30.
On Wednesday, Florida State released its initial injury report against the ‘Hoos. Among notable names that did not see the field last week against Central Arkansas, Darryl Desir was listed as out, with Quindarrius Jones and Caleb LaVallee listed as probable. Given Tony White’s defense has struggled in the run and pass game, getting those two back will be critical to securing an upset home victory.
On offense, RB Samuel Singleton Jr. was listed as out.
Switching gears to UVA, the ‘Hoos are notably healthy, with only three players listed as questionable after playing Delaware last weekend.
The full availability of both teams can be found below:
