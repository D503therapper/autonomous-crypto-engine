TITLE: Old Dominion vs. UConn: TV info, keys to the game, behind the numbers | The UConn Blog
DATES SEEN: ['Nov 20, 2026', 'November 21, 2026']

Last week, Connecticut responded to consecutive losses with a commanding home win. The Huskies have two chances at bowl eligibility: this game against 3-7 Old Dominion and a visit to Wyoming, a competitor at the top of the Mountain West at 7-3. The Huskies have to set the tone early in this matchup, as their strongest side of the ball, the offense, goes up against ODU’s top unit.
The Monarchs’ defense is ranked No. 43 nationally in total defense and 24th in Net EPA/play. However, Coastal Carolina still managed to complete seven 15-plus-yard passes in a loss to them last week. The Husky offense is 29th in Net EPA/play, 24th in scoring, and 27th in explosive play rate, ranking an impressive 13th in yards per play.
Osborne will be looking downfield for his top targets Porter, Ryder Treadway, JD Willoughby, and recent contributors Ky “Flash” Wilson, Cam Abshire, or Xavier Watson in the passing game. The other programs UConn has faced with current top-45 defenses are UMass and Air Force.
In the run game, Cyncir Bowers has been leading the charge out of the backfield while Osborne has been a factor as well. Trey Cornist and Kenji Christian have still been seeing time, and all have a chance to play a role in Saturday’s success.
