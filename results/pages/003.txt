TITLE: Tulane Must Get Back To Run Game Basics Against Charlotte
DATES SEEN: ['Oct 28, 2026']

The
Tulane Green Wave
need their running game to show back up, and fast as they get ready to play the Charlotte 49ers on a short week.
The touted room of talented backs has certainly taken some hits in the injury department. It was a strong, deep room that could handle a fair number of injuries, but the room thinned out quick against the UTSA Roadrunners.
Maurice Turner did not play a snap in the game, and Jamauri McClure re-injured his foot on the first carry of the contest. He clearly did not look right, was in the medical tent a bit, and only had four carries on the evening. McClure hasn’t participated in practice this short week.
Turner, optimistically, has been practicing in full. But it’s put a lot of
pressure on Johnnie Daniels
, who was the leading back on the night with eight carries – which is a negligible number at best.
That seemed like a solid and obvious formula for a team with really no answer at quarterback and one that has really seen correlated success on the field with a running game leading the attack.
It’s not clear what leads this Green Wave team’s attack. But they need to
get back to the basics against Charlotte
, and that includes figuring out how to run the ball effectively again.
