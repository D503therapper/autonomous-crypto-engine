Colorado football injury update: First participation report for game vs. Utah
Brian Howell
@ BrianHowell33
10/15/26
Colorado
football team will seek its first Big 12 win of the season when No. 11 Utah visits Folsom Field.
The Buffs will once again play without receiver Danny Scudero, as well as a few others.
On Wednesday night, the first player availability report of the week was posted. CU (2-3, 0-2 Big 12) will host Utah (5-0, 2-0) on Saturday (8:15 p.m. MT, ESPN).
Several Buffs on report
Five players are listed as out, none of which are a surprise.
Scudero, who hasn’t played since Week 2 against Weber State, will miss his fourth consecutive game. Also out are freshmen defensive backs Preston Ashley and Braylon Edwards, senior linebacker Tyler Martinez and tight end Zach Atkins. None of them played in the Buffs’ last game, Oct. 3 against Texas Tech.
Receiver Kam Perry is listed as doubtful, while defensive end Lamont Lester Jr. is questionable.
Three other players are probable for
Colorado
: defensive end Immanuel Ezeogu, defensive lineman Dylan Manuel, and tight end Fisher Clements.
