SEC football fans now have more clarity on the status of their respective rosters ahead of Saturdays in the fall. The league instituted a new policy in 2024 that requires schools to submit
official availability reports
.
Each Wednesday of SEC contests, both schools will share an availability report. It will be updated nightly, with one final ruling an hour before kickoff on Saturdays. Players must be listed as out (0% chance to play), doubtful (25% chance to play), questionable (50% chance to play), or probable (75% chance to play).

There were a couple of notable updates to Thursday’s report compared to the initial one on Wednesday.
Linebacker Tavion Wallace, who was not listed on the first report, has now been added as questionable. The sophomore has appeared in three of Kentucky’s four games, logging six tackles and two pass deflections. Additionally, starting safety Jordan Castell was removed from the report after being listed as questionable on Wednesday.
We also continue to monitor DJ Miller. The sophomore pass-catcher has dealt with soft-tissue issues ever since he arrived on campus. It flared up during fall camp and returned last week. He was once again listed as doubtful on Thursday’s report. Miller has five catches for 96 yards on the season.
It’s not a big deal for many common fans, but you should keep a keen eye on the long-snapping situation. If there’s one team that can take advantage of special teams mistakes, it’s South Carolina. Those could flare up if Cole Marszalek, listed as doubtful for the second straight day, is unable to go.

There were not changes to Thursday’s report for South Carolina. We all knew that Dylan Stewart was going to be sidelined by a suspension for this contest. We will be keeping a keen eye on Gabriel Brownlow-Dindy’s status. The fifth-year player is a veteran on the interior of the Gamecocks’ defensive line. He did not play last Saturday in Tuscaloosa.
