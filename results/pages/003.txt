The initial Wednesday injury report is officially out, and Michigan certainly still has some health issues. As expected, Andrew Sprague is still out, and there are no new names that are officially off the table for Saturday. Wide receiver Kendrick Bell is listed as doubtful, which is new. The offensive line continues to be banged up beyond Sprague, with Jake Guarnera and Brady Norton being added as questionable, while Nate Efobi is listed as probable. The good news is that, after being out all season, Andrew Babalola is listed as questionable.
Dom Nichols and John Henry Daley continue to be questionable, and linebacker Aisea Moa joins them there. Andrew Marsh is listed as probable.
