New Mexico State vs Florida International Key Matchups and Handicap
The home team has protected its turf in each of the last two meetings between these programs, and the Aggies and Panthers did not meet in Conference USA play last season. Florida International won the most recent meeting in October 2024 by three touchdowns, out-gaining New Mexico State by nearly 200 yards. This time, the scheduling edge and the defensive edge both belong to the Panthers.
Florida International Rested and Ready
Florida International comes in off a bye, giving the Panthers an extra week to prepare. The advantage may be even larger than one week, considering their last game came against FCS-level Long Island as a 37.5-point favorite. That kind of matchup allows a coaching staff to rest key players and focus its preparation on the conference schedule ahead.
New Mexico State on a Short Week
New Mexico State is working on a condensed week after beating Western Kentucky at home last Thursday. Playing a road conference game on short rest against a team coming off a bye is a difficult spot for any program, and it adds to the challenge of facing one of the best defenses in the country.
NMSU Win Over WKU Was Flattering
The 34-13 final against Western Kentucky probably flattered the Aggies. The Hilltoppers out-gained New Mexico State and picked up more first downs, but they went 0-for-3 on fourth down, and the Aggies returned an interception for a touchdown in the second quarter to kick off their scoring run. Bettors should be careful not to overrate a result built on fourth-down stops and a defensive score.
Florida International Defense Among the Nation’s Best
The Panthers’ defense ranks No. 5 in the country in yards per play allowed and has given up an average of just 14.5 points per game this season. That unit is the main reason the under is 3-1 in Florida International’s games. Against a New Mexico State offense that was out-gained in its last game, the Panthers should be able to limit scoring opportunities and keep the Aggies from relying on big plays.
Panthers Building Under Willie Simmons
FIU head coach Willie Simmons has a proven track record of building winners. He took over Florida A and M in 2018, when the program was on a seven-year losing streak, and snapped it in his first season. The Rattlers went 9-2 the following year, and by 2023 they finished 12-1, won the SWAC, and captured the Celebration Bowl. In the 2.5 seasons since he left, FAMU has gone 13-16. Simmons can also recruit, as the Panthers signed the No. 3 class of incoming talent in Conference USA this season, while New Mexico State brought in the lowest-rated class in the league. Simmons may have options at the end of the year, but for now, Florida International has earned trust in a spot like this.
