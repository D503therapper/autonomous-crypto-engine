RUSTON –
Fresh and coming off a bye week, Louisiana Tech is set to close out the non-conference slate of the 2026 season hosting Army at Origin Bank Field at Joe Aillet Stadium on Saturday, October 3 at 6:30 p.m. CT. It will be the first time in the series the two programs meet in Ruston and just the fourth all-time meeting.
GAMEDAY INFORMATION
Date:
Saturday, Oct. 3
Army leads 3-0
In Ruston:
First Meeting
The Bulldogs (1-2) look to extend a three-game home winning streak dating back to last season, as well as seeking their first-ever win over the Black Knights (2-1, 1-1 American) in program history. Army came out on top over the Bulldogs in the 2024 Radiance Technologies Independence Bowl, 27-6, in their previous matchup.
It was the first time sharing the field since 2013—a neutral-site contest in Dallas that saw Army as the victors, 35-16. That was the highest-scoring matchup in the series' history. The 2008 inaugural matchup took place on Army's home turf in West Point, with the Black Knights taking a 14-7 win.
HONORING THE STARS & STRIPES
Saturday's game vs. Army will be Military Appreciation Day and Legends Day at Joe Aillet Stadium, honoring and celebrating all those that have served in the armed forces, past and present. In celebration of this special game day, the Bulldogs will wear their red Stars & Stripes helmet, blue jersey and white pants against the Black Knights.
