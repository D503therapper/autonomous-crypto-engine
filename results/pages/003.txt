Texas Tech football won't face TCU's QB1 in critical Big 12 showdown on Thanksgiving
The first Big 12 injury report for the Texas Tech vs. TCU game shows that the Horned Frogs will be without QB Jaden Craig, who has started 10 games this season.
By
Matthew Conner
|
On Thanksgiving night, the Texas Tech football team will host one of the most important games in program history. If the Red Raiders beat TCU, they will earn a spot in the Big 12 Championship Game next week in Arlington, Texas.
The opponent on Thursday night will be without starting quarterback
Jaden Craig
, who is listed as "Out" on the first Big 12 injury report, which was released Monday night. However, it is fair to wonder if that is actually a good thing for the Red Raiders.
The senior QB has been a disappointment for the Frogs this season. He was one of the prized transfers in college football this offseason after earning first-team All-Ivy League honors at Harvard in 2025.
In fact, some reports suggested he was on Texas Tech's radar before the Red Raiders landed Brendan Sorsby in the transfer portal. Eventually, Craig was tabbed to replace Josh Hoover at TCU after Hoover transferred to Indiana.
