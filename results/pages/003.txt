One of
Georgia Tech’s
top offensive players was added to its lengthy injury list Wednesday.
Justice Haynes, the Jackets’ leading rusher this season, was listed as doubtful with an undisclosed injury on the initial ACC availability report ahead of Saturday’s game at Virginia Tech.
Haynes, a senior transfer from Michigan, has rushed for 462 yards and scored five total touchdowns in five games this season.
“Justice is banged up,” Tech coach Brent Key said. “We’ll see how he is as we move forward.”
Tech’s running back room has already been shorthanded for weeks.
Senior Malachi Hosley will miss his third straight game with what Key called an ankle injury.
