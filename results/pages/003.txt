Notre Dame injury report vs. Syracuse: Two key starters ruled out for the game
Kyle Kelly
@ ByKyleKelly
11/23/26
Add as preferred source
Add
Share
The injury bug continues to take its toll on the Notre Dame football program as the regular season draws to a close and the College Football Playoff approaches.
On Monday, the Fighting Irish announced that senior safety
Luke Talich
is
out for the season
with an Achilles injury. Senior wide receiver
Jaden Greathouse
(right hand) and junior linebacker
Kyngstonn Viliamu-Asa
(left knee) have also been ruled out for Saturday’s game at Syracuse (noon ET, ABC). They will have the chance to return in the postseason.
Talich’s season-ending injury is yet another blow to the Irish defense. Senior linebacker
Drayk Bowen
and cornerback
DJ McKinney
both remain out with right ankle injuries. Head coach
Marcus Freeman
said last month that McKinney would be out for “an extended period,” while no timetable for Bowen’s return has been announced. Although Talich’s injury is season-ending, Freeman would not give a definitive timetable on his return.
“(Syracuse’s) wide receiver
Calvin Russell
tore his Achilles playing basketball,” Freeman said. “And he’s back playing at a high level right now. I don’t know how long it was, but it ain’t a year. I think they’re all different in terms of the timetable and return to play. Everybody’s different. So, I don’t know exactly Luke’s timetable when he’ll be able to return to play football.”
On offense, Notre Dame will be without Greathouse, its starting slot wide receiver, and could also be missing starting left tackle
Will Black
for the second consecutive game. Black, who suffered a concussion against Boston College on Nov. 14, is listed as questionable for the second straight week after missing Saturday’s win over SMU.
Despite the collection of bad news, Notre Dame did receive some positive news regarding junior safety
Brauntae Johnson’s
availability. After being listed as questionable last week — and out every previous week since suffering a left foot injury against Wisconsin — Johnson does not have an injury designation for Saturday’s game. He dressed for the SMU game but was held out as a precaution. Talich was Johnson’s replacement at field safety after Johnson injury Week 1.
Aside from those changes, the rest of Notre Dame’s injury report remains unchanged from last week.
