The overall injury picture for NC State remains grim, but offensive guard Kamen Smith is no longer listed this week, which means he'll be back on the field. Thank goodness for that. Duke Scott remains out, as do JoJo Trader and Tristan Teasdell.
If you think NC State has it bad, just have a look there at Cal, which is so depleted at the skill positions that it had to play a trio of defensive players on offense at SMU last week. One of them, starting linebacker AJ Tuitele, is now listed as questionable.

The Bears could be without their top three running backs, second-leading receiver, and a pair of starting offensive linemen—all are among the group listed as questionable for Saturday. They moved a couple defensive backs over to help at running back and receiver against SMU and it's looking like that will be necessary again. That's rough, man.
