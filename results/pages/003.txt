According to Aztecs head coach Sean Lewis, nearly three dozen players are affected by injuries and illness designations heading into its Week 5 matchup against the Bobcats. 17 players are currently listed as doubtful or out. In particular, the Aztecs tight end room currently has all seven players listed as either out, doubtful or questionable.
"The football gods have not really favored us with the injury luck," Lewis told reporters Wednesday. "Guys have got to be ready to step into the light when the time comes. ... We've been given a nice storm here. But this too shall pass, and we've got to be able to see our way through it."
Lewis noted that, because of the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), he would not identify which players tested positive.
