It's been a year full of injuries for this talented
Ohio State
team meaning we've had more interest in the availability report that the Buckeye program releases the morning of every game day.
The fine folks at OSU just released injury information for the Maryland game and there are still some key members out. There is a bit of good news as it appears as though several players that were banged up will be back in the lineup on this frosty Saturday.
TreVeyon Henderson
is available after missing last week, as is cornerback
Denzel Burke
and right tackle
Dawand Jones
Advertisement
Missing, however, is still some running back depth with
Miyan Williams
listed as unavailable. And, as we suspected, star receiver
Jaxon Smith-Njigba
will be out yet again recovering from the hamstring injury he sustained in game one vs.
Notre Dame
. Also underrated perhaps is that long snapper
Bradley Robinson
is out.
