Penn’s quarterback situation remains up in the air following injury to starter Miles Teodecki
Freshman Miles Teodecki has been the starter, but he left last week's game with a knee injury. Karson Siqueiros-Lasky was effective in his place, and says he's ready to start.
Penn quarterback Karson Siqueiros-Lasky, who stepped in last week after Miles Teodecki was injured, could get the start this week.

Quakers freshman quarterback Miles Teodecki suffered a knee injury early in the second quarter last Saturday that sidelined him for the rest of the game. Backup Karson Siqueiros-Lasky then

Teodecki was seen taking snaps at practice Tuesday, but coach Rick Santos declined to commit to a starter for Friday’s game at Franklin Field (7:30 p.m., ESPN+). Teodecki will continue to test his knee in the coming days.

Columbia senior quarterback Chase Goodwin also is off to a hot start. He is averaging 313 passing yards and with eight touchdowns.
Columbia is allowing a conference-worst 28.5 points per game. Santos hopes his offense can build off the success it had late against Howard.
“We have shot ourselves in the foot offensively,” Santos said. “I think that’s been the most frustrating thing as a team overall, especially being an offensive-minded coach. I felt like it wasn’t necessarily some of the things the other teams were doing. We had some plays that we left out there and didn’t make.
“Halfway through the third [against Howard], we did make some mistakes, but then really, for a quarter and a half, we played nearly perfect football. It gives us a ton of momentum, a ton of confidence.”
