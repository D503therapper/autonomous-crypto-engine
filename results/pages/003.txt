Main Story
Nicholls closes its 2026 season Thursday against Southeastern in the River Bell Classic, with the
Colonels
looking to end a two-game series skid and send their seniors out with a home win. Nicholls is coming off a 42-24 loss at No. 22 UIW, while Southeastern snapped a three-game losing streak by rallying past McNeese. The game kicks off at 6 p.m. CT at Manning Field in Thibodaux and streams on ESPN+.
