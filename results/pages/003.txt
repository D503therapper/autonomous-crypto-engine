Listed as questionable on Wednesday night’s ACC availability report, Pitt true freshman running back
Damon Ferguson Jr.
was removed entirely on Thursday, signaling that the Panthers’ top rusher will take the field against Georgia Tech on Saturday night.
Defensive tackle
Tyreek Robinson
joined tailback
Ja’Kyrian “Boosie” Turner
as questionable to play against the Yellow Jackets.
Earlier in the day, Pitt head coach Pat Narduzzi gave some insight into Turner’s injury situation, being that he was tabbed last Thursday as probable to play against No. 1 Miami, but was ruled out on Friday.
Pitt / pgt trucking
“No setback, it’s just seeing where he is and how he feels,”
Narduzzi said
. “I mean, you think he’s gonna go, you think he’s gonna go and then you go, ‘Oh.’ So we’ll just see how it goes. I mean, you’ve got 48 hours right now — well, more than that — really 50-something hours until game time, so we’ll see how it is. Again, we’re never gonna put a kid in jeopardy to get re-injured. He’s played 91 plays all year. It’s disappointing. Just like after a game, I look at myself and go, ‘Hey, what could I have done better?’ And take responsibility. It’s the same thing. We’ve just gotta get him on the field.
“Nobody wants to put him back out there and see him re-injured. He should’ve never got re-injured from the beginning, and we take that personal.”
