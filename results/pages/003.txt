New Matchup Model

The six seasons using that predictive matchup model
produced good results on Best Bets (2103-1776-51, 54.2%) despite being an
unlucky 18-40-4 on games decided by overtime (instead of 38-20-4 on those games had they been graded at the end of regulation
) and winning only 46.7% of the close games (283-323-51 on Best Bets decided by less than 3 points) instead of the 50% expected in the long run.
Had we won 50% of the close games and had those games altered due to OT
(i.e. dogs not covering)
produce the results they would have in regulation then we’d have been 55.2% winners the last five seasons
, which is incredibly good in Basketball. The new model has won despite the bad luck – particularly in the last for seasons (+112.5 Stars on Best Bets).
