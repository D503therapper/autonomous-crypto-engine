Clemson head coach Dabo Swinney gave the latest injury update on starting slot wide receiver Tyler Brown on Monday night.
During his radio show, Swinney was jokingly asked by a texter if he could donate an ankle to Brown, to ensure he gets to play this week in Saturday’s game vs. rival South Carolina.
“I’m with ya,” Swinney said.
Brown has missed the last three games with an ankle injury.
Swinney said they’ll have to wait and see on Brown as far as his status and availability for Saturday.
“He’s still working his way back, so we’ll see,” Swinney said.
Saturday’s game against South Carolina at Clemson’s Memorial Stadium is slated for a 3 p.m. kickoff on ESPN2.
Brown has recorded 36 catches for 332 yards in eight games this season. The senior has also rushed for 83 yards and a touchdown.
