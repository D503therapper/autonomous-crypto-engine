In the National Polls
Yale received votes in the AFCA FCS Coaches Poll this week. The Bulldogs fell out of both national polls following Saturday's 41-23 loss to Rhode Island.
The Series
Yale has won six of its last eight meetings with Penn. The Bulldogs are 15-22-1 all-time against the Quakers at Franklin Field and 52-39-1 overall.
In their most recent meeting on Oct. 25, 2025, Yale defeated Penn 35-13 at the Yale Bowl. The victory was the second in a six-game winning streak that propelled the Bulldogs to their 19th Ivy League title.
