First and Ten Notes
Missouri State closes out its October slate on Tuesday when the Bears entertain Sam Houston in a nationally-televised ESPN2 showdown at 7 p.m. The "Black Out" game will mark the first-ever meeting between the Bears and Bearkats as CUSA and FBS foes.
Missouri State is 4-2 in nationally-televised Weekday CUSA games. The Bears are also 9-3 in their last dozen October games (4-1 at home).
