A new SEC policy has arrived in 2024. Kentucky football will be featured first in this new game week development. The conference now requires each of their 16 members to provide two “availability reports” during game week.
We got the first one on Wednesday night
.
Kentucky listed 13 players on the first availability report ahead of this weekend’s game against South Carolina. Players must be listed as out (0% chance to play), doubtful (25% chance to play), questionable (50% chance to play), or probable (75% chance to play).
Chip Trayanum | RB | Out
Darrion Henry-Young | DL | Out
Courtland Ford | OL | Out
Hardley Gilmore IV | WR | Out
Josaih Hayes | DL | Out
Tavion Gadson | DL | Out
Brandon White | WR | Doubtful
Devin Smith | LB | Doubtful
DJ Waller Jr. | CB | Questionable
Keeshawn Silver | DL | Probable
Jamarion Wilcox | RB | Probable
Grant Godfrey | LB | Probable
Josh Kattus | TE | Probable

Chip Trayanum
