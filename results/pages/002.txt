The resumes
South Carolina was unranked in the majority of major polls this preseason; however, the Gamecocks received votes in most major rankings. That continued through two weeks of the season but ended with USC’s loss to Mississippi State in week three. A four-game winning streak put Carolina back in the rankings, though a loss to Arkansas kicked them out of the AP poll and the coaches poll. The CFP Selection Committee showed mercy to Shane Beamer’s team for losing without LaNorris Sellers, ranking them at No. 21. However, after the Georgia loss, the team fell out of the CFP rankings, too.
USC is currently No. 35 overall in ESPN’s Football Power Index (FPI). South Carolina is No. 34 on offense, No. 59 on defense, and No. 39 on special teams in the metrics’ efficiency rankings.
This summer, the Gamecocks put EDGE Dylan Stewart and wide receiver Nyck Harbor on the Coaches Preseason All-SEC team. Other Preseason All-SEC teams included Stewart, Harbor (as both a wide receiver and as a kick returner), quarterback LaNorris Sellers, left tackle Jacarrius Peak, linebackers Fred “JayR” Johnson and Justin Okoronkwo, cornerbacks Vicari Swain (also as a punt returner) and Judge Collier, safety Peyton Williams, and punter Mason Love. Safety J’Zavien Currence and EDGE Julian Walker also made various Preseason All-SEC Freshman teams.
Last season, South Carolina went 4-8 and just 1-7 in the SEC. That was tied for 13th overall in the conference. Only Arkansas had a worse league record in 2025.
Other assistants like Jason Smith (assistant offensive line coach), Dre Bly (senior defensive assistant), Luke Doty (assistant defensive backs coach), Cade Fortin (assistant wide receivers coach), Michael Krysl (assistant special teams coach), Jacob Oehrlein (assistant tight ends coach), and Jordan Williams (assistant defensive line coach) are also new to the staff.
Rex Hogan is now the program’s director of scouting, Marius Aleksa is the new director of process development, and LaFayette Stewart Jr. is the director of recruiting for defense.
South Carolina promoted Torrian Gray to co-defensive coordinator this offseason. Trey Money, who has long helped defensive coordinator Clayton White with linebackers, is now the official linebackers coach.
After a preseason injury before the 2025 season, defensive line coach Travian Robertson is back in a full-time capacity.

Following a transfer from Purdue, Will Heldt‘s second year in Clemson has been strong, both against the run and as a pass rusher. Heldt has seven sacks this season, and he has 14 tackles for loss. He has also accomplished that while being a focal point of opposing blocking schemes. All-American honors could be incoming for Heldt, and he will surely be an early NFL Draft selection next April.
Sammy Brown is the best second-level player on the Clemson defense, and he has sideline-to-sideline game-wrecking ability against the run. Brown is fast and physical, and when the defensive line plays well in front of him, he is a tackling machine. Coverage isn’t the best part of Brown’s game, but he is athletic enough to hold his own in certain matchups. However, when given see-ball-get-ball freedom in the run game, he is always around the ball.
The top NFL Draft pick from Clemson in next April’s NFL Draft will likely be cornerback Ashton Hampton. He could come off the board in the top half of the first round. For this fall, the Thorpe Award finalist has been the top coverage option for the Tigers. He is sticky in man coverage and has good instincts in zone. Though not often tested, Hampton is a playmaker. He has two interceptions and 14 pass breakups this season. He is also a strong tackler on the perimeter.
Injuries for South Carolina
*Because this week’s game will be against a non-SEC opponent, there will not be an SEC-mandated availability report.*
Quarterback LaNorris Sellers suffered an ankle injury against Texas A&M but returned to lead the Gamecocks to a win. He did not play against Arkansas. Sellers returned against Georgia and appears set to play this weekend against Clemson.
Wide receiver Nyck Harbor got banged up against Texas A&M and could not go against Arkansas. He played against Georgia, but head coach Shane Beamer said on Sunday that Harbor might not be available against Clemson.
Leading receiver Mazeo Bennett got knocked out of the Arkansas game with an unspecified upper-body injury. He missed last week but should return against Clemson this weekend. It is the same story for starting EDGE Anthony Addison and starting corner Judge Collier. Bennett and Addison received more positive updates from Shane Beamer than Collier, but he said that all three should play on Saturday.
Starting running back Matt Fuller suffered a season-ending injury earlier this season against Oklahoma.
Nitro Tuggle, Dayne Arnett, Kobby Sakyi-Prah, Jalewis Solomon, and Seth Smith have been out for a couple of weeks and have had no recent health updates.
Star EDGE Dylan Stewart, defensive tackles Gabe Brownlow-Dindy, Kelby Collins, and Noah Clark, linebackers Fred “JayR” Johnson and Justin Okoronkwo, and defensive back Peyton Williams all got banged up against Georgia. Only Stewart, Brownlow-Dindy, and Collins were mentioned by head coach Shane Beamer as still dealing with their injuries during practice this week.
Wide receivers Jayden Sellers and Jayden Gibson are out for the year with lower-body injuries.
Offensive lineman Josiah Thompson and receiver Jackson Repp are also out for the year.
