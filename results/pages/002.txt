TITLE: Cajuns ready to open Sun Belt play against Arkansas State for homecoming
DATES SEEN: ['Oct. 1, 2026']

LAFAYETTE, La. (Acadiana News First) - The Louisiana Ragin’ Cajuns begin Sun Belt Conference play on homecoming weekend when they host Arkansas State on Saturday at 7 p.m.
The Red Wolves enter the matchup with a 2-2 record and momentum from a recent win over Kennesaw State. Head coach Michael Desormeaux emphasized the team has been preparing for Arkansas State’s tendencies since early in the week.
“Getting ready for a really good Arkansas State team,” he said. “Certainly, that will be a challenge with things that they do we talked about on Monday. We’re preparing for that.”
Desormeaux reported mixed injury news ahead of the game. Starting linebacker Conor McPherson will be out for the rest of the season, though wide receiver Shelton Sampson looks ready to play this weekend.
Lineman Kaden Moreau noted that preparation follows a similar routine each week, with specific attention paid to Arkansas State’s personnel.
“Same thing every week; you got a game plan for your match-ups,” he explained. “Their linebackers, they got a guy that was injured. He is a really good player, so their backups are having to come in, but they are solid on defense, and their secondary does a good job as well.”
Defensive lineman Priest Ashe expressed a similar readiness heading into the game.
“Rain, sleet, or snow, we going out there to ball,” he described. “It don’t matter if it’s in the parking lot, the grass; hey, line the football up. We ready to play.”
Ashe also addressed the start of conference play.
“It feels amazing, but we know the job isn’t finished. We keep the main thing the main thing. We always have something to work on every week. Improve on and we challenge ourselves amongst the group to see how much better we can get from the previous week to the next week,” Ashe noted.
The Cajuns kick off against Arkansas State on Saturday at 7 p.m.
