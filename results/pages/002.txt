SEC releases first availability report for Arkansas football at Vanderbilt
October 14, 2026 at 7:34 p.m.
by
Ethan Westerman
Arkansas wide receiver Antonio Jordan (center) and teammates look on during a timeout, Saturday, Sept. 12, 2026, during the third quarter of the Razorbacks' 43-10 loss to the Utah Utes at Rice-Eccles Stadium in Salt Lake City. (Hank Layton/WholeHogSports)
The SEC released the first player availability report Wednesday night for the
Arkansas football
team’s Week 7 game at Vanderbilt.
Most notable on the report for the Razorbacks (2-4, 0-3 SEC) is wide receiver Antonio Jordan being questionable.
Jordan was on the sideline for Arkansas' 24-17 loss to Tennessee last week but was not fully dressed out. He ranks seventh among the team's wide receivers with 3 catches for 25 yards, and at 6-foot-6 is the backup option for star wideout Chris Marshall.
Defensive back Braydon Lee, who has not recorded any tackles, was listed as out. Backup left tackle Josiah Clemons was listed as doubtful.
Arkansas coach Ryan Silverfield has not specified the nature of any injuries to players on the report.
Vanderbilt (3-3, 0-3)
has seven players listed as out, headlined by backup quarterback Blaze Berlowitz, who broke his collarbone in the fourth quarter of last week's loss to Ole Miss.
The Commodores will also be without backup offensive linemen Gunner Givens and Mitchell Smith. Starting edge rusher Brian Allen and backup safety Dontae Carter are questionable.
The conference requires availability reports for football games between SEC opponents. Arkansas and Vanderbilt must submit daily updates leading up to a final report 90 minutes prior to kickoff Saturday.
Schools that report late or inaccurately will be penalized between $25,000 and $100,000, according to the SEC's reporting policy.
Saturday’s game at FirstBank Stadium in Nashville, Tennessee, is scheduled to begin at 6 p.m. on ESPN2.

Availability status definitions, per SEC policy:
Available
– The player is fully available for the next game.
Probable
– Likely to play, barring any setbacks.
Questionable
– Uncertain to play due to injury or condition
Doubtful
– Unlikely to play due to significant concerns.
Out
– Player will not participate in the upcoming game.
Game Time Decision
– Final decision will be made closer to the game.
