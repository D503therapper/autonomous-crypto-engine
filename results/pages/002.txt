OPENING DRIVE
UTSA and Rice will meet for the 14th time on Saturday, making the Owls tied with North Texas for the most-played opponent in program history.
The Roadrunners lead the series, 9-4, and set a program record for points in the last meeting, a 61-13 home triumph on Oct. 10, 2025.
The series is even at 3-3 in games played in Houston, with Rice pulling out a 29-27 win on Oct. 12, 2024.
At 3-1, UTSA is off to its best four-game start since a 4-0 mark in 2021.

LAST TIME OUT
UTSA registered 542 yards of offense and scored the second-most points in a game in program history, holding off Colorado State, 59-45, in the nonconference finale last Saturday at the Alamodome. The Roadrunners rallied from a 14-0 deficit early in the contest with 35 unanswered points to take the lead for good, keeping the Rams at bay while running their record to 3-1, their best four-game start since 2021. UTSA passed for a season-high 303 yards and rushed for 239 in coming up just two points shy of the school record of 61 points set last year against Rice. The two teams combined for 104 points, the most in any UTSA game, and both squads had a kickoff return for a touchdown, also a first in program history. Six different Roadrunners reached the end zone, paced by
Mekhi Anderson
and
Jeff Traylor
. The Roadrunners welcome back 49 members of last year’s team that capped a second straight seven-win campaign with a 57-20 victory over FIU in the First Responder Bowl, their third consecutive bowl win. UTSA lists 10 returning starters with six on offense, two on defense and a pair of specialists.
SCHEDULE BREAKDOWN
UTSA’s 12-game regular season schedule features four non-conference games and eight American Conference contests. The Roadrunners kicked off the campaign on Sept. 5 with a 45-16 home triumph over UTRGV, which played its inaugural season of football in 2025 as a member of the Southland Conference. UTSA played back-to-back road games at Texas State and Texas and closed out the non-league slate at home against Colorado State on Sept. 26. The Roadrunners have home conference games scheduled against South Florida (Oct. 8), Navy (Oct. 17), North Texas (Nov. 14) and Tulsa (Nov. 27 or 28), while they will travel to Rice (Oct. 3), Tulane (Oct. 24), Florida Atlantic (Nov. 5) and UAB (Nov. 21). The American Conference Championship Game will be played Dec. 5.
STAYING IN STATE
The Roadrunners will be staying close to home for the majority of the 2026 schedule. UTSA is slated to play its first seven games in the state of Texas, not leaving the Lone Star State until the Oct. 24 contest at Tulane in New Orleans, Louisiana. The Roadrunners have two more home games after that, giving them nine total regular season matchups inside their home state. In addition to the game in New Orleans, UTSA also will visit the states of Alabama (UAB) and Florida (Florida Atlantic) this fall.
