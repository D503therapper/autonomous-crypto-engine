Southern Miss Injury Report & More: Robert Briggs Out for Season as Arkansas State Looms
Southern Miss will be without running back Robert Briggs for the remainder of the season as the Golden Eagles deal with injuries and look to bounce back Saturday against Arkansas State.

HATTIESBURG, Miss.
– Southern Miss running back Robert Briggs will miss the rest of the season after tearing an upper hamstring tendon during the Golden Eagles’
loss at Troy
, Coach Blake Anderson said at his weekly press conference on Monday. Briggs sustained the injury on his 44-yard touchdown run in the third quarter of the 55-34 defeat.
The injury will likely require surgery, and the news came after Southern Miss’ staff worked hard to get Briggs healthy enough to return to the field. “Just when he gets back, he pops it again,” Coach Anderson said.
Injury Updates and Linebacker Depth
Avery Sledge (#33) with Coach Joe Bolden going over last-minute adjustments before the scrimmage at The Rock. | Josh House
The Golden Eagles are expected to be without linebacker Avery Sledge for approximately four to six weeks because of a broken rib, Coach Anderson said. “He’s in a lot of pain,” Coach A said. “That’s not going to change in the next few weeks.”
Southern Miss could regain some help at linebacker, however. Coach A said he expects Tyler Lassiter to return after missing the Troy game. Beyond the injuries to Briggs and Sledge, he described the team’s remaining health as minor bumps and bruises that should benefit from the extra recovery time from playing last Tuesday night.
Southern Miss linebacker Tyler Lassiter (#2) returns an interception during the Golden Eagles' first fall camp scrimmage on Saturday. | Southern Miss Athletics
The Golden Eagles return home to play Arkansas State at 6 p.m. this Saturday, Oct. 17, at The Rock (Renasant Stadium) hoping for a key home win in SBC play. The game will be streamed live on ESPN+. Southern Miss will enter the contest 1-4 overall and 0-1 in Sun Belt play.
Arkansas State is 2-4 and 0-2 in conference games after falling in a shootout this past Thursday, 56-49, to South Alabama.
The Golden Eagles also must protect the football after committing three turnovers at Troy. Coach A emphasized that blockers must handle pressure, receivers must compete for the ball, and the quarterback must make sound decisions.
Execution Remains the Focus
South Alabama vs. Arkansas State (#22 - Ezell Jolly) at Centennial Bank Stadium in Jonesboro, Arkansas on October 8, 2026. | Photo by Carla Wehmeyer - Arkansas State
Arkansas State’s offense is coming off a barnburner of a game, putting up a 49-point performance against South Alabama. Coach Anderson said the Red Wolves will arrive hungry for a conference win and confident in their passing game. Even as Southern Miss makes final adjustments to the injuries, Coach A said the preparation must stay focused on improving its own execution. “It’s way less about who we’re playing,” Anderson said. “We have to be our best team.”
Coach Anderson and his staff have a few more days to go before kickoff Saturday night against the Red Wolves at The Rock in Hattiesburg, MS. Stay tuned to
Southern Miss Golden Eagles On SI
for more football coverage leading into the weekend.
