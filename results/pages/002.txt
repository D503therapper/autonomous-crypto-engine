The Badgers will travel to the Rose Bowl to take on the No. 12-ranked UCLA Bruins in a game that could have major implications for both teams moving forward.

With the
initial availability report
being released ahead of Saturday’s matchup, it allows us to get an idea of what to expect in this matchup
OUT vs. Wisconsin
Nico Iamaleava, Quarterback
Tao Johnson, Safety
Mikey Matthews, Wide Receiver
Jonah Smith, Wide Receiver
Sam Yoon, Offensive Line
There are some big names on this list. You could argue that the first three players would all be massive additions to this team, especially in their respective positional groups, yet they will be out against Wisconsin.
Oregon outside linebacker Matayo Uiagalelei sacks UCLA quarterback Nico Iamaleava as the Oregon Ducks host the UCLA Bruins on Oct. 10, 2026, at Autzen Stadium in Eugene, Oregon. | USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect
Mikey Matthews and
Nico Iamaleava
are expected, as Matthews has been ruled out for the season and Iamaleava is likely in the same boat, but Tao Johnson is a loss that takes some life out of this defense. Johnson has not been able to catch a break all season long, as he missed the first couple of games this season with an injury sustained in the opening drive against Cal, and now he will land back on the injury report for another week.
He finally played last week against Oregon and had a solid game, as he logged one of the many takeaways by this UCLA defense in the form of an interception. It’s a tough break for Johnson and the Bruins, but hopefully we can see him get healthy enough to see the field soon.
Oregon running back Dierre Hill Jr., right, carries the ball as UCLA defensive back Tao Johnson pursues as the Oregon Ducks host the UCLA Bruins on Oct. 10, 2026, at Autzen Stadium in Eugene, Oregon. | USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect
Questionable vs. Wisconsin
Rodrick Pleasant, Cornerback
Ashton Sanders, Defensive Line
Jordan Davis, Offensive Line
Some huge potential losses are also deemed questionable ahead of the home matchup. Starting tackle Jordan Davis would be a huge loss, especially for a team that will need protection for their
new starting quarterback.
Not to mention, the run game will be on full display, and Davis has been fantastic so far this season.
