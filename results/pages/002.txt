Southern Miss vs. Troy prediction
Led by senior quarterback Landry Lyddy, the offense turned a corner in the loss to Tulane. Lyddy, who started in place of injured senior Ethan Hampton, threw for 243 yards and three touchdowns, and the Golden Eagles scored 21 points and did not turn the ball over.

Why Troy has an advantage:
Senior quarterback Goose Crowder is one of the most experienced quarterbacks in the nation.
Crowder, a sixth-year senior, has been at Troy since 2023. He suffered injuries in both 2024 and 2025, but has stayed healthy this season. Crowder has thrown for 822 yards, seven touchdowns and four interceptions through four games and can run when necessary. He rushed for 48 yards on five carries against Utah State in Week 4.
Southern Miss has trouble defending quarterbacks who can run. Auburn quarterback Byrum Brown ran for 88 yards and UConn's Kalieb Osborne scored twice. Crowder will be a similar challenge.
Southern Miss vs. Troy prediction
Troy 27, Southern Miss 23:
