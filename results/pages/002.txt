Ohio State
football released its first of four injury reports Wednesday ahead of this week's
matchup with USC
.
Here's a look at the notable Buckeyes who have been dealing with injuries and where they landed on the report.

Kenyatta Jackson, defensive end
Designation:
Not listed
Jackson was hurt late in Ohio State's win at Indiana on Oct. 17. He played through injury on the Buckeyes' final defensive snap, but he was helped off the field postgame.
Julian Sayin, quarterback
Designation:
Out
Sayin is expected to be out indefinitely. The Buckeyes are preparing to not have him back this season, per coach Ryan Day.
Leroy Roker, safety
Designation:
Out
Roker hasn't played since Ohio State's win at Iowa on Oct. 3. He's a fixture in the Buckeyes' dime packages.

Ja'Kobi Jackson, running back
Designation:
Out
Jackson hasn't played since Ohio State's win against Maryland on Oct. 10. He hopes to be back by The Game against Michigan,

Jay Timmons, defensive back
Designation:
Questionable
Timmons has dealt with an injury for multiple weeks. He did not play against Iowa and Maryland but returned for the win at Indiana. However, in that matchup, he missed a majority of the second half.
On Wednesday, Day said the plan was for Timmons to travel to USC.

John Walker, defensive tackle
Designation:
Not listed
Walker was injured against Indiana. He logged 41 snaps in the victory.
Austin Siereveld, offensive lineman
Designation:
Not listed
Siereveld missed the Maryland and Indiana games with an illness. However, he's expected to be back and play tackle against USC.
"Whether he's able to play a full game or not, we'll kind of see," Day said Wednesday. "He's had a full week of practice. He'll be available, and he'll be ready to go on Saturday."
Max LeBlanc, tight end
Designation:
Out
LeBlanc has played 73 snaps this season, including 10 at Indiana.
