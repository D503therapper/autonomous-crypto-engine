Sam Houston (1-3, 0-0 CUSA) is proof that records can be deceiving this early in the season. The Bearkats are flying under the radar after a strong non-conference showing — at least compared to last year’s 2-10 product. Sam Houston fought reigning Sun Belt runner-up Troy down to the wire, was five yards away from upsetting Tulsa, and battled Texas Tech for a half. There are multiple reasons for the Bearkats’ improvement. One is the emergence of Landyn Locke. In his first three starts this season, Locke threw for 335, 316, and 322 yards. Those are the three highest passing total since Sam Houston joined the FBS in 2023. The receiving corps is strong, and the Bearkats are improved on defense. NC State transfer outside linebacker Elijah Groves has reinvigorated the pass rush with 2.5 sacks and two forced fumbles, while cornerback Ishmel Atkins has been a force in the secondary with five pass breakups.
Liberty (4-1, 1-0 CUSA) is on a tear right now. The Flames stumbled in their first two matchups, scoring just 13 against James Madison and requiring a late comeback to survive FCS Gardner-Webb. But in the three games since, Liberty looks exactly like a CUSA contender. Jamey Chadwell’s team has fonud its groove on offense, getting things done in both facets. Deshawn Purdie has seven touchdown passes and zero interceptions, while his No. 1 target Makai Jackson has been the definition of explosive — generating a 50-yard reception in all four games of the win streak. Kam Davis has hit his stride in the run game with 313 rushing yards across the last two weeks. And most impressively, the defense has been dominant. Liberty has shut out opponents in seven of the last 12 quarters, and two of those games were on the road.
