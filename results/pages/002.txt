Notre Dame football gets an early Christmas present with some incredibly positive injury updates
Notre Dame HC Marcus Freeman met with the local media on Tuesday. The Fighting Irish could have some reinforcements on the way.
Ryan Roberts
—
National College Football Writer
Nov 16, 2026 2:21 PM EST
Notre Dame offensive lineman Charles Jagusah on the field during warm ups before a NCAA football game against Michigan State at Notre Dame Stadium on Saturday, Sept. 19, 2026, in South Bend.
Notre Dame is starting to get some encouraging news on the injury front, and while there are still plenty of questions about who will be available moving forward, the latest developments are reasons for optimism.
The biggest news involves
offensive lineman Charles Jagusah
, who is back practicing with the team this week after a difficult recovery process involving a humerus injury and an infection. There is no definitive timetable for his return to game action, but simply seeing him back on the practice field is a significant step in the right direction.
There are also some positive developments on the defensive side, including a potentially important return for star safety
Brauntae Johnson
.

Jagusah suffered a serious humerus injury in an ATV accident during the summer of 2025. His recovery has been complicated by setbacks, including an infection, and the process has kept him off the field for an extended period. There have been moments when his progress offered hope, but getting back to football activities has been a long road.
Marcus Freeman has continued to express optimism about Jagusah’s recovery. Back in October, Freeman discussed the progress he was making in the weight room, particularly with his biceps and triceps. Now, he’s taking another important step by getting back into practice.
Freeman was clearly excited about the development when discussing Jagusah on Monday.
“His journey has been amazing. I’m so excited to see that guy in practice this week.”
It’s important not to get ahead of ourselves here. Practicing doesn’t mean Jagusah is ready to play, and there isn’t a definitive timetable for his return to game action. He still has to work his way back into football shape and demonstrate that he can handle the demands of playing offensive line.
However, this is a significant development for a player who has endured so much adversity.
Jagusah was viewed as a potential high-level offensive line prospect before his injury. Getting him back into the mix, even if it takes additional time before he’s ready for a game, would be a tremendous development for Notre Dame.
The most important thing right now is that he’s making progress and getting back to doing what he loves.
Brauntae Johnson could be nearing his return
Notre Dame could also be getting one of its most important defensive players back in the lineup.
Safety Brauntae Johnson has been upgraded to questionable on the latest availability report ahead of Saturday’s matchup against SMU. It’s the first time he’s appeared in the questionable category after being sidelined with a left foot injury suffered in the season opener against Wisconsin.
That’s a significant development.
Johnson entered the season with preseason AP Second-Team All-American recognition and was expected to be a major piece of Notre Dame’s secondary. His absence has been felt, particularly with the Irish having to adjust their safety rotation and defensive personnel.
Freeman previously indicated that Johnson was expected to return sometime in November. Now, with the calendar reaching the second half of the month and Johnson officially listed as questionable, there’s legitimate reason to believe his return is getting closer.
He will be integrated back into practice, and the coaching staff will evaluate how his foot responds.
I’m not ready to assume Johnson will play against SMU, but this is exactly the type of development Notre Dame wanted to see. Getting a player of his caliber back for the stretch run would be a significant boost to the defense.
More questions remain along the offensive and defensive lines
There are a few other injury situations worth monitoring this week.
Left tackle
Will Black
is listed as questionable after suffering a concussion against Boston College. That’s obviously something Notre Dame will need to handle carefully, especially given the importance of his position.
Black has been a key part of the offensive line, and his availability is worth watching as the Irish prepare for SMU. The team will need to follow the appropriate concussion protocols before considering a return.
On the defensive line,
Francis Brewu
and
Jason Onye
remain questionable with knee injuries.
Both players have been out since suffering their respective injuries against Navy. Brewu injured his left knee, while Onye is dealing with a right knee injury.
I still have my doubts that we will see either player against SMU.
Both have now missed multiple weeks, and Notre Dame shouldn’t rush either one back before they’re ready. The defensive line has had to lean on its depth in their absence, and that could continue for another week.
That said, seeing both players remain in the questionable category is better than having them ruled out. There’s at least some possibility that they’re getting closer to returning.
The timing couldn’t be much better for Notre Dame
Notre Dame is entering an important stretch of the season, and getting healthier is going to be critical.
The Irish have dealt with several significant injuries throughout the year, particularly on the defensive side of the football. Losing Johnson, Brewu and Onye has forced the coaching staff to adjust its personnel, while the absence of Jagusah has kept an experienced offensive lineman away from the program’s on-field rotation.
Not every player listed above is guaranteed to return this weekend, and Jagusah’s situation is still more about long-term progress than an immediate game-day possibility.
But there’s a difference between hoping players will eventually get healthy and actually seeing them take steps toward returning.
That’s where Notre Dame is right now.
Johnson appears to be getting closer, Jagusah is back practicing, and Black, Brewu and Onye all have a chance to work their way back.
The Irish don’t need to get everybody back this week, but having more healthy bodies available as the season moves toward its most important games would be a welcome development.
For Jagusah in particular, seeing him back on the practice field is a victory in itself.
