Nebraska could be without one of its top offensive weapons Saturday against Maryland, as running back
Jamal Rule
is listed as doubtful on the Huskers’ latest availability report.
Rule has emerged as a major piece of Nebraska’s offense early this season. His status will be one to watch leading into kickoff, especially after the freshman’s strong start through the first month of the year. A new availability report will be published Thursday, Friday and Saturday.
