Stewart
was suspended after punching an Alabama player in the helmet Week 4 and missed last week's game vs Kentucky. Though
he's back
practicing with the program, Beamer has not said if he will play vs Florida. He was listed on the injury report for the Kentucky game as out.
Three South Carolina starters are on the injury report Oct. 9. Edge rusher Julian Walker and defensive lineman Gabe Brownlow Dindy and offensive lineman Carter Miller were listed as out.
In a positive update, wide receiver Mazeo Bennett Jr was removed from the report.
The Gamecocks (2-3, 0-3 SEC) play at No. 15 Florida (4-1, 2-1) on Oct. 10 (12:45 p.m. ET, SEC Network).
