The second Big Ten-mandated availability report has been released for both the
Michigan Wolverines
and Michigan State Spartans ahead of Saturday night’s rivalry tilt at the Big House.
6 questionables from Wednesday’s report have been upgraded to probable: S Rod Moore, S Jordan Young, EDGE Dom Nichols, OL Brady Norton,EDGE John Henry Daley. C Jake Guarnera is also probable. TE Hogan Hansen and CB Shamari Earls, who is a new addition to the report, are questionable.
The Spartans have been hit with a rash of injuries with 13 players listed as out for Saturday.
