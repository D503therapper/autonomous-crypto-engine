Boise State football fans got a mixed bag of news Wednesday night when the first
Pac-12 availability report
of the week was released.
A couple of big names were removed from Boise State's report ahead of Saturday's game at Fresno State, but a few key players will also still be watching from the sideline.

Here's the report as of Thursday afternoon:
QUESTIONABLE:
WR Rasean Jones
LB Sam Brooks
DL Deuce Alailefaleula
OUT:
WR Cam Bates
LB Logan Brantley
WR Darren Morris
OL Carson Rasmussen
EDGE Jaden Williams
Starting with the positive for Boise State: Junior running backs Sire Gaines and Dylan Riley look set to play this weekend. Gaines had missed the last two games with a hand injury, while Riley suffered an injury late in last week's game against Utah State and was questionable heading into the week.
"Those are two huge parts of our offense, huge parts of our team, and so having them be able to go out there and compete on Saturday is a huge piece for us being successful," Boise State head coach Spencer Danielson said on Thursday.

However, the situation is less optimistic in the wide receivers room.
Freshman Rasean Jones is Boise State's leading receiver this year, with 359 yards and a touchdown off 23 receptions. Jones appeared to suffer a head injury last week against Utah State. While Danielson wouldn't provide specifics, he said Jones practiced Thursday, and the team will make a decision on his availability Friday.
Along with the out designation for receivers Cam Bates and Darren Morris, Boise State may have to lean heavily on sixth-year receiver Ben Ford and senior Akeem Wright this weekend. It's also a chance for some of the team's younger receivers, like sophomore Qumonte Williams Jr., to earn some more snaps.
"The great teams have the depth to be able to step up and make sure the standard does not change. We've got a great receiver room," Danielson said. "… There are some receivers in our room that haven't played a ton. They're going to be asked to play more, and that's the reality of where we're at."
