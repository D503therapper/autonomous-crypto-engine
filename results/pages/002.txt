CORVALLIS —
Oregon State
's offensive line, which has been dangerously thin at times during the first half of the season, is expected to be nearly fully healthy coming out of the bye week.
"We got some time to lick our wounds and get back to refining our technique," center Van Wells said. "We've been anxious to play again."

Josiah Timoteo
and backup interior lineman
Carter Guillaume
, who each suffered foot injuries in the season opener, played against Washington State and could see an increased workload on Saturday at Fresno State.
Timoteo played sparingly against Montana and Colorado State and missed the games with UTEP and San Diego State. His absence allowed Jacob Anderson to start the last four games at left tackle and he's played well.
Timoteo split the reps at right tackle with Teko Shoats against WSU, leaving an open question as to who will start this week.
