The Big 12 Conference released its initial availability report Monday night ahead of Texas Tech’s Thanksgiving Day matchup against TCU, with 12 Red Raiders appearing on the report. With a trip to the Big 12 Championship on the line, the availability of several key contributors on both sides of the ball remains in question for Texas Tech heading into its regular-season finale.

Quinten Joyner
, wide receiver
and safety
