PHILADELPHIA –
The University of Pennsylvania football team (2-2, 0-1 Ivy League) returns to Franklin Field Friday night to host Columbia (3-1, 0-1 Ivy League) in its second Ivy League contest of the season. Kickoff is set for 7 p.m. on ESPNU. Eric Frede (play-by-play) and Jack Ford (color) will be on the call.
The Quakers are coming off a thrilling 24-20 comeback victory over Howard last Saturday, rallying in the fourth quarter to secure their second win of the season. Columbia enters Friday's matchup following a 41-28 road victory over Marist, scoring 20 unanswered points in the fourth quarter to close out its non-conference schedule.
