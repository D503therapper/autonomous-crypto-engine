TCU travels to Baylor Saturday facing key injury questions, a struggling offense and mounting pressure on Sonny Dykes to deliver the Horned Frogs' first Big 12 victory.

FORT WORTH - TCU football heads into Saturday night's rivalry showdown against Baylor with something considerably more important than bragging rights at stake. The Horned Frogs desperately need a victory, and an uncertain injury situation could complicate their attempt to salvage a disappointing season.
Sonny Dykes' team enters the 122nd meeting between the longtime rivals at 2-3 overall and 0-2 in Big 12 play. Baylor, meanwhile, is riding a four-game winning streak, sitting at 4-1 and undefeated in conference competition.

The latest injury updates provide TCU with some encouraging news.

Tight end Kari Ashley and wide receiver Jeremy Scott were both listed as probable in Wednesday's Big 12 availability report, potentially giving quarterback Jaden Craig two important offensive options.
Kicker Kyle Lemmermann remains a question mark, while receiver F. Fierro and linebacker M. Short are among the Horned Frogs listed out in separate injury reporting.
Baylor has its own personnel problems.
Defensive lineman Devonte Tezino and wide receiver Ashton Jones were listed out in Wednesday's report. Safety Cole Redding has also been sidelined by a season-ending knee injury, while receiver T. Williams Jr. has been dealing with a collarbone injury.
Those absences could influence a matchup featuring two offenses heading in opposite directions.
TCU managed just 10 points in its Oct. 3 loss to BYU despite a defensive performance that kept the Horned Frogs within striking distance throughout the afternoon.
Craig threw for a season-low 187 yards against the Cougars, a significant drop from his previous production.
