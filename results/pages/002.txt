MIAMI, Fla. –
The Atlantic Coast Conference released its initial injury report on Wednesday night and the No. 4
Miami Hurricanes
are close to regaining much-needed bodies and depth ahead of this Saturday's prime-time clash against
Clemson
.
As expected, sophomore tight end Luka Gilbert (undisclosed), redshirt junior running back CharMar "Marty" Brown (right knee), and sophomore wide receiver Joshua Moore (right shoulder) have been ruled out – the case for the rest of the season.

Cornerbacks junior Romanas "OJ" Frederique Jr. (left shoulder) and Ja'Boree Antoine (undisclosed) are ruled out for the weekend. Both defenders are expected to return at a later portion of the regular season. Antoine has yet to play and Frederique Jr. has been sidelined since the first drive against Stanford.
Newly added to the list, redshirt junior tight end Owen Ruskavich (undisclosed) has been ruled out. He's logged a new collegiate career high, playing 26 snaps this season. Ruskavich didn't appear in last Saturday's 52-3 win over Central Michigan at Hard Rock Stadium.
Junior cornerback Xavier Lucas (undisclosed) is listed as questionable. Head coach Mario Cristobal described a return this weekend as "possibly." Lucas will likely be a game-time decision. The Hurricanes' secondary has run thin over the last three weeks; a return would add much-needed depth.

Sophomore linebacker Ezekiel Marcelin Jr. (undisclosed) is listed as questionable. He has shown promising signs this season. Marcelin Jr. is one of nine Hurricanes defenders with at least 10 tackles, including three solos and seven assists, a tackle for loss, and a forced pressure.
Seniors running back Mark Fletcher Jr. and defensive tackle Ahmad Moten Sr. didn't play against Central Michigan with prior injuries and sidelined for precaution. Neither is listed in the initial injury report.
