Some significant developments have taken place on Penn State's availability report ahead of Saturday's game against Michigan.
Running back Carson Hansen and cornerback Josiah Zayas are off the report after being listed as probable Wednesday, while wide receiver Chase Sowell and linebacker Cam Smith are now listed as probable.
Smith was listed as questionable on Wednesday. His upgrade is good news for the Nittany Lions after Matt Campbell announced linebacker Alex Tatsch is out for the season on Wednesday.
Meanwhile, Penn State's starting running back appears good to go against the Wolverines.
Sowell, the team's leading receiver, is now on the report. He went to the locker room early against USC before the half with what he said was a shin injury.
Defensive tackle Armstrong Nnodim and defensive end Yvan Kemajou are still listed as probable, while kicker Cristiano Rosa was downgraded to out.
