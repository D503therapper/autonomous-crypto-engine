TITLE: FOOTBALL PLAYS AT BALL STATE ON SATURDAY AFTERNOON - Sacramento State
DATES SEEN: ['Oct. 17, 2015', 'Oct. 24, 2025']

A BRIEF PREVIEW
Sacramento State will visit another new team as the Hornets play at Ball State will meet for the first time on Saturday, Oct. 24, in Muncie, Ind. The game kicks off at 12:30 p.m. PT from Scheumann Stadium.
This week's game will be broadcast on ESPN+. Joe Tordy will serve as the play-by-play announcer with former Florida State offensive lineman Forrest Conoly as the analyst.
Hornet fans can tune in to ESPN 1320 AM to hear the radio call of Jason Ross (play-by-play), Steve McElroy (analyst) and Danny Sullivan (sideline). Live audio streaming is also available via www.hornetsports.com and the Audacy app.
STORYLINES
Sacramento State suffered a 48-7 Homecoming loss to Ohio on Saturday. The defeat dropped the Hornets to 1-6 overall and 0-4 in MAC games.
The game against the Cardinals will be the first in Indiana in program history. Indiana will become the 19th state in which the Hornets have played a football game and the third new state this season (Michigan and Ohio). Sacramento State will add a 20th state to its resume when it ends the year at Hawaii.
A week after playing a 3-4 defense at Bowling Green, Sacramento State reverted back to a base of 3-3-5 against Ohio. In the formation, the Hornets returned to three safeties while
Kenwick Thompson
remained the defensive play caller.
The initial change to the formation was dictated by a rash of injuries which led to the team being without its three backup safeties against the Minutemen. Two weeks ago against the Falcons,
Caleb Reese
made the move from corner to safety and responded with a career-high seven tackles.
