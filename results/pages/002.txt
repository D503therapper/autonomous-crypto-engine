Kansas sophomore quarterback Isaiah Marshall is listed as probable, while last week's starter, Cole Ballard, remains questionable for Saturday's game against UCF, according to Thursday night's Big 12 football injury report.
Marshall — he missed last week's loss to TCU because of a lower-leg injury — and Ballard (undisclosed injury) both were listed as questionable in Wednesday's first injury report of the week.
Third-string KU quarterback Chase Jenkins (undisclosed injury) also was upgraded from questionable to probable Thursday night.
KU linebacker Bam Crouch and defensive back Syeed Gibbs are listed as questionable, while receiver/kick returner Bryson Hayes is doubtful.
For UCF, quarterback Alonza Barnett III is listed as probable. Fellow quarterback Keyone Jenkins is out for the game.
Kickoff will be 2:45 p.m. Saturday at David Booth Kansas Memorial Stadium.
