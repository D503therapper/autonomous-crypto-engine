Iowa and Washington don't have an extensive history, having played only seven times since the Huskies joined the Big Ten Conference. Iowa holds a slim advantage with a 4-3 record, including a 40-16 win in October 2024 at Kinnick Stadium.
Early predictions
Sep 5, 2026; Iowa City, Iowa, USA; Iowa Hawkeyes quarterback Jeremy Hecklinski (10) looks to make a pass against the Northern Illinois Huskies during the third quarter at Kinnick Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Jeffrey Becker-Imagn Images | IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect
Both teams are coming off losses, and Iowa is a slight underdog in this road matchup. Washington has played Minnesota and USC very closely over its past two games, but hasn't done enough offensively to pull out a win. The Huskies averaged 299.0 total offensive yards, with the outlier coming against a suspect USC defense last week when they put up 319 total yards. Washington has been far better passing (221.0 yards per game) than running (77.5 yards per game) over the past two, which plays into Iowa's hands. Iowa has been slightly better at stopping the pass than the run, and has another opportunity to do that against Washington.
Iowa's defense has been exhausted from being on the field so often, but they haven't been as elite as in previous seasons. That's because the defensive line hasn't been getting as much pressure, but that started to shift last weekend. Phil Parker sent more blitzes and mixed in more players on the line. Ohio State quarterback Julian Sayin got what he wanted, but Iowa did pressure him. Iowa's main issues are on offense, and it will be interesting to see what happens if starting QB Hank Brown can't play. Jeremy Hecklinski looked good against Ohio State in limited duty, and might be forced to plkay again. Ferentz has expressed confidence in Hecklinski, and he could give the offense an edge.
The rushing game has struggled over the past two games, and this is a good game to get back on track. With Brown sidelined, the quarterback run game will come into play as Hecklinski throws for two scores and runs for another. The defense also gets back on track and stifles the Washington running game en route to a win.
