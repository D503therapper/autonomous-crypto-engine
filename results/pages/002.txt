The defense is where Sam Houston shines. The Bearkats rank third in all of FBS in passing yards allowed, although they have struggled against the run, ranking 129th. With three one-score losses this season in games they easily could have won, Sam Houston has shown it can stay competitive as an underdog.
Missouri State Defense Ranks Among the Worst
Missouri State enters off a 61-38 loss to Delaware, a game that featured 99 combined points. The Bears are 2-5 overall and 1-2 in conference play, and their only two wins came against Kennesaw State and FCS Lindenwood.
Missouri State has kept some games close, losing by six to Marshall and by 10 to SMU at the end of September, but it was also blown out at Western Kentucky and rolled by Texas A&M.
The defense is the biggest concern. Missouri State ranks 130th in FBS in points allowed per game, and the Bears have given up at least 30 points in every one of their losses. In their two wins, they allowed 21 and 14 points.
Offensively, Missouri State is serviceable, and its biggest strength is throwing the ball. Unfortunately for the Bears, that plays directly into the strength of a Sam Houston defense that ranks third in the country against the pass.
Missouri State does have home-field advantage, but laying close to a touchdown with a defense this vulnerable is a tough ask. If Sam Houston’s offense can find any rhythm against this unit, covering the number becomes very difficult for the Bears.
The total has dropped from the 50.5 to 51.5 range down to 49.5.
Current public betting data shows 86% of the spread money on Missouri State.
On the total, 78% of the money and 75% of the bets are on the Over.

ATS and Total Picks: Sam Houston vs Missouri State
Sam Houston has proven it can compete as an underdog, and its pass defense matches up perfectly against what Missouri State does best on offense. On the other side, a Missouri State defense that ranks 130th in points allowed gives a struggling Bearkats offense its best opportunity in weeks to find some production. Missouri State may grind out a win at home, but laying 5.5 points with this defense is not appealing. Take the points with Sam Houston.
The total is less appealing, but there is a slight lean to the Under 49.5. Sam Houston’s defense should do a good job of keeping Missouri State’s passing attack in check, and the Bearkats’ offense has had trouble producing points all season.
