THURSDAY Ole Miss vs. Auburn Injury Report: Crawford, Davis Still Questionable; Lacy Out
Posted by
Evelyn Van Pelt
|
,
Ole Miss
OXFORD, Miss. –
The second SEC availability report of Auburn week is out, and nothing has changed for No. 12 Ole Miss.
Cornerback
Jalyn Crawford
and left tackle
Terez Davis
remain questionable on the Thursday report, released at 7:10 p.m. CT. Running back
Kewan Lacy
is still out after dislocating his shoulder in last week’s loss at Texas.
Auburn’s report is also unchanged from Wednesday, with wide receiver Jeremiah Koger still questionable.
Ole Miss (5-2, 3-2 SEC) hosts Auburn (4-3, 2-3 SEC) on Saturday, Oct. 31, at 11:45 a.m. CT at Vaught-Hemingway Stadium on SEC Network.

What changed from Wednesday?
Nothing. Both teams’ lists are identical to the
Wednesday Ole Miss vs. Auburn injury report
. Ole Miss still has 10 players out, two questionable and three probable, and Auburn has two players out and one questionable.
That means the biggest questions for the Rebels will carry into Friday’s report and possibly all the way to Saturday morning.
Jalyn Crawford still questionable
Crawford’s status is the one Ole Miss fans will watch most closely. The cornerback intercepted Arch Manning twice at Texas, including one early in the fourth quarter right after the Rebels tied the game 24-24. Pete Golding said Monday that Crawford “played his best game” in Austin and is “starting to show who we thought he was.”
The Ole Miss secondary is already thin. Safeties Edwin Joseph and MJ Preston are out again, along with cornerbacks Cedrick Beavers and Iverson McCoy and safety Nascar McCoy. If Crawford can’t play, more could fall on Jaylon Braxton, who has given the Rebels flexibility at both corner and safety, and Nick Cull, who has two interceptions this season.
That matters against an Auburn offense led by dual-threat quarterback Byrum Brown, the FBS’s active leader in career total offense.
Terez Davis still questionable
Davis missed the Texas game after being a game-time decision. Golding sounded confident Monday that the left tackle would be back this week.
“So that’s one definitely that we feel like, throughout this week, that we’ll get back for this weekend,” Golding said.
Even without Davis, the Ole Miss line did not allow a sack at Texas, with Connor Howes sliding to left tackle and Tommy Kinsler starting at right tackle. Howes and center Brycen Sanders are both listed as probable again. The Rebels have allowed just five sacks all season, the fewest in the SEC, while Auburn has allowed 30, the most in the conference.
Kewan Lacy out, Makhi Frazier steps in
Lacy’s status was never in doubt this week. Golding said Monday there was “no chance” Lacy would play after he dislocated the same shoulder that has bothered him all season.
Read more on Lacy’s injury and the running back plan.
Makhi Frazier, who leads the team at 5.9 yards per carry and ran for 101 yards against Missouri, is expected to lead the backfield, with Shekai Mills-Knight also getting more work.
When is the next Ole Miss injury report?
The Friday availability report comes out at 7:10 p.m. CT. The final report is due about 90 minutes before Saturday’s 11:45 a.m. kickoff, around 10:15 a.m. CT.
