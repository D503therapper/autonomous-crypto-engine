Alabama Football issued its first availability report of the week on Wednesday evening.
Right tackle
Jayvin James
, who is dealing with a lower-extremity injury, is questionable for the Week 5 game at Mississippi State. That matches what Alabama head coach
Kalen DeBoer
said on Monday. James did not finish the Crimson Tide’s 49-18 win over South Carolina, walking to the locker room with a boot on his left foot.
Tyrell Miller
Justin Hill
and
Desmond Umeozulu
remain out. Both edge rushers suffered lower-extremity injuries against Florida State in Week 3 and missed last week’s game. Hill was on the sideline with a walking boot on his left foot and used crutches to move around. Umeozulu was not present. To kick off MSU game week, DeBoer offered the latest on the Wolves.
“Both of them will continue to miss some time,” DeBoer said. “Justin will not be as long. Des will be a little further into the season. … Those guys obviously played valuable snaps for us. Gonna get some other guys to play some snaps that haven’t got as many reps, and other guys that are on the field played more snaps.
“So, just it’s unfortunate for those guys because they’re playing some good football for us.”
The good news for Alabama is left guard
William Sanders
was not listed on the initial report. He left the South Carolina game with an upper-body injury and did not return. But he is good to go.
The No. 8 Crimson Tide (4-0, 2-0 SEC) will travel to play No. 16 Mississippi State (4-0, 2-0) on Saturday, Oct. 3, in Starkville, Miss. The conference matchup kicks off at 11 a.m. CT (on ABC).

SEC Availability Report Policies
Beginning with the 2024-25 athletics season, all Southeastern Conference schools will provide public reports on the availability of student-athletes to participate in each conference game in the sports of football, men’s and women’s basketball and baseball.
Prior to a Saturday football game, an institution must issue the Initial Report on Wednesday by 7 p.m. CT and update the Availability Report once per day on Thursday and Friday (after the conclusion of practice and before 7 p.m.). On game day, an institution must provide one Game Day Update no later than 90 minutes before game time.
Designated Participation Statuses
If an institution has knowledge that a student-athlete may not be able to participate in the upcoming game for any reason (including, but not limited to, injury, illness, suspension, ineligibility, or personal matter), the institution must accurately designate the student-athlete’s participation status on the Initial Report as follows:
– Out – Will not play / 0% chance to play
– Doubtful – Unlikely to play / 25% chance to play
– Questionable – Uncertain to play / 50% chance to play
– Probable – Probable to play / 75% chance to play
