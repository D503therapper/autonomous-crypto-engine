A preseason AP second-team All-American who has been sidelined since the season opener is now listed as questionable

SOUTH BEND, Ind. (WNDU) - One of Notre Dame’s top players on defense might return to the field for the first time since Week 1 when the Fighting Irish host SMU this upcoming Saturday.
According to the team’s latest availability report, safety Brauntae Johnson has been upgraded from out to questionable.
Johnson,
a preseason AP second-team All-American
, has been sidelined since injuring his left foot in
the season opener against Wisconsin
. Head coach Marcus Freeman said Monday that he will be integrated back into practice to see how his body responds and whether he has an opportunity to play against SMU.
Other Notre Dame injuries
Several other Notre Dame starters on both sides of the ball are either out or questionable for Saturday’s game.
Linebacker Drayk Bowen has been ruled out for the third consecutive game after suffering a right ankle injury
against Navy
. Cornerback DJ McKinney will miss his fourth consecutive game because of a right ankle injury suffered in
Notre Dame’s loss to BYU
.
Starting defensive linemen Francis Brewu and Jason Onye are listed as questionable for the third consecutive week. Neither has played since being injured against Navy. Starting left tackle Will Black is also questionable after suffering a concussion Saturday
against Boston College
.
Offensive linemen Charles Jagusah and Chris Terek are no longer listed on the injury report and will return to practice this week. Freeman said their return will be similar to Johnson’s, with the team evaluating how they respond in practice.
