The
Arkansas football team
is set to hit the road this weekend to play the Texas A&M Aggies at Kyle Field in College Station, Texas.
The SEC Student Athlete Availability Report was released for both teams on Wednesday night.
Introduced in 2024, availability reports will be updated daily ahead of Arkansas’ SEC contest on Saturday. The final update is provided 90 minutes before kickoff.
According to the SEC, student-athletes will be designated as “available”, “probable”, “doubtful” or “out” for their next game. For additional clarity on game day, student-athletes will be designated as “available”, “game-time decision” or “out.”
Below is the second availability report update of the week ahead of Arkansas’ game against Texas A&M, which will kick off from College Station at 6 p.m. CT on Saturday.
