The first availability report for Saturday’s Big Ten conference game between the Minnesota Golden Gophers and
Michigan Wolverines
was released on Wednesday night, and it already features a notable Gopher out for Saturday.
Starting center Ashton Beers, who was ruled out of last week’s game against Washington, has already been ruled out for Saturday’s game and as first reported by Andy Greder of the Pioneer Press, the rest of the season. Beers left Minnesota’s week three matchup against Akron with a lower leg/ankle injury before returning to the game.
Last week, the Gophers slid starting right guard Greg Johnson to center with Jaden Ball filling in at right guard.
Unfortunately for the Gophers, Jaden Ball is now listed as questionable for the game alongside starting defensive tackle Riley Sunram
.
No other player was listed as questionable for the game
. Listed as probable are running backs A.J. Turner and Zeke Bates, while defensive end Adam Kissayi is also probable
.
For Michigan, the Wolverines have listed key offensive tackle Blake Frazier as probable and defender John Henry Dale as questionable. Daley has recorded 16 tackles, 4.5 tackles for loss and three sacks this season in four games.
