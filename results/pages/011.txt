MIAMI — Vice Night is upon us. The fourth edition of the jerseys were released the Friday before the game, meaning it is time to open Conference USA play against the New Mexico State Aggies.
“I’m going to keep my thoughts to myself about the results of Vice Night historically, but we’ve got to find a way to turn that tide,” FIU coach Willie Simmons said. “Vice Night is always something special, always been a hit. This year’s uniform is definitely going to draw some attention, turn some heads and we’re always excited to see how the guys respond to it when they see it, and being able to see how it comes out on television.
