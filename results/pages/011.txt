South Carolina football
receiver Donovan Murph was upgraded to probable on the Nov. 5 SEC injury report for the Texas A&M game.
Murph was questionable on the initial report. Murph has 11 catches for 205 yards and three touchdowns.
Texas A&M leading rusher KJ Edwards was still listed as questionable. He has 360 yards and two touchdowns. Linebacker Noah Mikhail, the Aggies second-leading tackler with 36, was upgraded from questionable to probable.
The Gamecocks (5-3, 3-3 SEC) are home to face Texas A&M (4-4, 1-4) on Nov. 7 (3 p.m., ESPN) and on a three-game winning streak.
The reveal of the initial College Football Playoff poll on Nov. 3 highlights the importance of the game for South Carolina. The Gamecocks earned the No. 21 ranking in the initial poll.
