Notre Dame football injury report vs. BYU: Status of three defensive starters upgraded in Week 7
Kyle Kelly
@ ByKyleKelly
10/12/26
Share
Notre Dame football will have star linebacker
Kyngstonn Viliamu-Asa
and potentially two other key defensive starters available in its toughest test of the season to date on Saturday vs. No. 8 BYU.
On Monday, the Notre Dame football communications team removed Viliamu-Asa from the availability report after he missed last Saturday’s game vs. Stanford due to a left thumb injury. They also upgraded starting cornerback
Leonard Moore
and defensive tackle
Jason Onye
to questionable. Moore missed the Stanford game due to a concussion and Onye has been out since Week 2 due to a left ankle injury.
Meanwhile, a fourth defensive starter, junior safety
Brauntae Johnson
, will have to wait at least two more weeks before he returns to the lineup. The junior out of Fort Wayne was ruled out again due to the left foot injury he sustained in the first half against Wisconsin on Sept. 6.
Although the Irish upgraded Onye’s status and not Johnson — despite both injuries occurring in the same game — head coach
Marcus Freeman
said Johnson’s return to play progression as been as expected. The Irish look to get their preseason All-America safety back sometime next month.
“He’s exactly as we thought once we did the scans and did the surgery,” Freeman said. “We knew his recovery would be longer than Jason. You’re talking about a foot that (Johnson) previously injured. So, he’s right on the timeline that we expected. He’ll be November-ish when he’s ready to come back.
“Even though it was the same type of foot injury (to Onye), two different recovery timelines because one having a previous injury to that foot.”
In addition to Johnson, Freeman also provided an update on senior offensive linemen
Charles Jagusah
who has not played since the 2024 season due to a humerus injury in his left arm
.
It remains a possibility that Jagusah returns at some point this season.
“Absolutely,” Freeman said when asked if he continues to be hopeful Jagusah can play this season. “We have a developmental lift on Friday mornings and I was in there with those guys and I saw big Charles hang cleaning some good weight. He said that’s the first time he’s hang cleaned that much weight and there’s progress. He’s making progress in his bicep and tricep.
“I continue to remain hopeful, as does he. We just kind of got to trust the process. Like, keep working, keep working. When you’re dealing with nerves, you’re dealing with a traumatic injury like he had, it takes time. But as long as you continue to see progress, there’s a lot of hope. I hope we do see him this year.”
Along with Jagusah, three other players have been ruled out against BYU who have yet to suit up for a game this season: Senior offensive lineman
Chris Terek
(right knee), sophomore defensive lineman
Christopher Burgess Jr.
(right knee).
Also on Monday, the Irish listed freshman cornerback
Chaz Smith
as out for the season due to a left knee injury. Smith had missed the first five games of the season due to a right wrist injury suffered during fall camp.
Notre Dame listed no other players on the availability report. So, if they are on the depth chart, they should be available to play. On Sept. 28, Notre Dame ruled sophomore wide receiver
Quincy Porter
out for the season
due to a left knee injury
.
