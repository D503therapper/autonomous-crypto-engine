Penn State's top two receivers are listed as questionable for Saturday's game against Purdue, a positive and negative development for the Nittany Lions.
Chase Sowell, the team's leading receiver, is listed as questionable on the first Big Ten availability report of Week 9. It's the first time Sowell has been listed as questionable this season. He was a game-time decision before Penn State's 21-18 loss to Michigan and went on to catch 11 passes for a career-high 151 yards.

Penn State's other injury issues
Elsewhere, two defensive linemen are on the first availability report. Tackle Alijah Carnell is listed as questionable, while end Alex McPherson is questionable.

McPherson's status is one to watch, as he has grown his snap count over Penn State's last three games. Penn State is thin at defensive end without Max Granville and LaVar Arrington II, who is no longer listed as out. Arrington has not played this season after undergoing a procedure earlier this season.
Safety Jeremiah Cooper is probable after playing just seven snaps against Michigan. Campbell said that Cooper, a two-time all-Big 12 safety, had his workload reduced at Michigan after playing heavy snap counts in the preceding three games. Cooper sustained a torn ACL at Iowa State last season, and Campbell wanted to get him some rest entering the season's final five games.
Defensive linemen Yvan Kemajou and Armstrong Nnodim are listed as probable. Nnodim missed the season's first two games because of a training camp injury but has been a lead player at tackle since.
Safety Omarion Davis is a new Penn State player listed as out. Davis has yet to play this season.
