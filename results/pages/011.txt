The Big 12 availability report for Iowa State’s Homecoming matchup with West Virginia was released Wednesday night.
The Cyclones face the Mountaineers at 11 a.m. Saturday, the teams have given an initial update on the health status of some of its players.
Here is the full availability report from Wednesday, which can be
found on the Big 12’s website
beginning three days before each conference game.
Iowa State
OUT
Wide receiver Carter Pabst
Offensive lineman Caden Maas
Defensive end Caden Crawford
Defensive tackle Ka’Mori Moore

Cornerback Jaheim Singletary
West Virginia
OUT
Nickel/Safety Emory Snyder
