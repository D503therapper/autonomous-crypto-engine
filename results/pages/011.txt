DeSean Bishop, Donovan Haslam injured at South Carolina
DeSean Bishop appeared to suffer a lower-body injury late in Tennessee’s 27-24 loss at South Carolina two weeks ago and offensive guard Donovan Haslam left earlier in the game with an undisclosed injury.
Neither player has been listed on Tennessee’s SEC Availability Report this week. Head coach
Josh Heupel
addressed Bishop’s status during the open date last week.
“We feel like he’ll be in a good spot (against Kentucky),” Heupel said last Wednesday. “He got some work today, we limited him some today. Part of that was the ankle, but part of that’s just the volume that he’s gotten in the early part of the conference play, too.”
Seven Tennessee players out for the season
Offensive lineman
Wendell Moe Jr.
became the latest Tennessee player to be lost to a season-ending injury after suffering a lower-body injury against Texas. Seven Vols out for the season with injuries.
Sophomore wide receiver
Joakim Dodson
(knee) was lost to injury in the second half of the 56-9 season-opening win over Furman, senior tight end
Ethan Davis
(ACL) suffered a season-ending knee injury at Georgia Tech on Sept. 12 and sophomore defensive back
Dylan Lewis
was injured in the 42-9 win over Kennesaw State on Sept. 19.
Safety
Edrees Farooq
(knee), linebacker
Jaedon Harmon
(ACL) and corner
Jadais Richard
(upper body) were out before the start of the season. Farooq suffered his injury during preseason camp. Harmon tore his ACL during the first week of the preseason and Richard was hurt while training on his own in July.
