Big Sky Now: Cat-Griz preview, former Grizzly and former Bobcat break down the Brawl of the Wild
Share
Print
Cat-Griz week is here, and two former players who have lived the Brawl of the Wild break it down ahead of Saturday’s game in Bozeman.
by
BIG SKY NOW PODCAST
PODCAST
|
November 20, 2026 8:00 AM
Cat-Griz week is here, and two former players who have lived the Brawl of the Wild break it down ahead of Saturday’s game in Bozeman.
Luke Schmit sits down with Ryan Tirrell, a former University of Montana football player, and Rylan Oritt, a former Montana State football player, for a full look at the rivalry, from the nerves and the film room to the final whistle.
They talk about growing up in Montana watching Cat-Griz, going from tailgating as a kid to preparing to win it, and whether rivalry week is really “just another game” inside the building.
They also cover playing with emotion without letting it take over, what the end of the game feels like win or lose, and the memories that stick with them.
Along the way: the 2026 Grizzlies, last year’s Bobcat national championship run, Eli Gillman’s record-setting season, and Bobby Kennedy’s first year as head coach. Does the first drive set the tone again? Who settles in first once the emotion fades? Can the Bobcats keep their recent run in this series going, or do the Griz flip it? And do field position, turnovers, stopping the run and third down decide it?
