Malachi Toney injury update: Miami leading WR out vs. Notre Dame
Kyle Kelly
@ ByKyleKelly
11/05/26
on Saturday night against Notre Dame.
On Wednesday night, On3 college football insider
Brett McMurphy
reported that Toney will undergo surgery for an ankle injury sustained in practice earlier in the day. Toney’s status was initially deemed uncertain. But now he is expected to be out for the Notre Dame game and beyond. Kickoff is at 7:30 p.m. ET on NBC.
The University of Miami confirmed Toney’s injury after McMurphy’s
initial report
Wednesday afternoon.
“Malachi Toney sustained an injury during Wednesday’s practice and is currently undergoing further evaluation by the team’s medical staff,” Miami said in an update, according to McMurphy. “We will share additional updates as appropriate.”
Toney’s absence is a huge loss for the Hurricanes. The 5-foot-11, 193-pound sophomore is a Heisman Trophy candidate, ranking second nationally in receptions (73) and receiving touchdowns (13) and third in receiving yards (1,027).
According to McMurphy, the Hurricanes are expected to move sophomore
Daylyn Upshaw
Romanas “OJ” Frederique Jr.
and starting right guard
Max Buchanan
. Frederique has not played since suffering a shoulder injury Sept. 4 against Stanford. Buchanan has been out since injuring his knee Oct. 3 against Clemson.
Notre Dame will also be without multiple starters, too.
The Irish will not have linebacker
Drayk Bowen
Jason Onye
and fellow junior defensive lineman
Francis Brewu
, both due to knee injuries sustained last Saturday against Navy.
The Irish will provide another availability update at noon ET on Thursday and then again at approximately 6:30 p.m. Saturday. Miami has not provided an official availability update.
