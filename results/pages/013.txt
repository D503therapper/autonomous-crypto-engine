While the Spartans are suffering the loss of a ton of players, the most important category may be the questionable players, considering they have the best chance at playing in the game. The list of questionable players include: Tre Bell, Cam Stodghill, Dion Crawford, Brady Pretzlaff, Conner Moore, Eli Bickel and Cal Thrush.
Meanwhile, for Wisconsin, they have three players listed out, three players listed as questionable and three players listed as probable.
