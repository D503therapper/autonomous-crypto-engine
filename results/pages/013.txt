No. 1 Miami at No. 11 Notre Dame: How to watch, weather, injuries, odds, announcers and more
No. 1 Miami travels to South Bend on Saturday night to face No. 11 Notre Dame.
By
Patrick Previty
All-time series: Notre Dame leads 17-9-1
The Fighting Irish lead the all-time series 18-9-1, although the NCAA's vacation of Notre Dame's 2012 victory brings the official win total to 17.
Miami has won the last two meetings, including a 27-24 victory at Hard Rock Stadium in the 2025 season opener. The Hurricanes also defeated Notre Dame 41-8 in 2017, but they have not won in South Bend since a 31-13 victory in 1984. Notre Dame won the most recent meeting at Notre Dame Stadium, 30-27, in 2016.

Miami vs. Notre Dame: Key storylines
The Hurricanes earned the committee's top ranking for the first time since the CFP began in 2014. Notre Dame is No. 11, seven spots lower than where the AP top 25 had the Irish.
