Series vs the Bears: Tied 1-1
SPRINGFIELD, Mo. –
Sam Houston looks to get into the win column in CUSA play on Tuesday night when it closes out midweek play at Missouri State.
Kickoff is set for 7:00 p.m. CT and will air on ESPN2. Jason Ross Jr. and Tyoka Jackson will have the call from Plaster Stadium.
The game can also be heard on the Bearkat Sports Network with Jason Barfield and Brian Adams on the call. The BSN broadcast can be heard online on GoBearkats.com, the Bearkat Sports app and locally on KSHU 90.5 FM.
