The first availability report for the Miami Hurricanes game against the Clemson Tigers has been submitted to the ACC.
As I say every conference game:
Each ACC schools are required to share an availability report the week of kickoff to provide transparency on player status. Players are listed as:
Out (0% chance to play)
Doubtful (25%)
Questionable (50%)
Probable (75%)
Heading into Week 5, the Hurricanes will have a total of six players ruled out for Saturday night's game against the Tigers: Joshua Moore, CharMar Brown, Ja'Boree Antoine, O.J. Frederique, Owen Ruskavich, and Luka Gilbert.
The Hurricanes received some positive news on Monday after Mario Cristobal stated that defensive back Xavier Lucas will have a shot at playing Saturday after missing the last three games due to an injury in the Stanford game. Linebacker Ezekiel Marcelin Jr. is also listed as questionable, just like Lucas.
