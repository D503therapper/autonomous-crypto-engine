Liberty heads to the desert this weekend looking to keep its Conference USA championship hopes firmly in its own hands. The Flames have won seven straight games since dropping their season opener at James Madison, including a dominant 49-10 Homecoming victory over FIU this past Saturday, and remain unbeaten in conference play.
Standing in the way is a New Mexico State team that has been difficult to figure out. The Aggies are 3-5 overall and 2-2 in CUSA, having alternated wins and losses throughout much of the season. They are coming off a disappointing 24-0 shutout loss at home to Jacksonville State, but their recent history against Liberty suggests this matchup could be more competitive than the records indicate.
Liberty and New Mexico State will meet Saturday, Nov. 7, at Aggie Memorial Stadium in Las Cruces, New Mexico. Kickoff is set for 6 p.m. Mountain Time, 8 p.m. Eastern, on ESPNU.
Analyzing the Aggies
New Mexico State enters the matchup looking to rebound from its shutout loss to Jacksonville State. The Aggies had opportunities to make the game competitive, but turnovers and missed field goals proved costly as they were unable to get on the scoreboard.
