UCLA Bruins
The initial injury report for Week 6 has finally been released, and it’s a much more positive sign for UCLA than the last few we have seen.
OUT vs. Oregon
Mikey Matthews, Wide Receiver
Jonah Smith, Wide Receiver
Sam Yoon, Offensive Line
It’s not surprising to see some of these names listed
out against the Ducks
, as Mikey Matthews has been ruled out for the entire season already.
Oregon quarterback Dylan Raiola celebrates a touchdown by Oregon running back Dierre Hill Jr. as the Oregon Ducks face the USC Trojans on Sept. 26, 2026, at Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum in Los Angeles, California. | USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect
Oregon’s injury report listed quarterback Dante Moore as out for the game against the Bruins. That means UCLA will face off against backup
quarterback Dylan Raiola
in their toughest matchup of the season.
DOUBTFUL vs. Oregon
Ashton Sanders, Defensive Line
It’s been a quiet season so far for Ashton Sanders, as he’s only logged a handful of stats, including four total tackles and two solo on the season. The defensive line will have its hands full against Oregon’s run game, so you’d like to have all hands on deck against the Ducks.

QUESTIONABLE vs. Oregon
Tao Johnson, Safety
The most interesting bullet point on this initial injury report is Tao Johnson. He’s been listed as out for the past three games, but is finally getting an upgrade toward progress, as he is at least questionable to play against Oregon.
Nov 15, 2025; Waco, Texas, USA; Utah Utes safety Tao Johnson (5) in action during the second half against the Baylor Bears at McLane Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Chris Jones-Imagn Images | IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect
Main Takeaways From Injury Report
With the
extra week of rest
the Bruins got from the bye, I figured we’d see some progress towards being ready to go for Johnson, but his status still remains up in the air. For the weeks he has been sidelined, Ta’Shawn James has been an excellent replacement, as he has grabbed three interceptions this season already.
It will be interesting to see whether Johnson makes his return against Oregon. A final availability report will be released hours prior to kickoff, and you’d love to see Johnson elevated at some point between now and game time.

Another note to make with the short list of questionable players is the absence of
Aidan Mizell
. He’s been on the injury report lately, but he finally seems to be 100% (or at least close) against Oregon. We saw what he can do last week, and if he’s able to replicate it again this week, good things could be on the horizon.
Even prior to Week One, UCLA had been dealing with injuries. Overall, it seems like the Bruins will head into Week 6 with arguably the healthiest roster they’ve had all season.
