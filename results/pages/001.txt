Coming into Friday night's ACC matchup between the Louisville Cardinals and Florida State Seminoles, each team is trending in a different direction. Louisville is coming off consecutive three-point losses to Wake Forest and NC State, while Florida State is on a two-game win streak, including a 31-point win over Virginia last weekend.
Sep 16, 2022; Louisville, Kentucky, USA; ALouisville Cardinals cheerleader carries a flag down the field before the first half against the Florida State Seminoles at Cardinal Stadium. Florida State defeated Louisville 35-31. Mandatory Credit: Jamie Rhodes-USA TODAY Sports | USA TODAY Sports
The Cards are banged up in the secondary with Koen Entringer ruled out the past two games, as well as Antonio Watts being ruled out for the season. This week, there is hope for Entringer, but fellow Safety TJ Banks appears on the "questionable" list.

Cason Henry as a starting tackle is one to keep an eye on here. The Florida State run defense is 44th in the country, allowing 119.2 rush yards per game. Also, on the "probable" list is Anwar O'Neal. Fingers are crossed that both are good to go for Friday night, if not I'd imagine you will see Lance Robinson bounce out to tackle.

Keyjuan Brown is the most concerning on this list. During Coach Brohm's radio show on Tuesday night he alluded to Isaac Brown's strong game against NC State on the ground. Isaac carried the ball nine times for 125 yards and a score.
"Well, we need him to continue to showcase what he is all about every game, we need him to do so even more so coming up, we have a few injuries here and there at that position."
That quote would lead me to believe that Keyjuan is not 100% after the NC State game. Keyjuan rushed the ball 8 times for 88 yards but had two critical lost fumbles.
