SMU
and Syracuse released the initial availability report Thursday night ahead of Saturday’s contest in Dallas. Head coach Rhett Lashlee was excited to see his team get healthier following the bye.
“Football comes with nicks and bruises,” Lashlee said Tuesday. “So a lot of those guys hopefully are feeling better. Today’s ten days since we last played. So we’ve had ten days to somewhat still get work in and all that, but hopefully get a little healthier as we go into conference play.”

Tyren Polley

La’Modrick Spencer
, TE James Larson and DT
Jonathan Jefferson

, LB

New for the 2025-26 academic year
, ACC institutions will provide public reports on the availability of football, men’s and women’s basketball, and baseball student-athletes to participate in each Conference game.
Reports will identify when a student-athlete’s availability to participate in the upcoming contest is uncertain for any reason. This policy is intended to promote the well-being of student-athletes and institutional staff members, the protection of sensitive information, and the integrity of competition.
Schools will release reports at these times for conference matchups:
Initial Report two nights before the game, no later than 8:00 p.m. local time
where the game will be played (e.g., Thursday night for a Saturday game).
