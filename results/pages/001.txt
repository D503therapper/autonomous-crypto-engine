The 14th-ranked Tennessee Volunteers are set to host No. 6 Alabama in a Third Saturday in October clash this weekend in Knoxville. The game will be played at 3:30 p.m. ET on Saturday in Neyland Stadium.
Tennessee is 5-1 on the season and is coming off a road win against Arkansas last weekend. Alabama, meanwhile, is among the league’s best this season. The Crimson are 5-1 on the year with wins over Mississippi State, South Carolina, Florida State, and Kentucky. As of Wednesday night, the Vols are an 8.5-point underdog against the Crimson Tide.
Ahead of SEC games this fall, the conference will release updated availability reports on Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday nights. There will also be a report before kickoff on game days.
Here’s a look at the availability report for the Volunteers and the Tide on Thursday night:

Tennessee Notes
There are no changes on the Tennessee side from Wednesday to Thursday
Most of Tennessee’s “OUT” players are dealing with season-ending injuries, so there are no big surprises on that front for the Vols. Reports this season indicate that Wendell Moe Jr. is out for the season with an injury.
Tennessee quarterback Faizon Brandon is listed as questionable after getting banged up against Texas a handful of weeks ago. Brandon did not play against Arkansas last weekend and was listed as a game-time decision against the Razorbacks.

Alabama Notes
The biggest news for the Crimson Tide is that star wide receiver Ryan Coleman-Williams is listed as out. Through six games this season, Coleman-Williams has 415 yards and four touchdowns on 23 receptions. He currently leads the Crimson Tide in all three categories, though the receptions mark is tied with wide receiver Lotzeir Brooks.
Delgatty was downgraded from questionable to doubtful from Wednesday to Thursday. He is the Tide’s starting center.
No. 14 Tennessee (5-1) will host No. 6 Alabama (5-1) in a Top 15 Third Saturday in October clash this weekend at 3:30 p.m. ET in Neyland Stadium.
