Offensive tackle
Michael Fasusi
, who has played just 17 snaps this season and hasn't played since the season opener against UTEP, is listed as doubtful for the game according to the SEC Availability Report. That's at least an improvement over him being listed as out from the jump for the game against Georgia.
But guard
Eddy Pierre-Louis
, who came off the bench against Georgia but still saw plenty of playing time, is listed as questionable.
Running back
Xavier Robinson
is listed as out for the game.
Robinson has appeared in all four games so far for the Sooners (2-2, 0-1 SEC) but has just 13 carries for 50 yards so far. Robinson had 30 rushing yards in the season opener but hasn't rushed for more than nine yards in a game since.
Oklahoma is averaging just 99 yards per game on the ground.
The Sooners will also be without reserve cornerback
Jacobe Johnson
, who was injured in the 41-13 loss to the Bulldogs.
