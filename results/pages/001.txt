Luckily, the Bears have played their best football at home. Missouri State's big win over Lindenwood and last-second win over Kennesaw State highlight this, along with a last-minute defeat to Marshall.
"This is going to be a clash," Woods said. "They have one of the most efficient quarterbacks in the league. We certainly anticipate a fighting football team rolling in here."
Missouri State's pass defense will have to step up against Sam Houston. Quarterback Landyn Locke has thrown for 1,653 yards, which leads CUSA. The Bears rank 115th, out of 138 teams, in pass yards allowed this season. The Bears are surrendering 247 yards per game in conference games, just a shade under the season average of 250.1 yards.

Another area Missouri State will have to juggle is Sam Houston's secondary. The Bearkats rank No. 3 in all of college football in passing yards allowed. With injuries to many running backs, the Bears have relied heavily on the pass game, led by quarterback Henry Belin IV, over the last few games.
Wide receiver Isaiah Myers has been a breakout star for the Bears. He ranks No. 6 in the nation in total receiving yards. Last week, Myers went for 242 yards against Delaware. That's the highest mark in college football this year.

Myers will have his hands full against a top-tier secondary, which might leave Jmariyae Robinson, who has Missouri State's all-time receptions record, open for opportunities.
