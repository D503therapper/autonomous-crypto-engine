Gavin Oliver

During his Sunday evening teleconference, Clemson head coach Dabo Swinney provided an update on the status of starting cornerback Elliot Washington II, starting linebacker Jeremiah Alexander and starting right guard Easton Ware.
All three of those players were out for last Friday’s 20-10 win at Duke.
Washington traveled and was a game-time decision for the Duke game, but did not dress out.
Swinney said Sunday that Washington is dealing with an upper-body injury, but the Tigers are “hopeful” that he’ll be able to return and play against the Gamecocks.
“He’s dealing with a little bit of an upper-body injury,” Swinney said. “But we’re hopeful that he’ll be able to return.”
Prior to being out for the Duke game, Washington had started each of the 10 previous games this season. He leads the team with five interceptions, which is also tied for second-most in the country.
Meanwhile, Swinney expects Alexander to be back in action against the Gamecocks.
Alexander had started all 10 games this season before missing the Duke game. He has tallied 56 total tackles, including 1.5 for loss, a half sack and a pass breakup.
“Expect Jeremiah to be back,” Swinney said.
Meanwhile, Swinney said they’ll have to wait and see on Ware’s availability for the South Carolina game.
“Not sure on Easton,” Swinney said.
Ware got banged up in the loss to Georgia Tech on Nov. 14. He had started the previous four games at right guard before missing the Duke game.
Saturday’s game against South Carolina at Clemson’s Memorial Stadium is slated to kick off at 3 p.m. and will be televised on ESPN2.
