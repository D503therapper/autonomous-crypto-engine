North Texas Mean Green Quarterback Tayven Jackson Feared To Have Season-Ending Upper-Body Injury
Matt Zenitz of CBS Sports reported that Jackson, who has 3,005 passing yards this season, faces more testing and will miss at least the Tulane game.
JR
By
Tulane Green Wave
after leaving the Mean Green’s 35-16 loss to the
UTSA Roadrunners
on Nov. 14 with an upper-body injury, CBS Sports’
Matt Zenitz
reported Sunday.
Zenitz reported that the injury is feared to be season-ending, with additional testing pending. He listed Jackson as out for at least the Tulane game.
Chris Jimerson Jr.
replaced Jackson early in the second half at the Alamodome in San Antonio. North Texas got within 21-16 before UTSA scored two late touchdowns.
Jackson was 10-of-20 for 125 yards with 1 interception before he left. The loss was North Texas’ first in American Conference play and dropped the Mean Green to 7-3 overall and 5-1 in the conference.
