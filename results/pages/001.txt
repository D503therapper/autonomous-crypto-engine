MIAMI AT WESTERN MICHIGAN (7 PM, ESPN2)
MRO Staff
These two teams met in last year's MAC Championship, and more than a few prognosticators predicted a return matchup.
While the Broncos have a good shot at returning, Miami's chances are slim. Chuck Martin's squad is on its fourth quarterback, with injuries taking out the first three. It's been a rough road for MU, and very close home losses to Bowling Green and Buffalo have put the RedHawks in a likely insurmountable hole. Profootballnetwork.com puts Miami's title game appearance changes at 2%.
To their credit, the RedHawks . . .
. . . have battled their way to seven wins and bowl eligibility. They have also to be a dangerous opponent. One week after a devastating loss to Buffalo, Miami overcame a 17-0 second half deficit to upset previously MAC-undefeated Ohio 34-17.
Western Michigan, meanwhile, is part of a four-way tie of one-MAC-loss teams. According to PFN.com, tie-breaker formulas allow WMU to control its own destiny, and it has a 63 percent chance of reaching Detroit for the Dec. 5 title game.
• Kent State ran 19 times for 31 yards (1.6 ypc).
• Miami ran 65 non-kicking plays, including 58 runs. The ground game totaled 301 yards (5.2 ypc) and provided three TDs.
• D'Shawntae Jones led all rushers, logging 29 carries for 121 yards with two TDs. It was his third straight week of eclipsing 100 yards.
• Freshman Drew Bellisari, who started the year fourth on MU's depth chart, got his first start following Caleb Heavner's injury against Ohio.
• In Miami's last 10 victories (dating back to Oct. 25, 2025), Bellisari is the sixth different RedHawk starting quarterback to earn a win. This season alone David McComb picked up two wins, Caleb Heavner had four and Bellisari won his first game as a starter last week versus KSU
• Bellisari completed 7-of-12 passes for 124 yards against Kent State. He was 1-of-1 after intermission as Miami rode its ground game success and drained the clock.
• MU registered 38:36 of clock time, while the Flashes finished with 21:24 in time of possession.
