sophomore quarterback Adam Schobel is expected to get his second start on Thanksgiving.
Senior quarterback Jaden Craig was ruled out for the Horned Frogs' (5-6, 3-5) regular season finale at Texas Tech at 7 p.m. Thursday on ESPN in the initial Big 12 player availability report released Monday night.
Tech (8-1) is No. 12 the College Football Playoff rankings, which will be updated Tuesday. The Red Raiders moved up to No. 9 Monday in the latest Associated Press poll.
Craig also missed TCU's 38-17 loss to No. 2 Utah last Saturday night and this likely marks the end of his TCU career unless the Horned Frogs reach a bowl game. The Harvard transfer threw for 2,664 yards, 13 touchdowns and seven interceptions in his first FBS season. Craig also rushed for 114 yards and four touchdowns in 10 games.
Schobel completed 15-of-31 passes for 106 yards, two interceptions and rushed for 28 yards against the Utes.
The Horned Frogs will be led by interim head coach and defensive coordinator Andy Avalos against the Red Raiders after TCU parted ways with Sonny Dykes on Sunday.
