Notre Dame injury report vs. SMU: Two major return possibilities emerge, key starter in doubt
Kyle Kelly
@ ByKyleKelly

On Monday, Fighting Irish coach
Marcus Freeman
confirmed to reporters that Jagusah is no longer listed as out on Notre Dame’s availability report ahead of Saturday’s game against SMU (7:30 p.m. ET | NBC). The 6-foot-5, 330-pounder has been sidelined since the start of the 2025 season due to a left humerus injury suffered in an ATV accident over the Fourth of July weekend about 16 months ago.
Jagusah’s potential return is one of two major developments for the Irish entering Week 12.
Notre Dame preseason All-America safety
Brauntae Johnson
is listed as questionable on the availability report for the first time this season. The 6-2, 200-pound Johnson has not played since the first half of the Wisconsin game due to a left foot injury.
Johnson hinted on his Instagram account
Sunday that he would return to the lineup this week.
The availability of three other Irish starters remains in question.
Starting defensive linemen
Francis Brewu
and
Jason Onye
also remain questionable. Brewu, a junior, and Onye, a sixth-year player, both sustained knee injuries Oct. 31 against Navy and have missed the last two games. Starting left tackle
Will Black
, who did not play in the second half last Saturday against Boston College, is also questionable due to a concussion.
Four other Irish players who have yet to suit up this season will also be unavailable against SMU. Sophomore defensive lineman
Christopher Burgess Jr.
(left tibia) and freshman
Tiki Hola
(right knee) remain out for the game but have a chance to return this season. Sophomore wide receiver
Quincy Porter
and freshman cornerback
Chaz Smith
remain out for the season due to left knee injuries.
In other encouraging news, senior offensive lineman
Chris Terek
has also been removed from the availability report and could make his season debut Saturday. He has yet to suit up in a game this season due to a right knee injury sustained in fall camp.
Will Charles Jagusah make his long-awaited Notre Dame return against SMU?
The answer to that question is more complicated than Jagusah simply being available to play. Although he is no longer listed on the availability report, neither Jagusah nor Terek appeared on Notre Dame’s two-deep depth chart Monday. Freeman is keeping expectations low for Jagusah’s return.
“His next phase is getting to practice,” Freeman said. “And start to return to play. Let’s see what he can do in practice. Same thing with Chris Terek. We took him off the injury report. This is the next phase for him.
“That doesn’t mean he’s going to go out there and start on Saturday, but that means he is actually practicing starting this week. That is a huge — that’s amazing for that young man that has been through so much to be able to be at the point now where he can return to practice. …
“If he can help us and we can find a role, how cool would that be to do that on Saturday? His journey has been amazing in terms of the things he’s overcome. I’m so excited to see that guy out in practice this week.”
Could Brauntae Johnson return to Notre Dame’s lineup against SMU?
Johnson’s situation is similar to Jagusah’s, with his availability for Saturday also uncertain. He did not appear on Monday’s two-deep depth chart despite starting at field safety in Week 1 against Wisconsin.
“He hasn’t been in practice,” Freeman told reporters in his Monday news conference. “He’s been training and rehabbing. We’re going to try to integrate him back into practice and see how his body responds. There are some football-specific movements that you do in practice that you can’t simulate and just rehab with Coach Landow (strength and conditioning coach
Loren Landow
) and (head athletic trainer)
Rob Hunt
.
“So, we’ll start to incorporate him Tuesday and see how he responds. If he responds well, get him in there Wednesday and see if there’s an opportunity for him this weekend to help us. If not, we’ll try to get him ready for the next one. This is the next phase of his progression of returning to play.”
