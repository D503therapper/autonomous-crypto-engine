Last Season:
Despite starting as pre-season favorites, the Longhorns would finish 10-3, including going 6-2 in the SEC, and would miss out on the College Football Playoffs. The Bulldogs would finish the season 5-8, but would still make a bowl game.
Series History:
The Longhorns lead the all-time series 4-2 and are currently on a three-game winning streak that dates back to 1998.
Meet the Coaches
Mississippi State Bulldogs head coach Jeff Lebby reacts during the second quarter against the Alabama Crimson Tide at Davis Wade Stadium at Scott Field. | IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect
Jeff Lebby, Mississippi State:
Lebby is in year three at the helm for the Bulldogs, and has amassed an 11-21 record over that time, but has continued to improve his record each year. He suffered a career-ending injury while playing for the Oklahoma Sooners.
Steve Sarkisian, Texas:
In his sixth season in the Forty Acres, Sarkisian boasts a 52-22 record and has led the Longhorns to back-to-back college football playoff appearances in 2023 and 2024. Tasked with bringing back the Longhorns program to its glory days, they entered the 2026 season as the preseason number five-ranked team in the AP poll. He previously served as an assistant at Alabama and played at BYU during his collegiate career.

What to Know About the Bulldogs
Unfortunately for the Bulldogs, star quarterback Kamario Taylor was ruled out for the rest of this season with foot surgery, harboring the outlook for the rest of the season. However, this isn't a new change for the program, as they have been without him for two weeks now.
That leaves freshman quarterback Parker Puckett back in the saddle against the Longhorns, and while he has shown signs of improving, he still has a ways to go. In the week eight loss to the Oklahoma Sooners, he went 17 for 24 with one touchdown and one interception.
His struggles have been heightened by a struggling offensive line, who surrendered four sacks against the Sooners, and are allowing sacks 8.54 percent of the time which ranks 110th in the country.
