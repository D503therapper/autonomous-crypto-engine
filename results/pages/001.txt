Ohio State added two more starters to the availability report for its game
against Maryland
on Oct. 10.
It listed linebacker Christian Alliegro and nickel back Jay Timmons as questionable on the latest report, filed two days ahead of kickoff.
Ohio State Buckeyes linebacker Christian Alliegro (14) reacts during the NCAA football game against the Kent State Golden Flashes at Ohio Stadium in Columbus on Sept. 19, 2026. Ohio State won 59-3.
Alliegro and Timmons joined wide receivers Brandon Inniss and Devin McCuin and right tackle Phillip Daniels as starters who could be out this week against the Terrapins.

Alliegro, a transfer from Wisconsin, has appeared in all five games and has made 20 tackles starting alongside Payton Pierce at linebacker.

Daniels has been out the last three games, while Inniss and McCuin have been available throughout the year as the leading receivers behind Jeremiah Smith. McCuin has caught 17 passes for 245 yards and two touchdowns, and Inniss has 10 receptions for 147 yards and one touchdown.
