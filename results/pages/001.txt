What does this mean for UVA?
Not being able to have Jekail Middlebrook would be a big blow to this team on Saturday. With the injuries they already have at the wide receiver position and the inconsistency of Beau Pribula and the rest of the unit, this makes pulling off an upset that much more difficult given how Wake Forest is playing.
Yesterday at his midweek press conference ahead of the game, UVA head coach Tony Elliott had a lot of
praise
for Wake Forest and how they are playing:
you saw that last year, and there's a lot of carryovers this year. But what you see is you see experience, especially on the defensive side, and man, the guys are playing fast, got a ton of
belief in what they're doing. They know the scheme, and they're confident, and they're
playing fast. They capitalize on mistakes, and then when you flip over to the offense, the quarterback just looks like a different guy.
He looks like he's free. He's playing inside the pocket, outside the pocket. He's running the ball, man. He's dishing it like a point guard. Then he's throwing it down the field. And so, what you see is you just see a ton of confidence in all three phases. And the guys are playing with a ton of energy, a ton of passion, and a ton of belief. And it's a good thing to see on tape. Like, as a coach, man, you always want to see that out of your team. And I see that out of my team at times, you know."
