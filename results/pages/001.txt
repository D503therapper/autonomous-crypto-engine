Injury Report: Wisconsin Safety Marvin Burks Questionable For Iowa
Evan Flood
@ Evan_Flood
10/29/26
MADISON, Wis. —
Wisconsin (5-2, 3-1) senior safety
Marvin Burks
is listed as questionable for Saturday’s road contest at Iowa (5-2, 3-2), according to the official injury report which was released on Wednesday.
Burks (upper body), who exited last week’s win over USC and did not return, has started all seven games this season for the Badgers. He’s recorded 16.0 tackles and two interceptions.
A new addition to the injury report this week, starting right guard
Barrett Nelson
is listed a doubtful, along with sophomore tailback
Nate Palmer
. Per a source, Nelson is dealing with an ankle injury and could miss a couple of weeks. He made his first career start last week against the Trojans.
Senior defensive lineman
Nolan Vils
is also listed as questionable. A key piece to the defensive line rotation, Vils as 11.0 tackles and a sack this season.
