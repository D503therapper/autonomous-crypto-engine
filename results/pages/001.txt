The new addition in the "OUT" column for Michigan State this week is linebacker
Caleb Wheatland
, who left the game against Wisconsin last week. He's been one of the main players filling in for some of the other injuries MSU has been dealing with elsewhere in the linebacker room, though it seems like things have stabilized a tiny bit there.
Those three names on the "questionable" line will be the big ones to watch this week.
Cam Fancher
still serves a real role in the Spartans' offense as a change-of-pace QB, cornerback
Tre Bell
Jordan Hall
is, of course, one of the team's star players.
Oct 3, 2026; Madison, Wisconsin, USA; Michigan State Spartans quarterback Cam Fancher (2) during the game against the Wisconsin Badgers at Camp Randall Stadium. Mandatory Credit: Jeff Hanisch-Imagn Images | IMAGN IMAGES via Reuters Connect
Fancher left the Wisconsin game with a leg injury, but it feels like good news for him to be in the "questionable" column right now. Bell played last week at Wisconsin, though it seemed to be a heavy dose of Charles Brantley and true freshman Jeremiah Favorite at corner as the game progressed. Hall was out last week against the Badgers.
Illinois has some notable players on their availability report, too. Ca'Lil Valentine has the most carries on the team (50), as well as 225 rushing yards this season. He's out for Saturday. DB Tywan Cox started for Illinois last week, and he's doubtful. Guard Champ Smith has started in each of his two appearances for the Illini this season. He's questionable.
