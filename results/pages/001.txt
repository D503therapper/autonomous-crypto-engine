Ethan Hampton Injury: Will miss multiple weeks
Written by
RotoWire Staff
Published on September 25, 2026
Hampton (hand) is expected to miss between four and six weeks due to a hand fracture, Pete Thamel of ESPN.com reports.
Hampton suffered what was initially deemed as a "pretty significant hand injury" in Week 3, and he'll be on the shelf for a considerable time as he works his way back from a fracture. Landry Lyddy will remain Southern Miss's starting quarterback as long as Hampton is out.
