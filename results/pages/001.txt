• QB
Keely Watson
has thrown for 1,368 yards and 11 touchdowns. Through eight games, he has completed 112 of 178 passes (63%) for 959 yards and three interceptions. Watson can stretch the field, highlighted by a 65-yard touchdown pass against Furman, and posted his best statistical outing against The Citadel, throwing for 245 yards and two touchdowns.
• Last time out - The Bulldogs improved to 6-2 (1-0) in MEAC play after using a strong defensive performance and two rushing touchdowns from Mason Pickett-Hicks to defeat Norfolk State 22-7 during Homecoming at Oliver C. Dawson Stadium on Willie Jeffries Field. The Bulldogs built a commanding 19-0 halftime advantage before relying on their defense to secure the conference-opening victory.
• RB
Mason Pickett-Hicks
(Sophomore, "MPH"): Pickett-Hicks leads the MEAC in rushing with 793 yards (No. 12 in FCS) on 108 carries and seven touchdowns. He ranks No. 2 in the nation, averaging an impressive 7.3 yards per carry, making him a key playmaker in the Bulldogs' ground attack.
