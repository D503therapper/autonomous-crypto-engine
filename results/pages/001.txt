We are less than 24 hours away from kickoff between Kentucky and South Carolina. That means it’s time for one more
SEC Availability Report
.
Each Wednesday of SEC contests, both schools will share an availability report. It will be updated nightly, with one final ruling an hour before kickoff on Saturdays. Players must be listed as out (0% chance to play), doubtful (25% chance to play), questionable (50% chance to play), or probable (75% chance to play).
On Thursday night, Kentucky fans learned that
DJ Waller
will not return for the SEC road contest, while South Carolina lost two starters on the offensive line. The Wildcats and Gamecocks will collide on Saturday at 7:45 p.m. ET (SEC Network) in Columbia. Here is the latest on each team’s health.
Kentucky Availability Report
DL Nic Smith: Out
WR David Washington Jr.: Out
WR Preston Bowman: Out
RB Jamarion Wilcox: Out
TE Elijah Brown: Out
CB DJ Waller Jr.: Out
QB Zach Calzada: Questionable
OL Aba Selm: Questionable
Mark Stoops did not make any changes to the injury report over the last 24 hours, however, it’s highly unlikely that
Zach Calzada
is able to play if Kentucky needs to break the glass in case of a quarterback emergency.
South Carolina Availability Report
OL Cason Henry: Out
OL Nolan Hay: Out
DB Buddy Mack III: Out
Texas A&M transfer
Gabriel Brownlow-Dindy
started the week as probable, but the interior defensive lineman has been taken off the availability report by Friday. Starting cornerback
Judge Collier
has not played since Week 1, but is now expected to play against Kentucky.
