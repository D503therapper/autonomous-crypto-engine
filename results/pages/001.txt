The latest injury report ahead of Minnesota’s Week 5 matchup against Michigan is out, and one day after losing starting center Ashton Beers for the season, the Gophers will now be without Week 4 starting left guard Jaden Ball.
After being listed as questionable on Wednesday’s initial availability report, Ball was ruled out on Thursday. The Golden Gophers, now without Ball, could turn to senior Jerome Williams to start on Saturday against the Wolverines.
Williams has fewer than 40 career offensive snaps, all of which came earlier this season against Eastern Illinois and Akron. Ball was forced into a starting role last week against Washington after Beers missed the game due to his now season-ending injury. If it’s not Williams, the Gophers could also choose to move tackle Bennett Warren inside or look towards a younger talent such as Nick Spence or Gavin Meier to play earlier than expected.
With Beers out, the Gophers moved potential All-American left guard Greg Johnson to starting center, leaving the left guard position open. Now, Minnesota will likely start Williams—their third-string left guard—on Saturday, alongside Johnson as their theoretical third-string center.
