Oregon State
will be without one of its more productive defenders against San Diego State.
Kord Shaw
is among four players listed as out on OSU's initial availability report this week. Shaw, who has 20 tackles with one pass breakup this season, has been mostly a situational inside linebacker behind Aiden Sullivan, Dylan Layne and Jabari Odoemenem.

Left tackle
Josiah Timoteo
, who has been coping with a foot injury since the season opener against Houston, is listed as questionable. Timoteo traveled to both UTEP and Colorado State but did not play, with Jacob Anderson starting in his place.
Receiver

, OSU's primary kick and punt returner, is listed as questionable.
