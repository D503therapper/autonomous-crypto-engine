California wide receiver Cooper Perry (undisclosed) and running back Adam Mohammed (undisclosed) are each questionable for the team's Week 7 matchup against Wake Forest. Mohammed has 336 rushing yards and three touchdowns across 73 rushing attempts in his five games played - he also has 17 receptions for 168 yards and a touchdown. Perry has played in six games, making eight receptions for 111 yards and a touchdown. Mohammed leads the Golden Bears' backfield, so if he cannot play, Ashten Emory will likely lead the way. Kyion Grayes (undisclosed) is another wideout who is questionable for California, so more could be put on the plates of Chase Hendricks and Ian Strong, the team's two leading pass catchers so far.

While it awaits word on wide receiver Vernell Brown III, Florida will have wide receiver Bailey Stockton (back) available against Texas on Saturday. Stockton is "good to go" for the Gators, according to ESPN's Pete Thamel, as they plan to start Tramell Jones Jr. at quarterback in place of an injured Aaron Philo. Stockton is Florida's third-leading receiver, recording 23 receptions for 258 yards and two touchdowns this season. Texas limited Oklahoma to just 155 yards passing a week ago. The Longhorns have allowed just 168.4 yards per game through the air this season.

With Oregon getting set to square off with Nebraska on Saturday night, the Ducks will start Brock Thomas at quarterback, according to On3's Pete Nakos. Texas Tech quarterback Thomas Castellanos was granted a temporary injunction in his fifth-year eligibility lawsuit, according to On3's Pete Nakos, making him eligible for the Red Raiders. "It clears the way for the 38 athletes listed as plaintiffs to play while the order is in effect, Nakos reported. Ole Miss starting safety Edwin Joseph (ankle) is out for the team's Week 7 matchup against Missouri. Over his first five games, Joseph had posted 18 combined tackles and also swatted down two passes. He transferred to Ole Miss from Florida State, where he played 28 games over three seasons and racked up 51 tackles, four interceptions, 1.5 sacks, seven tackles for loss and nine passes defended. Ole Miss will need others to step up with Joseph out this week. Oklahoma offensive lineman Michael Fasusi (shoulder) is questionable to play in the team's Week 7 game against Kentucky, while another offensive lineman, Eddy Pierre-Louis (undisclosed), is doubtful. There's optimism that Fasusi plays this week, while Pierre-Louis is not expected to suit up for the Sooners. Fasusi has not played since the team's season-opening game, but he was a former five-star recruit, so his return would be a boost for the Sooners' offensive line, especially if Pierre-Louis is out. Pierre-Louis has played in four games this season and has 21 career collegiate games under his belt. Oklahoma secured a massive win over top-ranked Texas last week, but ranked matchups against Kentucky and Mississippi State are next, so a battered offensive line is not a good sign for an offense that's already struggled to consistently move the ball.
