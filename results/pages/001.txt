UCF Faced With Depleted Secondary For Kansas Road Trip: Injury Report Breakdown
Both the UCF Knights and Kansas Jayhawks sport lengthy first player availability report ahead of their Saturday matchup in Lawrence.

Kansas Jayhawks
Once again this season, the UCF Knights have regained some players, only for another to add their name to their Big 12 Player Availability Report on Wednesday.
This week, it's the defensive back room that has officially been hit with the injury bug, as a third player from it has been ruled out for the Knights' matchup against Kansas at 3:45 p.m. on Saturday.
However, this week, UCF is not the only school in its matchup having a tough go against the injury bug, as the Jayhawks have a whopping nine players designated
questionable
and six ruled
out
.
Below is a breakdown of both teams' player availability reports and what they mean for both teams:

Kansas Jayhawks

Kahmel Johnson

Owen Spell

"O-line's a place where, you know, we're still in okay shape right now with the guys we got that have played football," Frost said on Monday.
Quarterback
Following Friday's Space Game, coach Scott Frost said
Keyone Jenkins

One of those guys Frost is referring to is linebacker
Jayden Jennings

probable

's name is nowhere to be found in the report for the first time this season.

Kansas Jayhawks
Quarterback
The Knights are not the only team that has gone through quarterback injuries this season, as all three of the Jayhawks' quarterbacks that have thrown a pass this season: Isaiah Marshall, Cole Ballard and Chase Jenkins have been ruled
questionable
for Saturday's game.
Ballard stepped in for an injured Marshall in the Jayhawks' last game against TCU, though he himself is now also on the player availability report.
Coach Lance Leipold said it was "too early to tell right now" who his quarterback would be on Monday, and it seems to remain that way on Wednesday.
In the event of a worst-case scenario in which all three are unable to play on Saturday, Kansas would likely need to turn to one of their trio of freshman quarterbacks: Jaylen Mason, Braylen Rooney or Connor Hanika.
THURSDAY UPDATE:
Both Marshall and Jenkins were upgraded to
probable
in the Jayhawks' Thursday report, paving the way for Marshall to resume his starting role on Saturday. Ballard remains
questionable
.
Wide Receiver
Three wide receivers can be found in the Jayhawks' report. Freshmen Tyren Parker and Griffin Baker have been ruled
out
, though they have not caught a pass for the team this season. Meanwhile, Bryson Hayes, who has caught six passes for 111 yards so far this season, is designated
questionable.
THURSDAY UPDATE:
Hayes was downgraded to
doubtful
in the Jayhawks' Thursday report.
Tight End
While neither Jack Utz, a freshman ruled
out
for Saturday's game, nor Jailen Butler, who is designated
questionable
, are the Jayhawks' main tight ends, it will leave them lacking some depth at the position as Leyton Cure is now going to need to step into a backup role behind starter Carson Bruhn.
Fun Fact: Utz was a teammate of UCF quarterback Rocco Marriott and tight end Brooks Hall at Platte County High School.
THURSDAY UPDATE:
Butler was upgraded to
probable
in the Jayhawks' Thursday report.
Secondary
Both of the Jayhawks' free safeties listed on their depth chart, Taylor Davis and Jaden Harris, have been designated
questionable
. They are joined by cornerback Sayeed Gibbs, a senior who played in nine games for the Jayhawks last season, but has not seen the field since Oct. 10 this season.
Meanwhile, prospects look better for HAWK Elijah Cannon and strong safety Mason Ellis, who were both ruled
probable
.
THURSDAY UPDATE:
A pair of players trended in the right direction for Saturday's game on Thursday, with starting free safety Taylor Davis was upgraded to
probable
and reserve stroing safety Mason Ellis seeing his name removed from the report altogether, which indicates he is available to take on the Knights.
Other
The Jayhawks' starting center Kasen Carpenter has already been ruled
out
, making Saturday's game his third consecutive missed game. So, similarly to the Knights, they have turned to a redshirt freshman in Anderson Kopp, who is set to make his third career start on Saturday, according to the team's depth chart.
Starting defensive end David Santiago was designated as
questionable
. His absence would leave Alex Bray to start in his place, according to the Jayhawks' depth chart. Meanwhile, reserve defensive tackle Grady Seyfert has been ruled
out
.
THURSDAY UPDATE:
Two Jayhawks linebackers made their way onto their Thursday availability report, with both starter Bam Crouch and freshman Josh Galbreath both being ruled
questionable
.
According to Kansas' depth chart, redshirt freshman Malachi Curvey is in line to step in for Crouch should he be unable to play on Saturday. However, Quincy Davis, a redshirt junior and backup in another linebacker slot for the Jayhawks that has made 29 tackles this season, could also see an increase in snaps.
