Ole Miss vs. Georgia injury report: Kewan Lacy out, Luke Ferrelli in
BY
Taylor Hodges
1 day ago
Email
Print
The first
Ole Miss availability report
for Georgia landed Wednesday, and like most injury reports this time of year, it came with a little bit of everything.
Let’s start with the good news.
Linebacker Luke Ferrelli wasn’t listed at all.
Considering where things stood a week ago, that’s a significant development. Ferrelli has been battling an ankle injury for weeks and spent all of last week on the report after aggravating it in practice. He started against Auburn but was limited before leaving the game early. His absence from Wednesday’s report is about as positive a sign as Ole Miss could have hoped for heading into Saturday’s showdown with No. 2 Georgia.
Now for the bad news.
Running back Kewan Lacy and safety Edwin Joseph are both out.
Lacy’s status
was expected
. Ole Miss has been preparing as if it would be without its star running back, so there were no surprises there. Joseph’s designation is a little tougher to swallow. There was at least a sliver of hope he could return after missing four straight games following ankle surgery, but that won’t happen this week.
The rest of the report falls somewhere in the middle.
Defensive lineman Kam Franklin and leading tackler Keaton Thomas were listed as questionable, while cornerback Jalyn Crawford and left tackle Terez Davis were listed as probable. None of those designations are expected to keep them off the field Saturday.
