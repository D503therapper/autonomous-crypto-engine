Chauncey Bowens’ absence will reportedly extend through at least the Georgia-Auburn game.
Pete Nakos of On3 reported Wednesday that Bowens will miss the Georgia-Auburn game. Bowens also missed Georgia’s Week 6 game at Alabama.
Bowens suffered a lower-body injury in UGA’s Week 5 game against Vanderbilt. Nakos reports that there is currently no timetable for Bowens’ return.
