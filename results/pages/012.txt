CHAPEL HILL, N.C. — North Carolina will be without at least one starter for Saturday afternoon’s rivalry matchup with Duke in Durham, while the availability of several other key contributors remains uncertain after Wednesday’s injury report issued by the ACC.
Two defensive backs were injured in last Saturday’s win at Pittsburgh. Starting cornerback
Tre Miller
suffered a serious lower body injury while returning his third-quarter interception of Pitt quarterback Holden Geriner. Inside Carolina reported on Sunday that program sources were fearing that it was season-ending. Wednesday’s availability report confirms Miller is out for the Duke game.
Starting nickel corner
Kaleb Cost
was the other key injury last Saturday, as he exited the game after a frightening collision with the end zone wall on the opening drive. Inside Carolina reported on Sunday that Cost was expected to miss some time but was not expected to sideline him for long. Indeed. he’s listed as doubtful in Wednesday’s availability report.
While the outlook for Miller and Cost for Saturday’s game is less encouraging, there is reason for optimism elsewhere on the Tar Heel defense. Defensive lineman
Jaylen Harvey
, who has recorded five tackles for loss and two sacks this season, has been upgraded to probable after missing last weekend’s contest at Pitt — a positive sign for his potential return against Duke. Starting safety Peyton Waters is also listed as probable for UNC.
Two other starters on the Carolina defense,
Isaiah Johnson
and
Greg Smith
, are listed as questionable. Johnson, an anchor in the middle of the Tar Heel defensive line, suffered an injury during the second half of UNC’s loss to Notre Dame. He went through pregame warmups last Saturday in Pittsburgh before ultimately being ruled out. Meanwhile, Smith, who leads the Tar Heels in interceptions, makes his first appearance of the season on the availability report.
On the offensive side of the ball, starting left tackle
Jordan Hall
is listed as questionable after missing UNC’s last two games against Notre Dame and Pitt. Meanwhile, tight end
Jordan Washington
, who has yet to make his debut in Carolina blue, has been upgraded to questionable after sitting out the Tar Heels’ first five games of the season from an injury suffered in the offseason.

UNC will release updated availability reports at 8 p.m. each day leading up to Saturday’s matchup, with its final report due two hours before kickoff.
Under the ACC’s availability reporting policy, a probable designation indicates a 75-percent likelihood of playing, while questionable and doubtful designations indicate 50-percent and 25-percent likelihoods, respectively.
