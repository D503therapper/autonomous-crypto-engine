SOUTH BEND, Ind. (WNDU) - The Notre Dame football team will be without several key defensive contributors for Saturday’s matchup against No. 1 Miami, and could face additional absences before kickoff, according to the team’s latest availability report released Monday afternoon.
Linebacker and team captain Drayk Bowen has been officially ruled out with a right ankle injury sustained during
Notre Dame’s win over Navy
on Saturday.
The Fighting Irish secondary will also remain depleted. Safety Brauntae Johnson, who has been sidelined since injuring his left foot in the
season opener against Wisconsin
, remains listed as out. Cornerback DJ McKinney will miss his second consecutive contest due to a right ankle injury suffered in
Notre Dame’s loss to BYU
.
Notre Dame could also be shorthanded along the defensive front, with two defensive linemen listed as questionable after suffering injuries against Navy. Francis Brewu is dealing with a left knee injury, while Jason Onye — who recently returned to action after missing several weeks with an injury suffered against Wisconsin — is listed with a right knee injury.
On a positive note for the Irish defense, cornerback Leonard Moore and linebacker Kyngstonn Viliamu-Asa, neither of whom played against Navy, were not listed on Monday’s report.
Offensive linemen Charles Jagusah (left humerus) and Chris Terek (right knee), along with defensive linemen Christopher Burgess Jr. (left tibia) and Tiki Hola (right knee), remain out for Saturday’s game.
