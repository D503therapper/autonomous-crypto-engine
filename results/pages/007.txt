TITLE: Tall hill for Grizzlies to climb against Bobcats | Daily Inter Lake
DATES SEEN: ['November 20, 2026', 'November 21, 2026']

Two wins over quarterback Keali’i Ah Yat and running back Eli Gillman don’t necessarily make Saturday easier. Gillman is having a season for the ages.
“He’s hard to tackle,” Vigen said of the Griz senior, who has 19 rushing touchdowns and 27 TDs overall this season. “That’s a testament to his endurance, his lower body strength, his moves, his speed. He’s kind of got it all. He’s a power back, he’s patient. You have to get there with the right kind of leverage and you’ve got to hang on for dear life, sometimes. And ideally, you get a second and third guy there.
“He’s going to be the best back that we’ve seen to this point, and we’ve seen him a lot.”
Both defenses have had their issues at times but seem to be on the upswing. After back-to-back losses to Northern Arizona and Idaho, Montana reset with more blitzing and stunting and now leads the league in sacks. It is, as Kennedy pointed out after UM’s hard-won 38-30 verdict over Idaho State, a group that had nine of its 11 starters miss time and 15 out of the top 22.
“We had a couple bumps in the road where there was a lot of criticism,” Kennedy said.
Similarly, Vigen said, “We’ve found our way back to playing a more aggressive style of defense.” Zac Crews, one of seven Missoula products on the Bobcats, has helped pick up the slack on a defensive line that lost four all-Big Sky performers from 2025.
“Zac’s had a really impressive year, and we needed him to,” Vigen said.
Young players dot the secondaries on both sides, though the Cats have obvious leadership from Caden Dowler and JJ Dolan. Griz receiver Brooks Davis has been a handful for every opponent; extra attention gives opportunities to the likes of Landon Ramson-Goelz, who had nine catches for 159 yards against Idaho State.
“They do a nice job in their zone defense of spreading it out and getting to the right holes,” Kennedy said of the Bobcats. “Then they do a great job of rallying to the ball. They are really good tacklers. So yeah, we know we’re going to have to beat zone, but if they throw some man in also, they’re very capable. I actually think their corners are very good.
“It’s going to present a challenge for us.”
