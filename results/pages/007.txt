Both Florida quarterbacks make initial injury report for showdown with Georgia
By
Braden Ramsey
Published:
Oct 29, 2026 · 10:46 AM EDT
Football coaches will exercise any advantage they can find when preparing for a game. Sometimes that’s putting a star player on the availability report, like
Lane Kiffin
did with Sam Leavitt against Ole Miss in Week 3.
Jon Sumrall
doesn’t seem to be acting intentionally deceptive, like Kiffin, but his recent injury updates are making things cloudy for his opponent.
On Wednesday night, the
Florida Gators
listed both quarterback Aaron Philo and Tramell Jones as questionable for this weekend’s game against the
Georgia Bulldogs
. Running back Duke Clark joined them in the questionable category. Wide receiver Bailey Stockton and linebacker Jaden Robinson are out, but wideout Vernell Brown III is probable for the matchup.
Florida has WR Bailey Stockton is doubtful. Both Gator quarterbacks are questionable — Tramell Jones and Aaron Philo. Vernell Brown III is probable. Georgia's KJ Bolden is questionable, along with Chauncey Bowens and Isiah Canion.

Philo, Florida’s starter from Weeks 1-6, missed the Gators’ upset win over Texas with a shoulder injury. Jones got the start against the Longhorns and was relatively efficient, completing 62.9% (22/35) of his passes for 155 yards and two touchdowns. He added 10 carries for 41 yards as a rusher. His efforts helped Florida hold a 38:11-21:49 edge in time of possession vs. Texas in their battle.
Georgia will now spend the next two days preparing for both passers. The Gators and Bulldogs take the Cocktail Party indoors to Atlanta’s Mercedes-Benz Stadium on Halloween afternoon (3:30 p.m. ET, ABC).
