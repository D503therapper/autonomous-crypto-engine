Here are some things to know, players to watch and a score prediction for UNC vs. UConn.
UNC third-down defense has to be better vs UConn
The Tar Heels have struggled to get off the field, allowing opponents to convert 44.2% of third-down situations. That mark ranks 123rd nationally, with only four Power Four defenses posting a worse third-down percentage defensively. UConn is 65th nationally with a third-down conversion percentage of 40.6%, so the opportunity for improvement is there for Bill Belichick's defense.
Billy Edwards raising game for Tar Heels offense
Questions about the quarterback position were warranted after UNC's loss at Clemson, but Billy Edwards Jr. has responded to the challenge since that setback in Death Valley to put the Tar Heels in position for wins. The reigning ACC Quarterback of the Week, Edwards had 341 yards and four TDs against Miami. He continues to find different receivers with quick decisions and has consistently shown an ability to bounce back after big hits. UConn is 115th in passing defense, allowing 13 TDs and an average of 249.1 yards per game.
UConn QB Kalieb Osborne is unique dual threat
UNC continues to look for its first win against a marquee quarterback and this week offers another opportunity to breakthrough in that category. UConn quarterback Kalieb Osborne is a true dual threat, averaging 273.4 yards per game in total offense to rank 27th nationally. Osborne has limited turnovers, throwing 14 passing TDs with two interceptions. He's logged a rushing and passing TD in half of the games.
UNC vs UConn score prediction
UNC 28, UConn 23
: It's been an emotional four-game stretch for the Tar Heels, with two road wins and a pair of disappointing home losses. So it'll be interesting to see how UNC responds in a game that could ultimately determine its postseason fate. In his return to familiar territory, Bill Belichick will see his team make enough plays to leave with a victory.
