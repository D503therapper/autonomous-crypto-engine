TITLE: South Alabama hosts ULM for Sun Belt opener
DATES SEEN: ['September 29, 2026']

MOBILE, Ala. (NBC 15) —
Homecoming weekend is bringing a little extra buzz to Hancock Whitney Stadium, where South Alabama is set to open Sun Belt Conference play Saturday against ULM with a chance to keep its home-field magic rolling.
The Jaguars return to Mobile after closing their non-conference schedule with a 45-21 loss at Kentucky. Even with that setback, South Alabama has been a different team at home, going 2-0 at Hancock Whitney Stadium while putting up 39 points against Southeastern Louisiana and 41 against Ohio.
South Alabama has also come out fast, scoring on its opening possession in three of four games this season, including touchdowns in both home contests.
A big reason for the early fireworks has been a productive ground game. PJ Martin, Keenan Phillips and Trae’von Dunbar have combined for 653 rushing yards and eight touchdowns through four games. Against Ohio, the Jaguars ran for a season-high 266 yards and four touchdowns.
South Alabama has paired that rushing attack with a clock-controlling style, ranking fifth nationally in time of possession at 34:40 per game and holding the possession advantage in three of its four games.
Even in the loss at Kentucky, quarterback Jared Hollins delivered a career day, setting highs with 23 completions, 266 passing yards and two touchdown passes. He hit Anthony Eager for a 36-yard score and Jeremiah Jones-Thomas for a 19-yard touchdown. Jones-Thomas has been a steady target this season with 19 receptions for 236 yards and a touchdown.
