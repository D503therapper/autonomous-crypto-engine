Vernell Brown III headlines pre-Week 10 Florida availability report designations
Emmanuel Oyebadejo, Bailey Stockton among other designations
By
Max Bernstein
Bayden Armstrong
|
The Independent Florida Alligator
Ahead of its Saturday night bout against Oklahoma (4-4, 2-3 SEC), No. 24 Florida (5-3, 3-3 SEC) has released its initial availability report on Wednesday night.
Wide receiver Vernell Brown III, who was originally listed as probable last week before being downgraded to questionable, a game-time decision and later out, was listed as doubtful on Wednesday's report.
Brown was Florida's leading receiver through its first four games, with 17 grabs for 271 yards and two touchdowns. However, he injured his knee against Ole Miss on Sept. 26 and has missed the Gators' last four contests.
Sumrall revealed Saturday that Brown suffered a setback in practice last Wednesday, and that his knee swelled up substantially the following morning. He tried to warm up hours before kickoff on Oct. 31 but was not physically able to go.
"As he started to kind of feel close to better, we've tried to expedite his return," Sumrall said Monday. "I think now we're gonna probably try to make sure he gets fully right before trying to return ... I do think it's frustrating for him and for everybody because he wants to play and he also makes our team better."
With Brown out of action, no wideout has stepped up more than Bailey Stockton. Despite being banged up for most of the season, the Georgia Tech transfer has played in all eight games and leads UF with 35 receptions. Stockton was given a questionable designation on Wednesday.
Stockton has suffered back and leg injuries this season. Against Ole Miss, his back was injured due to a hit that was called a targeting penalty. Then, against Texas, Stockton suffered a non-contact lower-body injury in the second quarter but returned in the second half.
Despite the pain, Stockton said post-Georgia game that he is going to do "everything" he can to remain on the field and help his team win.
"9-3 is the only thing we're thinking about," Stockton said.
Also listed as questionable ahead of Saturday's game was defensive end Emmanuel Oyebadejo. "Big E" leads Florida with 4.5 sacks and four pass deflections.
Linebacker Jaden Robinson remained on the injury report and was listed as out after missing the Florida-Georgia game. Sophomore Ty Jackson is expected to take on a larger role.
Joining Robinson as inactives were defensive lineman Jeramiah McCloud, right tackle Emeka Ugorji and tight end Evan Chieca.
