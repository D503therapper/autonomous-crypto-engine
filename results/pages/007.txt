NC State Football vs. Louisville: Kickoff Time, TV Channel, Injuries & What to Know
NC State and Louisville meet Saturday at Carter-Finley Stadium with both teams trying to keep a second ACC loss off the books
By
Eric Lusk
Game Center for the 2026 Louisville at NC State football game, including kickoff time, TV and radio information, stat comparisons, what to watch and more.
The NC State-Louisville football series doesn’t have a nickname or a trophy attached to it, but this year’s meeting might well be called the Anxiety Bowl.
Saturday’s matchup, which kicks off at 3:30 p.m. at Carter-Finley Stadium in Raleigh, features two teams trying to rebuild confidence and get their seasons back on track after a roller coaster opening month.
Louisville is coming off a stunning home loss to Wake Forest that knocked the Cardinals out of the Top 25. NC State survived Appalachian State last week but is still searching for its first win over a Power 4 opponent.
The loser will leave Saturday with two ACC losses and a difficult (impossible?) climb back into the conference race. The winner gets something both programs could use right now: a little momentum with the heart of the conference schedule on deck.
Here is everything you need to know about the matchup, including how to watch and listen, injury updates, series history, stat comparisons and more.

What to Know About Louisville at NC State
Pivotal conference game for both teams
Both NC State and Louisville enter the game with 2-2 overall records and one ACC loss apiece. Whoever picks up its second league loss will have little, if any, margin for error in the ACC race.
Louisville is looking to bounce back after losing at home as a double-digit favorite to Wake Forest. The defeat knocked them out of the national rankings on week after a win over SMU brought them in.
The Wolfpack defeated Appalachian State 41-31 on Saturday night, but the result left a sour taste in how it happened. After a catastrophic defeat at Vanderbilt the week before, Pack fans were none too happy that leads of 21-0 and 28-7 evaporated and that NC State had to sweat out a victory to an in-state G6 opponent seen as inferior.
NCSU is still looking for its first win of the season against a Power 4 opponent. Wins so far have come against Richmond and App State.
Will Louisville have starting quarterback Lincoln Kienholz?
Cardinals coach Jeff Brohm told media earlier this week that quarterback Lincoln Kienholz suffered an injury at the end of last Saturday’s loss against Wake Forest and would likely be a game-time decision for Saturday’s game against NC State.
“He has something. We aren’t going to elaborate. Will probably be a game-time decision,” Brohm said, according to Yahoo Sports.
Kienholz has completed 68.8 percent of his passes thus far through four games for 1,140 yards, seven touchdowns and one interception. He’s also been a strong factor in the run game, leading the Cardinals with six rushing TDs.
Davin Wydner, a transfer from West Georgia, is the likely Louisville option if Kienholz can’t go.
