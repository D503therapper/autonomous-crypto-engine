Treadway led UConn with 66 receiving yards but left the game late with a leg injury.
While the UConn offense stalled at times in the second half, the defense was staunch.
It didn't force a turnover but completely controlled the flow of the game.
Senior Huskies' LB John Lista had seven tackles, including three for a loss. On one of those plays, he delivered a punishing hit on a Monarchs' ballcarrier.
Next week, the Huskies play at Wyoming at noon in a tough road environment, at the highest altitude in FBS, where they'll look for a seventh win.
While six wins usually secures a bowl selection, it's no guarantee. Jason Candle's Toledo team went 6-6 in 2019 and was not chosen.
So, ask Candle, the Huskies are taking next Saturday's matchup as seriously as any.
