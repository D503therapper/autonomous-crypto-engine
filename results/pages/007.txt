TITLE: 3 keys for Washington State to get back on track at Utah State
DATES SEEN: ['Sep 12, 2026', 'Sep 19, 2026']

“I watched the tape (and asked), ‘How are they 1-4?’” Aggies coach
Bronco Mendenhall
said Monday during his weekly press conference. “I hope they are saying the same about us.”
Mendenhall pointed out a common factor in why both teams have only one win to their name: Strength of schedule.
“I am not going to make these teams the same, but there are more similarities than not in terms of circumstance,” Mendenhall said.

3. Turn Utah State's quarterback problems into takeaways

WSU (15 sacks) and Utah State (17 sacks) are the two teams in the Pac-12 whose quarterbacks have been sacked the most this season.
This is a golden opportunity for the Cougars to manufacture pressure on the Aggies’ two quarterbacks in Brosterhous, who has started the past four games, and McCae Hillstead, who started the season opener before suffering a forearm injury.
Combined, both Utah State signal callers have thrown a conference-high 10 interceptions this season. Hillstead was intercepted last week against Boise State and returned it for a touchdown in the first half.
When asked about WSU’s defense, Mendenhall specifically mentioned the presence of edge rushers Linus Zunk (career-high six tackles against Fresno State) and Matyus McLain (two tackles for loss and a shared sack against Fresno State).
