As mumps cases climb, San Diego State lists nearly three dozen players on Pac-12 availability report
By Kirk Kenney via Boulder Daily Camera,
23h ago
Getting your Trinity Audio player ready… San Diego State listed nearly three dozen players on its initial Pac-12 Availability Report released Wednesday night. It included 15 players listed on SDSU’s two-deep as either “doubtful” or “out” for this weekend’s game against Texas State. Among those listed out for the Aztecs are starting running back Lucky Sutton and right guard Michael Leville Watkins. Among those listed as doubtful are running back Javion Kinnard, who started in place of Sutton against James Madison and was injured last week against Toledo.
