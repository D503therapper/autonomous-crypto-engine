The second availability report has been announced by the Big Ten ahead of Purdue’s rivalry clash with Illinois on Saturday afternoon, as the
Boilermakers
and Illini do battle at 4:15 p.m. ET on BTN.
There were no changes to Purdue’s six players who were initially listed in the report on Wednesday.
Among those that are already ruled out of Saturday’s game are left tackle Joey Tanona, defensive back
Mister Clark
, tight end
Kylan Fox
.
Starting right tackle
Jude McCoskey
remains questionable for the Cannon Trophy game. McCoskey has started all four games for the Boilermakers this season and was replaced by true freshman Rico Schrieber after exiting the Notre Dame game early.
Tight end
George Burhenn
did not suit up last Saturday against Notre Dame, but is not included in the availability report tonight, which is a good sign for his status heading into Champaign.
