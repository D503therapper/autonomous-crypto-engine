TITLE: Offensive Players to Watch as Tulane Prepares for Charlotte Showdown - Yahoo Sports
DATES SEEN: ['October 28, 2026']

Which Uptown boys will be the ones to watch this Friday? Here's a look at who might take control offensively
RB Johnnie Daniels
All around, the Green Wave didn't see many contributions from its running backs against UTSA.
Daniels was the lone exception to that, with 104 yards and 1 touchdown on eight carries. Though 81 of those yards came on a single long run, he still had a bit more success than anyone else who played and was trusted with the ball the most out of anyone.

There's been high hopes for Jamauri McClure since his return, but he's had a quieter start than expected. Jaylin Lucas hasn't been consistent, and Maurice Turner has been sidelined for the past few weeks with an undisclosed injury. It's up to Daniels to lead the charge now, and he's going to be looking to feast against Charlotte.
WR Garrett Mmahat
Mmahat seemed to be a favorite target against the Roadrunners. The problem? He only caught two of his seven targets.
Those two receptions went for a total of 62 yards, so Mmahat has shown that he can be a downfield threat at times. The wideout just has to figure out a way to make the plays. He's a veteran at this point, and only catching 29 percent of passes is unacceptable, even if some of those misses weren't entirely his fault.
