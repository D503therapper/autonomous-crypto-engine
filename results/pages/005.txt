🩹 Injury & Availability Updates
Injuries could shape rivalry matchup:
The ACC released its second injury report Thursday night. Duke running back Nate Sheppard continues to be questionable for Saturday’s game against North Carolina, while the Tar Heels face uncertainty in their defensive secondary. UNC has ruled out Tre Miller and Jakob Weatherspoon, listed Kaleb Cost as doubtful and designated Greg Smith as questionable. Duke also lists veteran offensive lineman Matt Craycraft as questionable.
UNC added linebacker Ashton Blatt to the doubtful list Thursday. Duke’s list did not change from Wednesday to Thursday.

⏪ Last Time Out
North Carolina 21, Pittsburgh 17 (Oct. 10) –
The Tar Heels picked up their first win over a Top 25 team in the Bill Belichick era. Billy Edwards Jr. completed a 75-yard scoring pass to Shamar Easter with 12:36 left for what would be the winning points. UNC then turned to its defense to preserve the victory, turning away multiple drives inside North Carolina territory.
Georgia Tech 28, Duke 25 (Oct. 10) –
The Blue Devils were less than a minute away from improving to 5-0 before Georgia Tech rallied for the winning touchdown. Duke now faces another emotional test against its biggest rival, with a chance to avoid consecutive losses and remain in the thick of the ACC race.
How healthy is Duke RB Nate Sheppard?
There is no doubt Nate Sheppard has been the engine that makes Duke go this season. He’s one of the top rushers in FBS and has even been mentioned in early Heisman conversations. But he was less than 100 percent against Georgia Tech (still managing to rush for 99 yards and score twice).
The Blue Devils listed Sheppard as questionable for Saturday’s game in the ACC’s initial injury reports. If he can’t go, who will step up in his place? If he can go, how much of a workhorse back can he be?
On the flip side, UNC is dealing with its own injury issues, especially in the defensive secondary. This could be a coming-out-party type of game for Duke quarterback Walker Eget, who threw for 5,563 yards and 30 touchdowns in 2024 and 2025 while at San Jose State.
Eget threw 41 passes against Georgia Tech, completing 26 for 259 yards and a touchdown.
Duke loses kicker, linebacker for rest of the season
Duke coach Manny Diaz announced earlier in the week that starting kicker
Cosme Salas
and linebacker
Elliott Schaper
would be out for the rest of the 2026 season. Both suffered practice injuries that Diaz referred to as “freak” accidents.
Salas, a redshirt sophomore, had hit 4-of-5 field goals this season and made all 16 PATs. Freshman
Gavin Strange
will now assume primary kicking duties. Strange was 1-for-2 in field goals against Georgia Tech, missing a 33-yarder in the final seconds at Georgia Tech that would have tied the game.
Schaper, a sophomore, had made five tackles in Duke’s first four games. An injury also cut short his 2025 campaign.
Who will walk away with the Victory Bell
The Tar Heels and Blue Devils have been competing for this unique prize since 1948. North Carolina won five consecutive meetings from 2019-23, but Duke has claimed the last two, including a 32-25 victory in Chapel Hill last November. The Blue Devils will be seeking their first three-game winning streak in the rivalry since 2016-18.
