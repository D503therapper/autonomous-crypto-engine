Colorado football injury update: First participation report for game at Arizona State
Brian Howell
@ BrianHowell33
11/05/26
The
Colorado
football team could be without a pair of defensive starters this week.
On Wednesday night, the first player availability report of the week was posted, as CU (3-5, 1-4 Big 12) prepares for a trip to Arizona State (4-4, 2-3).
Defensive tackle
Dylan Manuel
and safety
Naeten Mitchell
are both listed as doubtful for the game against the Sun Devils on Saturday in Tempe, Ariz. (5:30 p.m., ESPN2).
Manuel, Mitchell doubtful
Manuel, who was questionable last week but still played, has 18 tackles and two tackles for loss this season. He has played 75.4% of the defensive snaps for the Buffs. Manuel and Santana Hopper have played the bulk of the season together at defensive tackle.
Mitchell has started every game but was injured in the second half of Saturday’s 27-24 overtime win against Kansas State. He’s third on the team with 62 tackles and has been on the field for 94% of the defensive snaps this year.
Welch, others still out
Four Buffs are listed as out for this week, three of which have been out.
Running back Micah Welch, the Buffs’ second-leading rusher, is out for a third straight game. Receiver Danny Scudero will miss a seventh straight game and defensive back Braylon Edwards a fifth straight. Walk-on receiver Kaleb Mathis, who also plays special teams, is also listed as out.
Defensive end Vili Taufatofua is also listed as doubtful. He missed last week’s game.
