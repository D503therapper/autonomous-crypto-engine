The official injury report for Tennessee vs. Arkansas has released. Here is every player on the list for both teams.

|

They will now play against the Razorbacks on the road, which is a place that the Vols lost the last time they played there, as they were defeated in the same season that they went to the college football playoffs with Nico Iamaleava at quarterback.
This is also a game that the Vols will have some uncharted water in. This is because
George MacIntyre
, a second-year gunslinger from Brentwood Academy and Franklin, Tennessee, is set to get his first career start at quarterback. This comes following an injury that Faizon Brandon suffered in the game against the Texas Longhorns, and a game in which he was benched for MacIntyre due to complications from the week, as he took the snaps from Brandon for part of the second quarter and the remainder of the game.
This game can be determined by a ton of different factors, but one of the things that fans should be closely watching is the injury report, which is why we are here. The Vols and the Razorbacks had an injury report release, which is expected every day before kickoff. The Vols are a banged-up team, but the Razorbacks have their fair share of key injuries as well.
Here is a full look at the report.
