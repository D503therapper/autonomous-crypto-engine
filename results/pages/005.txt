Chris Marshall questionable, Ahmad Hardy not listed on first availability report for Arkansas football vs. Missouri
October 28, 2026 at 7:23 p.m.
by
Christina Long
Arkansas receiver Chris Marshall (6) watches from the sideline, Saturday, Oct. 17, 2026, during the second half of the Razorbacks' game at Vanderbilt at FirstBank Stadium in Nashville, Tenn. (Hank Layton/WholeHogSports)
The SEC released the first player availability report Wednesday night for the
Arkansas football
team’s Week 9 game against Missouri.
Most notable on the report for the Razorbacks (3-4, 1-3 SEC) is wide receiver Chris Marshall being questionable.
The Razorbacks' leading receiver left the win over Vanderbilt in Week 7 in the second quarter and came out of halftime in street clothes. Marshall appeared hobbled as he went out of bounds on his lone reception of the game and was seen reaching for his hamstring on the sideline.
Arkansas offensive coordinator Tim Cramsey said Tuesday that Marshall "looked good" in practice.
"He's out running his routes, what he can do," Cramsey said. "We're not pushing pushing, you know. But he's out running routes. He has an unbelievable attitude, positive attitude on everything he does. He had a good practice today."
Two more key offensive players, running back Braylen Russell and tight end Jaden Platt, were listed as probable, as was rotational defensive lineman Caleb Bell.
Center Caden Kitler was listed as out after he missed the Razorbacks' win over Vanderbilt. Kitler suffered a broken toe earlier this season and has been battling plantar fasciitis. He was replaced by Kash Courtney during Arkansas' loss to Tennessee in Week 6.
Defensive back Braydon Lee, who has not recorded any tackles, was listed as out for a second straight week. Backup left tackle Josiah Clemons was also listed as out.
Other than Kitler, whose injury was reported on the pregame radio broadcast before the Vanderbilt game, Arkansas coach Ryan Silverfield has not specified the nature of any injuries to players on the report.
Missouri (5-2, 2-2) has three players listed as out: Running back Malae Fonoti, offensive lineman Josh Atkins and tight end Gavin Hoffman. None of the three have appeared in more than one game this season.
Notably, running back Ahmad Hardy was not listed for the Tigers. The SEC's leading rusher last year, Hardy has yet to play this season after he was shot in the leg in May while at a concert in Mississippi.
The program announced Monday that Hardy had returned to practice, but an exact timeline for his return to game action was not clear.
The conference requires availability reports for football games between SEC opponents. Arkansas and Missouri must submit daily updates leading up to a final report 90 minutes prior to kickoff Saturday.
Schools that report late or inaccurately will be penalized between $25,000 and $100,000, according to the SEC's reporting policy.
Saturday’s game at Reynolds Razorback Stadium in Fayetteville is scheduled to kick off at 6:45 p.m. on SEC Network.

Availability status definitions, per SEC policy:
Available
– The player is fully available for the next game.
Probable
– Likely to play, barring any setbacks.
Questionable
– Uncertain to play due to injury or condition
Doubtful
– Unlikely to play due to significant concerns.
Out
– Player will not participate in the upcoming game.
Game Time Decision
– Final decision will be made closer to the game.
