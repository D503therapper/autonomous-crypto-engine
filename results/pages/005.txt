Aidan Chiles remains questionable on Northwestern's second injury report ahead of Iowa. With kickoff approaching, the Wildcats face uncertainty at quarterback as they prepare for a crucial Big Ten matchup
Northwestern
quarterback Aidan Chiles remains listed as questionable on the Wildcats’ second Big Ten availability report of the week, keeping his status one of the biggest storylines to monitor with Saturday’s matchup against
Iowa
just two days away.
Chiles was also listed as
questionable
on Wednesday’s initial report, and Thursday’s update brought no change to his designation. The quarterback’s specific injury has not been publicly disclosed, leaving questions about what he may be dealing with and whether he will be ready to take the field at Ryan Field.
Although Chiles played through Northwestern’s 34-26
loss
to
Oregon
last Saturday, his continued presence on the injury report suggests there is something the team is monitoring.
It is worth noting that Northwestern has had players listed as questionable earlier this season who ultimately played, so the designation alone does not mean Chiles will be unavailable. Still, with kickoff approaching, his status is becoming an increasingly serious situation to follow.
Related stories
Chiles has been one of the biggest reasons behind Northwestern’s 6-2 start. Through eight games, he has thrown for 1,898 yards and 13 touchdowns while completing 68.8% of his passes and throwing just one interception. He has also added 10 rushing touchdowns, making him one of the most dangerous dual-threat quarterbacks in the Big Ten.
Chiles showed his playmaking ability again against Oregon, finishing with 281 passing yards and a touchdown while adding 93 rushing yards and another score. Even in the loss, he accounted for 374 yards of total offense and continued to give Northwestern opportunities to move the ball against a talented opponent.
If Chiles cannot play Saturday, Nicco Marchiol would likely take over at quarterback. The West Virginia transfer would face a difficult challenge stepping into the starting role against an Iowa defense known for its physicality and ability to pressure opposing quarterbacks.
Marchiol has limited game experience with the Wildcats this season, making the possibility of his first extended action an important storyline in its own. Northwestern would likely need to adjust its offensive approach depending on who starts.
Marchiol would have to find a rhythm with the Wildcats’ receivers and establish a connection with the running game while facing one of the tougher defensive tests on the schedule. The coaching staff would also have to determine how much of its existing offensive plan it could keep in place.
Iowa enters the matchup at 6-2 and is coming off a dominant 30-0 victory over Wisconsin. The Hawkeyes present a significant challenge, particularly for an offense that will need to avoid mistakes and sustain drives to keep pace. Having Chiles’ experience and dual-threat ability available would give Northwestern another important weapon.
The Wildcats are looking to respond after their four-game winning streak ended against Oregon. With a chance to earn an important Big Ten victory at home, Saturday’s game carries plenty of weight for Northwestern as it looks to get back on track.
For now, Chiles’ status remains uncertain. Northwestern will have another opportunity to provide an update before kickoff, but with just two days remaining, his availability is something fans will be watching closely.
Whether he is able to suit up or Marchiol gets the opportunity, the quarterback situation could play a major role in how the Wildcats approach Saturday’s matchup.
