YARDS? WHAT'S THE DEAL WITH 200?
Eastern Michigan's defense put itself in rare company Saturday (Sept. 26). For the seventh time since 1995, the Eagles held an opponent to fewer than 200 yards of total offense, limiting Lindenwood to 191 yards on 52 plays in EMU's 29-3 victory. The 191-yard effort marked EMU's first sub-200-yard defensive performance since 2024, when the Eagles held St. Francis (Pa.) to 154 yards. Since 1995, EMU has held an opponent below 200 yards only seven times. The other performances came against Central Michigan and Akron in 2018, Indiana State in 2008, Western Michigan in 2007, and UCF in 2003. Lindenwood averaged just 3.7 yards per play and scored only three points, giving EMU its lowest yards-allowed total since the 154-yard performance against St. Francis (Pa.).
200 YARDS OF SEPARATION
Eastern Michigan's offense created a 205-yard gap, Sept. 26, out-gaining Lindenwood, 396-191, in the Eagles' 29-3 victory. It marked the 14th time since 1995 that EMU has out-gained an opponent by more than 200 yards and the first since the Eagles held a 208-yard advantage over Central Michigan in 2022. Saturday's 205-yard margin ranks among EMU's 14 largest such advantages over the past three decades, a list topped by the 339-yard margin against Indiana State in 2008, when the Eagles piled up 505 yards of total offense.
RARITY: ALLOWING THREE POINT OR LESS
Eastern Michigan has held an opponent to three points or fewer 12 times since joining the Mid-American Conference in 1976. The Eagles have recorded seven shutouts in those games, along with two ties and one loss. EMU's most recent such performance came Sept. 26, when the Eagles defeated Lindenwood, 29-3. The Eagles also held St. Francis (Pa.) scoreless in 2024, Indiana State scoreless in 2008, and Akron scoreless in 1997. EMU's 12 games allowing three points or fewer include six against MAC opponents and six against non-conference foes.

GOING FOR 2(-0)
EMU has already played one MAC game, which came in Week 0 over Sacramento State (28-17). The Eagles are looking to add a win at UMass which would not only keep Eastern atop the league standings, but also see it start 2-0 in MAC play for the first time since the 2005 season. That year, Eastern claimed a 23-20 (OT) win at Central Michigan (Sept. 24) before grabbing a 27-20 Homecoming win over Kent State (Oct. 1) in Ypsilanti.
TOUGH OUTS
In the game with Lindenwood (Sept. 26), Eastern Michigan had the ball for eight drives and punted just once as the Eagles scored three touchdowns and attempted four field goals with three kicks converted. The last time the Eastern Michigan offense went the entire game without a 3-and-out drive was Sept. 17, 2022, in the Eagles' 30-21 upset of Arizona State inside Sun Devil Stadium in Tempe, Ariz. That night, Eastern was able to 'Fly the Flag' as the offense possessed the ball 10 times and scored six times, including three touchdowns and three field goals without going 3-and-out once.
BREAKING THE SEAL
Heading into the game at Wisconsin (Sept. 19), Eastern had been held scoreless in the opening quarter of its first three games. That run ended against the Badgers with a touchdown scored on the team's first offensive possession of the game. WINDEX TREATMENT
Against Lindenwood (Sept. 26), the defense stood tall in the first quarter once again as Eastern blanked the Lions in the opening stanza to mark its third consecutive home game it has shutout the visiting team. Additionally, the Eagles have allowed just 10 points in the first quarter through five games, including seven points at Michigan State and three points at Wisconsin. The Eagles also held the Lions scoreless for the first half of the game and didn't allow points to Lindenwood until 6:37 remaining in the third quarter when the visitors connected on a 44-yard field goal. The last time Eastern held an opponent in first half of a game came Sept. 21, 2024, at home in a 36-0 victory over visiting St. Francis (Pa.).
