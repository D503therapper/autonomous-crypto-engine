TITLE: Game Day: Sam Houston - Liberty University
DATES SEEN: ['Nov. 29, 2024', 'Oct. 5, 2023', 'Oct. 8, 2024', 'Oct. 8, 2025']

#1
Thursday will mark Liberty's first of four CUSA home games during its fourth season as a conference member. The Flames are 10-3 against CUSA foes at Williams Stadium since joining the league. Liberty ranks No. 1 in CUSA in rushing offense this season (208.2 ypg), while Sam Houston tops the conference in passing offense (308.0 ypg).
#2
Both previous Liberty-Sam Houston matchups have been won by the home team, and the two contests were decided by a total of seven points. The Flames scored 54 straight points during their back-to-back wins at Coastal Carolina and Delaware. They turned a 17-10 halftime deficit at Coastal into a 34-17 victory with 24 unanswered points, and then raced out to a 30-0 advantage during Friday's CUSA opener at Delaware.
#3
Liberty has only allowed one opposing QB to throw for 300 yards since the start of 2024 (Delaware's Nick Minicucci in 2025). The Flames will attempt to limit Sam Houston QB Landyn Locke, who already has three 300-yard games to his credit in 2026.
