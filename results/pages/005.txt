TITLE: Rockets, Bobcats Become Bowl Eligible With Saturday Victories - Mid-American Conference

Toledo 45, Western Michigan 38
Box Score
TOLEDO, Ohio -
The University of Toledo roared out to a three-score halftime lead and withstood a second-half rally to earn a critical 45-38 Mid-American Conference victory over Western Michigan on Saturday at the Glass Bowl.
The Rockets (6-2, 3-1 MAC) blasted off in the first half, building a 14-0 lead in the first quarter and led 31-14 at halftime. The Broncos (5-3, 3-1 MAC) got within three, 38-35, after the third quarter and tied it in the fourth, but the Rockets retook the lead and forced a 3-and-out and intercepted a pass in the final minutes. The Rockets handed its second consecutive opponent its first MAC loss.
Despite having just one opening day starter on the offensive line, CJ Miller didn't have any trouble finding space to run, rushing for 183 yards on 33 carries (5.5 yards per carry) and a touchdown.
Autavius Ison and John Alan Richter added rushing scores on the afternoon.
Adjatay Dabbs (124 yards, eight catches) and Bryson Hammer (107 yards, seven catches) made dual 100-yard receivers.
