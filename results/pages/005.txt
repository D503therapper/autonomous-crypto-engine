The Youngstown State football team concludes regular season play at home as they head into their last conference match up against Illinois State at Stambaugh Stadium on Saturday afternoon. Kickoff for the contest is set for 4:30 p.m.
Tailgating festivities are set to begin at 10:30 a.m. in the M-70 (Gault Heating and Cooling) and M-81 (Youngstown Tile Retail Design Center) Tailgate Lots. Beer will be on sale in all tailgate lots at that time. Parking will be available in various surface lots around the Stadium and in the M-30 Deck.
Gate B will be closed due to construction on the roof of Stambaugh; Will Call booth will be available at the North Ticket Booth
Handicap parking in the M-60 Lot located south of the stadium will be available starting at 3 p.m. YSU Parking Services will offer golf-cart shuttle service to the South concourse elevator.
Again this season, YSU will be doing a hybrid of acceptable tickets for entry. Mobile and printed tickets will both be accepted, however mobile tickets are highly encouraged to decrease wait times. All student tickets for the 2026 campaign will be digital.
The YSU Bookstore, which is open from 12:30 p.m. to 4 p.m., will also be selling merchandise at the game on the West Stands Concourse. They will be accepting credit cards for purchases.
Throughout the game members of the softball team will be selling 50/50 Raffle Tickets. The 50/50 raffle is also available
online
. The YSU women's and men's golf teams will be selling game programs.
As always, YSU students are admitted free with a valid student ID. Tickets on gameday are $25 for reserved seats, but only $20 in advance (Youth - $12/10 and Seniors $10). The YSU Athletic Ticket Office is open 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. on Wednesday.
Postgame:
Bryant Heating & Cooling 5th Quarter Radio Show on AM 570 WKBN
Fans are encouraged to use #GoGuins and #PenguinNation when posting gameday photos and videos on Instagram and X. If you need assistance in the tailgate lot, contact YSU Police at (330) 941-3527.
Fan Services and credit card sales are available at the YSU Athletic Ticket Office inside Stambaugh Stadium.
