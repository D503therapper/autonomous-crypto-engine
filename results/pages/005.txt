and guard
Michael Leville Watkins
are among the key players listed as “OUT” for Saturday’s contest. Head coach
Sean Lewis

“The football gods have not really favored us with the injury luck,”
Lewis said.
“Guys have got to be ready to step into the light when the time comes. … We’ve been given a nice storm here. But this too shall pass, and we’ve got to be able to see our way through it.”
Lewis declined to identify the players who were apart of the mumps outbreak, citing federal privacy laws. San Diego State confirmed three cases and two probable cases, all linked to the football team. The university later said the players were no longer contagious.
“The university’s email Wednesday from Dr. T. Ghosh, medical director of San Diego State Student Health Services, said that ‘because mumps symptoms can develop 12 to 25 days after exposure, these new cases reflect spread within the team and potential exposure to additional close contacts. The university continues to take a number of daily actions,'” Schlabach wrote. “The email said mumps is best known for causing puffy cheeks and a tender, swollen jaw, along with fever, headache, muscle aches, fatigue, loss of appetite and pain or tenderness near the ears or jaw.”
San Diego State is also offering students a free MMR booster in light of the outbreak. Without a good amount of players, it’ll certainly be a challenge for the Aztecs (1-3) as they take on the Bobcats (2-2).
