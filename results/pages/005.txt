Initial SEC injury report features long list of names for Oklahoma, Kentucky
By
Parker Gillam
Published:
Oct 14, 2026 · 9:41 PM EDT
As the season has progressed, the
SEC
‘s weekly injury reports have begun to resemble CVS receipts in their concerning length, and Week 6 is no different.
Kentucky
and
Oklahoma
are feeling the brunt of that reality, as both teams feature a plethora of names on the report ahead of their matchup Saturday in Norman.
In total, the Wildcats have 11 players listed on the initial posting, while the Sooners trump that number with 13 athletes in total. And in somewhat of a rarity,
Will Stein
‘s squad has players listed under every category (Out, Doubtful, Questionable and Probable).
The most notable inclusions are Oklahoma offensive linemen Michael Fasusi and Eddy Pierre-Louis being listed as questionable, along with star defensive back Eli Bowen.
Meanwhile, Kentucky’s standout running back CJ Baxter has been listed as questionable after he fought through ailments during the win over LSU and still ran for over 100 yards. The Texas transfer could exact revenge for his former teammates this weekend, but his status is up in the air.
Michael Fasusi is questionable for Oklahoma, along with Eddy Pierre-Louis and Eli Bowen. Kentucky tailback CJ Baxter is questionable.
