The
Big Ten availability report
for the season was released for
Indiana
on Wednesday night ahead of its matchup with Rutgers. The availability report is released every day in advance of league games, starting three days before the game.
Among the players listed as ‘Probable’ were running back
Turbo Richard
and linebacker
Rolijah Hardy
. Both players were injured in Friday night’s Big Ten opener against Northwestern. Richard is dealing with a high ankle sprain and Hardy, an upper-body injury.
“We got really good news on Turbo and he’ll be day to day,” Curt Cignetti said on Monday. “I’m optimistic he can play Saturday, as well as Rolijah Hardy. We expect him to be fine.”
On the season, Richard had been one of the top backs in the country running for 383 yards on 7.7 yards a carry with four touchdowns. Hardy has 53 tackles, three tackles for loss and one interception this season.
Cornerback
AJ Harris
, who has been out for three weeks after playing just eight snaps in the season opener, is listed as ‘Questionable’. He dealt with a knee injury throughout fall camp and is dealing with a lower leg injury since the opener.
“AJ Harris, when we brought him in here we thought he would help us and really he has probably practiced 20% of the time since he’s been here,” Cignetti said after Indiana’s win over Western Kentucky. “Some spots were really thin, but we got good players.”
Jaylen Bell
started in place for Harris last week, his first start of the season, and played all but one snap alongside
at cornerback.
Reserve running back
Sean Cuono
is also ‘Questionable’

Daniel Ndukwe
, reserve corner
Carson Williams
