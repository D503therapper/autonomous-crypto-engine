FIU Panthers Have Been Winning With Defense
FIU enters at 3-2 overall and 1-0 in Conference USA after consecutive victories over Long Island and New Mexico State. The Panthers defeated Long Island 20-3 before earning a 22-3 victory over New Mexico State on October 7. Those performances followed an uneven opening stretch that included losses to South Florida and Florida Atlantic, along with a 33-20 victory over Buffalo. FIU has gradually established greater defensive consistency, but the offense remains a concern against opponents capable of generating sustained pressure.
The Panthers have allowed only 61 points through five games, an impressive average of 12.2 per contest. FIU has held four of its five opponents to 19 points or fewer, demonstrating an ability to limit scoring opportunities and force opposing offenses into difficult situations. The offense, however, is averaging just 18.8 points, and the Panthers have surpassed 22 points only once this season. That imbalance means FIU often depends on its defense to protect relatively narrow advantages.
Tuesday presents a more difficult challenge against a Jacksonville State team that has scored at least 23 points in five consecutive games. FIU will need to control the Gamecocks’ rushing attack and prevent extended drives that wear down the defense. The Panthers have been particularly effective keeping opponents out of the end zone recently, but their own offensive limitations make playing from behind dangerous. If FIU can establish field position and prevent Jacksonville State from controlling possession, the Panthers should have an opportunity to remain competitive.

Pick: FIU Panthers +3
I’m taking FIU with the three points. Jacksonville State deserves respect for its three-game winning streak, but the Gamecocks narrowly escaped against Kennesaw State and now face a considerably more disciplined defensive opponent. FIU has allowed only 12.2 points per game and has kept four of its five opponents below 20. The Panthers don’t generate overwhelming offensive production, but they have demonstrated an ability to remain competitive by limiting mistakes and controlling the pace. Jacksonville State should have opportunities to establish its rushing attack, although FIU’s defensive consistency makes a comfortable Gamecocks victory difficult to anticipate. In a matchup that could be decided by a field goal, taking the points with the road underdog is the preferred approach.
Pick: Under 45.5 Points
The Under 45.5 is the total pick. FIU has held its last two opponents to three points apiece, and four of its five games have finished below the posted closing totals. Jacksonville State has shown greater offensive ability, but the Panthers present a difficult challenge because of their defensive discipline and ability to limit scoring opportunities. FIU’s own offense has averaged fewer than 19 points per game, making it unlikely the Panthers will force a particularly high-scoring contest without defensive or special teams contributions. The Gamecocks may establish possession through their rushing attack, which could also reduce the number of possessions available. A 23-20 or 24-17 result is realistic, making the Under the preferred selection.
Final Picks: FIU Panthers +3 and Under 45.5 Points
