The initial injury report for Louisville's matchup with Syracuse at the JMA Wireless Dome on Saturday night is out and there are a few significant notes for both rosters. We are going to start with the Syracuse side, as the spread opened with Louisville being a 10.5-point favorite which has since jumped to 15.5.
We are going to start with a look at the Orange to determine why the spread has jumped so drastically.

Syracuse has seen four quarterbacks take snaps this season due to numerous injuries in the first half of the season. Steve Angeli is a transfer from Notre Dame and was the day starter. Angeli hasn't seen game action since the Pitt game on September 17th. The senior from New Jersey struggled in the two conference games he saw action in, throwing five interceptions to just one touchdown.

When Angeli went down against Pitt, Amari Odom (who is listed as OUT) took the reigns and was a perfect 8-for-8 through the air and started the next week against Connecticut, throwing five touchdowns while playing hurt.
