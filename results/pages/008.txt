UNC Football vs. No. 3 Notre Dame: Kickoff Time, TV Channel, Injuries & What to Know
Tar Heels welcome unbeaten No. 3 Notre Dame to Kenan Stadium on Saturday. UNC is 1-45-1 all-time against Top 5 foes
By
Eric Lusk
North Carolina football doesn’t exactly have a sterling track record against elite competition. The Tar Heels are 1-45-1 all-time against opponents ranked in the Top 5 of the Associated Press poll.
They’ll get another chance Saturday when No. 3 Notre Dame visits Kenan Stadium. Kickoff in Chapel Hill is set for noon on ESPN.
UNC enters as a three-touchdown underdog and will face one of the toughest defenses in the country. While UNC has been stout on defense in its own right, this will be the toughest test yet for Bill Belichick’s stopping unit.
Here is everything you need to know about the matchup, including how to watch and listen, injury updates, series history, stat comparisons and more.

🩹 Notre Dame at UNC Injury & Availability Updates
North Carolina
Head coach Bill Belichick did not offer a comprehensive injury and player availability update Monday, and the Tar Heels are not required to release injury/availability information since this is not an ACC contest.
There are three UNC names worth monitoring:
Edge rusher Melkart Abou Jaoude
: UNC’s 2025 sacks leader (10.5) missed the past two games with a hand injury suffered against TCU. There has been chatter about Abou Jaoude’s return. Belichick has been less clear: “Hopefully, he’ll be back out there soon … He’s getting better every day. He’s been out there, he’s doing all he can.”
Kicker Rece Verhoff
: A court ruling has given Verhoff eligibility for now. But it is unclear if he’ll be available for Notre Dame. He would certainly be a lift to the kicking game as the Heels have missed three field goals from 40-49 yards thus far. Verhoff hit 19-of-23 field goals last season, was 10-of-11 from 40-49 yards and nailed a 57-yarder.
Wide receiver CJ Sadler:
He missed the Clemson game following a traffic stop. When asked about Sandler’s availability for Notre Dame, Belichick responded: “We’ll see.” Sadler started against ETSU, making three catches for 66 yards.
Notre Dame
The Fighting Irish released their weekly injury report Monday. Wide receiver Quincy Porter will miss the remainder of the season because of a knee injury that requires surgery. Safety Luke Talich returns from a wrist injury.

Notre Dame on a mission after playoff snub
The Marcus Freeman coaching era at Notre Dame, which started in 2022, has had its bumps and bruises for sure. The Irish lost at home to Marshall as a three-touchdown favorite in 2022 and fell to Northern Illinois early in 2024.
Notre Dame dropped two games – to Miami and Texas A&M – to open the 2025 campaign, and those defeats ultimately kept them out of the College Football Playoff. So far, the Irish haven’t come close to stubbing their toes in 2026, spurred on in part by last year’s playoff snub.
Freeman has adopted “Leave No Doubt” as a team mantra, and his team has certainly lived up to the slogan so far. UNC has had an extra week to prepare for the Irish, and it will be interesting to see if the Heels can introduce any doubt into the Notre Dame equation on Saturday.
Irish winning the turnover margin
Notre Dame has only turned the ball over once through four games – an interception by quarterback CJ Carr. In comparison, the Irish have forced nine opponent fumbles, recovering six, and picked off three passes. That’s a +8 turnover margin.
