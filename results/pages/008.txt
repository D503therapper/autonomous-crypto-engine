It's a bit hard to figure out exactly what the South Florida Bulls are going to do in their final game of the year against the
Tulane Green Wave
, with a bit more changes coming for their squad ahead of the regular season finale.
For one, the Bulls let go of offensive coordinator Tim Beck on Sunday following their 35-14 loss to the Florida Atlantic Owls, which was their third straight loss that put them at 6-5 with a 2-5 American Conference slate.
Beck was previously the head coach of the Coastal Carolina Chanticleers and came over under first-year head coach Brian Hartline. He had 12 years of experience as OC or co-coordinator in multiple stops.
They have decided that they won't wait until the season's done to make changes.
What exactly does that mean for the Green Wave? It's not exactly clear who will call plays, with Hartline not really revealing that info when talking about the decision.
It's also not clear who will be their quarterback, though it is probably going to be Luke Kromenhoek. The backup went in for an injured Michael Van Buren Jr. a few weeks back and has had a pretty electric last two weeks, throwing for 656 yards in the last two games and four passing touchdowns.
In addition, he's a bit more of a threat on the run than Van Buren, with 162 yards on the ground and one score in that same span.
While they might throw a few things at the Wave that they haven't shown this year, or play with a bit of a something-to-prove edge, it's hard to not be what and who you are at this point in the season, and with one week left remaining in the season, there's not really a good reason to change everything.
So Tulane can pretty much know what to expect, but there's definitely an element of the unknown here.
Luckily, they showed an ability to handle that last week when the North Texas Mean Green
essentially had to light their playbook on fire
once they ran out of serviceable quarterbacks.
It won't be a switch to the triple option this week, or anything that drastic, but perhaps they try to utilize Kromenhoek's legs a little more and play him against the
Bulls' more balanced rushing attack
led by D.J. Crowther.
