The goal is to make sure the weather does not create unnecessary problems once the game begins.
Louisiana defensive back Priest Ashe said the team is ready to play regardless of the conditions.
“Rain, sleet, snow, we go out there and ball. It don’t matter if we in the parking lot, the grass. Hey, line the football, we ready to play.”
Offensive lineman Kaden Moreau said keeping the football dry will be important, but the weather should not change Louisiana’s approach.
“He’s gonna have to have towels ready, y’all wiping the ball, but at the end of the day, we’re playing football.”
Moreau said the Cajuns still have to focus on the fundamentals, including running off the ball and protecting the quarterback.
