The Huskies are set to take on the
Nebraska Cornhuskers
at 5 p.m. PT on Saturday at Memorial Stadium in Lincoln, Neb. In the team's injury report for the game Wednesday, first-year edge rusher Ramzak Fruean, who's started every game as a true freshman for the team to this point,

Fruean suffered a knee injury in the team's 38-26 win against the Purdue Boilermakers on Oct. 16 and was held out of practice last week due to the same ailment.
In the day since that injury report, Fruean's status for the game against the Cornhuskers looks a lot more optimistic, per head coach Jedd Fisch.
"Yeah that was actually (Wednesday's) report," Fisch said in a weekly news conference held via Zoom on Thursday. "We adjusted that today. He had a really good day of rehab yesterday, so he is now questionable for the game and I think he's trending the right direction to have some ability to play. We're not exactly sure what that ability will be. ... He was listed as out yesterday and then really had a fantastic day of recovery and rehab and woke up this morning in a lot better place."
The team has used an injured defensive player in a unique but limited capacity before. Third-year defensive back and starting nickelback Rahshawn Clark didn't play on defense but played on special teams in his normal role as a returner in the team's loss against the Minnesota Golden Gophers on Sept. 27.
