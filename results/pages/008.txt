Western Kentucky was supposed to be a Conference USA powerhouse this season, and if it's going to happen, it has to happen now.
Getting rocked by Georgia and Indiana by a combined score of 108-20 is fine – the checks for those games cleared. But to get blown out by Nevada 49-14, and to start the CUSA season with a 34-13 loss isn't normal for a rock-solid Hilltopper program.
Missouri State also has yet to beat an FBS team this season, but it battled well in losses to Marshall and SMU. Get a win in Bowling Green, and the Casey Woods era gets a big early boost.
Missouri State vs Western Kentucky Quick Hits
Missouri State alternates road and home games the rest of the way, but it's in the midst of a run of three road games in four, hosting Kennesaw State next.

Why Western Kentucky Will Win
So what's Missouri State's problem? It's awful on third downs.
Bad at generating third down plays, and worse at stopping third down tries, the Bears can't seem to find any rhythm and can't come up with any groove. Throw in a massive penalty issue, and too many positives end up fizzling out with mistakes.
Missouri State vs Western Kentucky Prediction, Betting Lines
Western Kentucky won't exactly look the part, but the bleeding will stop for now. Rodney Tisdale and the passing game will be sharp, and this is where Missouri State's third down issues will really matter. The Hilltoppers will keep things moving in a fun game.
