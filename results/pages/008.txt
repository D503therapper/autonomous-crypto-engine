Iowa has four players listed on Wednesday's injury report, with two of them being listed as out — most notably starting strong safety Tyler Brown.
Brown appeared to pull up with a hamstring injury during Iowa's 20-19 win at Michigan, then stayed in the game and surrendered a 48-yard touchdown pass on the next play. Brown continued to play the rest of the fourth quarter and even met with the media on Tuesday this week, but was cagey about his status for the Ohio State game.
Now we have the answer.
With Brown sidelined, his likely replacement is Villanova transfer Anthony Hawkins, who started Iowa's first two games of the season. Hawkins won the job after Brown missed all of fall camp with a soft-tissue injury, believed to be a hamstring. Also available to fill in at safety would be Purdue transfer Ty Hudkins and Robert Morris transfer Xavier Styles. The Hawkeyes' starting free safety is Zach Lutmer, who led Iowa with a career-high 12 tackles vs. Michigan.
