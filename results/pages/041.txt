Blake Baker Injury: Questionable for Oct. 3 vs. Army
Written by
RotoWire Staff
Published on September 19, 2026
Baker (hand) is questionable for Louisiana Tech's clash with Army in Week 5 after their bye, per Matt Belinson of the Ruston Daily Leader.
Baker missed the Bulldogs' Week 3 loss to Baylor, and head coach Sonny Crumbie said that the starting gunslinger is "questionable at this point." If he can't go against the Black Knights, Louisiana Tech figures to go with Trey Kukuk once again, who tossed for 233 yards one touchdown and one interceptions with 75 yards on the ground to boot in the loss to Baylor.
